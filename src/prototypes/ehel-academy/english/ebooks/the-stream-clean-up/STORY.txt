THE STREAM CLEAN-UP
Grade 2 - Level 2
Zuri series, book 7b (Term 3, Unit 7: The World Around Us)

1. The Stream Clean-Up. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. On Saturday the whole class walked down to the stream. Miss Twiga brought the bags.
3. The water was not clean. There was litter on the bank and litter in the stream.
4. Zuri picked up a bottle. Somebody had left it there. Nobody knew who.
5. Kiki picked up the paper. Paper goes soft in water, so she had to be quick.
6. The little elephant carried the big bag. She was the only one who could.
7. Then they sorted it. Paper in one bin. Tins in the next. Glass in the last one.
8. By the afternoon the water ran clear. Zuri could see the stones on the bottom.
9. And a bird came back to drink. It had not been there in the morning.
10. "Who put it here?" asked Miss Twiga. "Somebody." "And who takes it away?" "We do."
11. So the class painted a sign for the bank. A green heart, and four words under it.
12. "Next Saturday," said Zuri, "we will come again." And they did.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: litter, picking up, recycling, paper, tins, glass, clean, water, bank, thankful, important.
Themes: somebody made the mess and we can still be the ones who clear it
Cameos: Miss Twiga asks the two questions; the little elephant carries what nobody else can.
