THE CALM PLACE
Grade 3 - Level 3
Amal is worried about speaking out loud, so Nora takes her to the shore where feelings go out like waves.

1. The Calm Place. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. All week I worried about reading my idea out loud.
3. Nora saw my face at the gate. "Come with me after school," she said.
4. We walked down to the shore, where the waves come and go.
5. "This is my calm place," said Nora. "I come here when my feelings feel too big."
6. We sat on the warm sand and watched the boats.
7. "Breathe in slowly," she said, "and out again, like a wave."
8. In and out. In and out. The knot got looser.
9. "Feelings come and go like waves," said Nora. "Even the heavy ones go out again."
10. I told her what I was scared of, and saying it out loud made it smaller.
11. We found one smooth shell for my pocket, to hold whenever I worry.
12. On the day I spoke, the shell was in my pocket, and my voice was calm.
