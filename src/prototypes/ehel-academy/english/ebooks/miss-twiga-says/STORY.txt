MISS TWIGA SAYS
Grade 2 - Level 2
Zuri series, book 3c (Term 1, Unit 3: Ready, Steady, Go!)

1. Miss Twiga Says. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. "Stand up, everybody!" said Miss Twiga. And everybody stood up.
3. "Touch your head." Zuri touched her head. It is a small head, so it was easy.
4. "Clap your hands." Clap, clap, clap. Kiki clapped the loudest.
5. "Wiggle your fingers." Ten fingers, all wiggling at once.
6. "Nod your head." Zuri nodded. Kiki nodded. Miss Twiga nodded a very long way down.
7. "Turn around!" Everybody turned. Now the whole class was facing the wrong way.
8. "Reach for the sky!" Zuri reached. She did not reach the sky, but she reached.
9. "Sit down." Everybody sat down. Except Kiki. Kiki was still reaching.
10. "Your turn, Zuri," said Miss Twiga. So Zuri went to the front. "Stand on one leg!"
11. Nobody could do it. Everybody laughed, and Miss Twiga laughed the loudest of all.
12. "An instruction is easy," said Zuri. "Somebody says it, and then you do it."

Unit vocabulary reinforced: stand, sit, touch, clap, wiggle, nod, turn, reach, head, hands, fingers.
Themes: an instruction is heard, then done; giving one is harder than following one
Cameos: Miss Twiga leads and then hands the front of the class to Zuri; Kiki is the one still reaching.
