2. "May I interview you for my school report?" Amal asked.
3. "Of course. That sounds like a good project. Come and sit by the counter while I finish weighing this parcel."
4. "What do you do in your daily work?" "I sort the mail, help people send letters, and serve customers."
5. "I also read letters aloud for neighbours who find long words difficult," Omar said.
6. "Why is mail still important?" "Because not everyone owns a phone. A letter can make someone feel remembered."
7. "Do you always work here alone?" "Mostly. But a good citizen from the market often stops to help me carry the heavier parcels."
8. "How do you keep going when the shop is busy?" "I work at a steady speed, and I never leave a job half done."
9. "Is it hard to serve people who speak a different language?" "Not for me. I learned two, so I can help almost anyone."
10. "What do you enjoy most?" "Watching a worried customer smile again, once their letter is safely on its way."
11. Amal read every answer back to him before she left, so that nothing in her report would be unfair.
12. At home she copied it out neatly. She had come to the counter for answers, and she had learned how to ask.
