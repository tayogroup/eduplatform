THIS IS MY PARTNER
Grade 2 - Level 2
Zuri series, book 1c (Term 1, Unit 1: Welcome and Calendar)

1. This Is My Partner. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. "Today," said Miss Twiga, "you will not talk about yourself. You will talk about your partner."
3. So Zuri asked first. "What is your name? How do you spell it?" "K-I-K-I. Kiki."
4. "And what do you like?" "I like mangoes," said Kiki. "And I like Fridays."
5. Then Kiki asked. "What do you like, Zuri?" "I like words," said Zuri. "All of them."
6. Zuri wrote every answer down. A name, a spelling, and two things her partner likes.
7. Then the whole class sang the days. Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday.
8. And the months, from January all the way to December. Kiki's birthday is in May.
9. Zuri stood up first. "This is Kiki. K-I-K-I. She likes mangoes and she likes Fridays."
10. Then Kiki stood up. "This is Zuri. Z-U-R-I. She likes words. All of them."
11. The whole class clapped. Nobody had forgotten a single name.
12. First Zuri, second Kiki, third the little elephant. Everybody had a partner now.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: name, spell, partner, friend, like, likes, hello, goodbye, the days of the week, the months of the year, first, second, third.
Themes: listening well enough to speak for somebody else
Cameos: Miss Twiga sets the task; the little elephant, the ostrich, a goat and a hen make up the class.
