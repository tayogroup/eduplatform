Ehel Academy · Intensive English

Level 1 — Everyday English

By the end: Can read and understand short familiar texts, ask and answer simple questions about people, places, routines and events, follow and give simple instructions, and write short notes and messages joined with and, but and because.

A112 lessonsweeks 7–18 of the programme4 days per lessonabout 108 hours

Part 1 · Me and my world

1Who I amYou will be able to give personal details, spell names, and fill in a simple form. 2People in my lifeYou will be able to describe your family and the people you know. 3My homeYou will be able to describe your home, say where things are, and ask about a flat. 4Learning EnglishYou will be able to say what you can do, ask for help in class and write a short note.
Then: Part 1 review & check (2 days)

Part 2 · Everyday life

5Money and shoppingYou will be able to understand prices and amounts, and buy things in a shop or market. 6My day, my weekYou will be able to talk about your routine, times and how often you do things. 7Food and cookingYou will be able to buy food, and follow and give a simple recipe. 8Health and the doctorYou will be able to say what is wrong, make an appointment and read a medicine label.
Then: Part 2 review & check (2 days)

Part 3 · Out and about

9Clothes and buying thingsYou will be able to describe and compare things, ask for another size and return something. 10Around townYou will be able to ask for and follow directions, and understand signs in town. 11Now and last weekendYou will be able to say what is happening now, and tell someone what you did. 12Getting around, making plansYou will be able to buy a ticket, make a plan with someone and give reasons.
Then: A1 review & check (2 days)
