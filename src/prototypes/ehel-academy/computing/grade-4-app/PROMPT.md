# The prompt this build was made from (improved)

The request arrived as five words, after Grades 1, 2 and 3 had been built,
verified, deployed and routed:

> Grade 4 on the same kit

Below is the version that states what those words assume, what they leave
open, and the checks that decide whether it was done. It is the Grade 3
prompt (`../grade-3-app/PROMPT.md`) with the Stage 4 parts stated. Stage 4
is the last stage the deck design serves (`BOTH_DESIGNS_MAX_STAGE` is 4 in
`shell/subjects/computing.js`), so it is also where this kit stops.

---

## Build a Grade 4 Computing standalone lesson course for Ehel Academy, on the Grade 1–3 kit

### What to build

A **standalone lesson build** at `src/prototypes/ehel-academy/computing/grade-4-app/`,
generated by the kit the earlier grades were generated by (`../lesson-kit/`),
wired by the shared pipeline in `mathematics/lesson-app-tools/` and gated by
its `check-lessons.py` and the kit's `check-coverage.py`. One self-contained
HTML page per lesson plus a hub, the design and voice of the earlier builds
unchanged.

**"On the same kit" means the kit is extended, not forked, and the earlier
grades are the proof.** Stage 4 asks for things Stage 3 has no renderer for —
algorithms with repeat and forever loops to follow and fix, algorithms to
compare for a purpose, sub-routines to follow, a branch that one input takes
and another does not, a loop to build from a pool, comments on code, a script
per input, objects to plan, a program tested part by part, Bitsy's sensors
and loops, a table to sort and to point at, the Caesar and Pigpen ciphers —
so `lib/computing.js`, `_rules.py`, `build-lessons.py`, `build-hub.py` and
`check-coverage.py` all grow. Every growth must leave Grades 1, 2 and 3
exactly as they were: rebuild all three on the extended kit and require their
`LESSON` data blocks to be identical, page for page, before building anything
at Stage 4, and again after the last fix.

**Design, style and UI: unchanged.** No new hue; Stage 4 styles are added to
`computing.css` under a marked heading.

### The content: Cambridge Primary Computing 0059, Stage 4, all of it

- The framework file exists (`src/curriculum/cambridge-computing-0059.json`);
  Stage 4 has **39** objectives across the same five strands. Nothing under
  `computing/grade-4/` (the Word-pack course) is read or written.
- Organise by **strand**, fourteen lessons: loops in algorithms; predict and
  compare; sub-routines; inputs decide outputs; programs with loops; inputs,
  outputs and parts; Bitsy loops; data and information; databases; clients,
  servers and the web; when networks fail; Caesar and Pigpen; software,
  sensors and files; computer scientists and service robots.
- **Every step names the objective codes it exercises**; the builder refuses
  a code Stage 4 does not publish; the gate fails if any of the 39 is reached
  by no step. Behaviours, not labels: a repeat loop is followed with a counter
  and a forever loop until Stop (4CT.01, 4CT.02); a wrong step inside a loop
  is fixed and the fixed loop watched (4CT.01); a looped Robo program is
  predicted before Go (4CT.05); algorithms are compared on facts for a stated
  purpose (4CT.04); a sub-routine is entered at its call and left at its end
  (4CT.08); one input takes one branch and the other input the other
  (4CT.09); a loop is built from a pool and unrolled to check it (4CT.10);
  comments are matched to blocks (4P.01); a repeat block builds and folds a
  program (4P.02–04); each input gets its own script (4P.05); an object's
  input and output are planned (4P.06); a program is tested part by part
  (4P.07); Bitsy answers its sensors and runs counted and forever loops
  (4P.08, 4P.09); records, fields and data are tapped (4MD.07); a table is
  sorted ascending, descending and alphabetically (4MD.04); a Caesar shift
  and a Pigpen grid are written and read (4DC.06).
- **Computed beats claimed.** Every key the data can decide is computed by
  `_rules.py` and its JS mirror: the unrolled loop, the walked loop
  algorithm, the sub-routine walk, the branch, the best algorithm for a
  purpose, the sort order, the Caesar shift, the Pigpen index, plus every
  Stage 1–3 rule the reused kinds carry — refused by the builder and
  re-checked by the gate on the shipped pages.
- Language: British English, eight-to-nine-year-old reading level, the
  learner addressed as "you". Home projects unplugged.

### Wiring and progress

- `app.config.json`: grade 4, stage 4, `courseKey` `ehel-comp-g04`,
  `progressUnitPrefix` `l`, `fromParam` `comp4`, hub `g4-index.html`, remote
  `Ehel Primary/app/computing/grade-4-v2`; `objectiveFloors` recorded at the
  measured value after the first green build.
- The same pipeline in the same order as Grades 1 to 3.
- Do NOT deploy Grade 4, do NOT redeploy Grades 1 to 3, and do NOT route:
  all owner decisions to be asked for.

### Definition of done

1. Grades 1, 2 and 3 rebuilt on the extended kit: pipeline and gates green,
   `LESSON` blocks identical to HEAD, 8 of 8, 10 of 10 and 14 of 14.
2. `build-lessons.py` builds 14 pages with 0 refusals and reports 39/39;
   `check-lessons.py` and `check-coverage.py` exit 0.
3. Every inline classic script of all four grades parses under `node --check`.
4. The gate is mutation-tested at Stage 4, pages restored byte-identical: a
   lesson losing an objective, an unpublished code, a lesson under its floor,
   a looped Robo level keyed to the wrong square, a compare purpose keyed
   against its facts, a loop-algorithm fix with no key and a round too short
   when followed, a sub-routine that expands too short, a branch whose arms
   agree, a loop built from a step the pool lacks, comments that miss a
   block, two inputs with one script, a plan with no key, a part whose fix
   does not make its expect, a device forever block last, a sort keyed wrong,
   a table part the table lacks, a filter count keyed wrong, and Caesar and
   Pigpen answers that disagree with their codes.
5. Every step of every lesson of ALL FOUR grades is driven to completion in a
   browser; dot rail, header and shelf agree; only the five platform-module
   404s in the console; no step overflows at 375px.
6. A README beside the build; the kit README's step-kind table updated.

### What is deliberately out of scope

Deploying or routing; recorded narration; a Scratch embed; anything at
Stage 5, which the deck design does not serve; a human reading of the
content.

### Why five words needed this

- "the same kit" is a constraint on the KIT: Stage 4 cannot be authored in
  Stage 3's step kinds, so the kit has to grow, and the only thing that makes
  growing it safe is a proof that the three live grades did not move. The
  prompt states the proof.
- "Grade 4" is Stage 4 of 0059 — 39 objectives with a different emphasis
  (loops indefinite and count-controlled, comparing algorithms, sub-routines,
  branches, comments, scripts per input, systematic testing, sensors,
  databases, clients and servers, the web against the internet, wi-fi against
  ethernet, network failures, encryption and two ciphers, systems software,
  data loggers, file sizes, computer scientists and service robots) — so the
  lesson plan is stated rather than inherited.
- What was NOT said (deploy, route, redeploy the earlier grades) is stated as
  not done, because each of the earlier flows did those only on an explicit
  instruction.
