A TRIP TO THE CAPITAL
Grade 4 - Level 4
Amal (Grade 4) series, book 2 (Term 3, Unit 9: Places, People, and Plans)

1. A Trip to the Capital. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Last month my family took a trip to the capital city, and I could not wait to see the tall buildings I had only seen in photos.
3. First we went to the big station and waited for the train under a large clock.
4. The platform was noisy with travellers carrying suitcases and baskets, and a vendor sold warm bread near the entrance.
5. When the train arrived, we found seats near the window.
6. We watched the horizon rush past as green fields slowly turned into rooftops.
7. In the city there was so much to see. We visited a museum full of old maps and tools.
8. A kind guide showed us a stone that was thousands of years old.
9. Then we walked past shops, offices, and a tall building with a glass lift that seemed to touch the sky.
10. Tourism brings many people to the capital, so the streets were full, and we held hands so we would not get separated.
11. At the end of the day we ate at a small restaurant near the entrance of the market: rice, grilled fish and cold juice.
12. My little brother fell asleep before we even reached the station. I was tired, but I would like to go again next year.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: station, railway, capital, museum, tourism, entrance, lift, horizon, restaurant, arrive.
Themes: a day that is too big to hold, and a little brother asleep before the end of it
