2. "Long, long ago," said Teacher Yasmin, "there were no rulers and no metre sticks."
3. "So how did people measure anything?" The class had no idea.
4. "In Egypt they used the width of a finger for very small things."
5. "The length of a hand for medium things. The length of an arm for a piece of cloth."
6. "But wouldn't that give different answers for different people?" asked Amal.
7. "Exactly right," said Teacher Yasmin. "A big person's arm is longer than a small person's."
8. "In Rome they counted footsteps. A market might be one thousand footsteps away."
9. "But a tall soldier's footstep is longer than a short one's. So they argued too."
10. "Then somebody invented a ruler," said Amal, "and a metre stick."
11. "One hundred centimetres in one metre. The same for everybody, everywhere."
12. "And that," said Teacher Yasmin, "is why nobody has to argue about it any more."
