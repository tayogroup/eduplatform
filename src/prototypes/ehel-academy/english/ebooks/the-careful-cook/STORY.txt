5. "Never use metal, not even a spoon or a piece of foil, because metal can cause sparks."

10. "Use a cloth to take it out — never your bare fingers."
11. "Stir the food with a clean utensil before you eat, because the middle can be much hotter than the edges."
12. "Let it cool for a moment if it is steaming. Now you know the safe steps. Enjoy your warm meal!"
