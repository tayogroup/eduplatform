THE BITTER LUNCH
Grade 4 - Level 4
Amal (Grade 4) series, book 2 (Term 1, Unit 3: Food and Health)

1. The Bitter Lunch. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Amal lived beside the river, where the fields were hot and green. She helped Grandma Hana grow rice and care for the cattle.
3. Every morning before school she fed the animals and carried water from the well. It was hard labour.
4. "We work hard now to eat well later," Grandma Hana always said.
5. At school Teacher Yasmin set a project. "Write a story about food, and give it a health message."
6. That evening Amal walked to the bakery. It smelled of warm bread, and she was hungry, so she bought a sandwich.
7. "I should tell you," said Omar, "this meat is not fresh." Amal was too hungry to care.
8. At home her cousin Noah frowned at it. "There is a strange smell." "You worry too much," said Amal, and she ate the rest.
9. Late that night her stomach hurt badly. Noah heard her and woke the family.
10. At the clinic Doctor Sarah checked her carefully. "This may be food poisoning. You must always check your food first."
11. The next day Amal stayed at home with warm soup, and she wrote: "I once ate a bad sandwich. It looked fine, but it was not fresh."
12. At the community lunch she read it aloud, and the room went quiet. Then she ate lamb and rice that were clean and fresh.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: sandwich, lamb, rice, spice, bakery, fresh, chewy, cattle, labour, stomach, chemicals, gather.
Themes: the warning you were given and ignored; writing the mistake down afterwards
Cameos: Omar warns her, Noah smells it first and Doctor Sarah names it — all from the Unit 3 story.
