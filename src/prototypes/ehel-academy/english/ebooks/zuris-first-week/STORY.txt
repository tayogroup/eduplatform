5. Kiki wrote the first day, Monday. Zuri wrote the second day, Tuesday.
6. On Wednesday they made a colour chart. Red, blue, green, yellow and pink.
7. On Thursday they counted the books on the shelf. One, two, three - twelve books!
8. On Friday Miss Twiga asked, "What do you like, Zuri?" "I like words," said Zuri.
9. Zuri found English words everywhere. On the chart. In her book. On the tablet.
10. Then Kiki pointed at the calendar. "Look! The twelfth of the month is my birthday."
11. The whole class made Kiki a card. Zuri wrote every letter by herself.
12. "Goodbye, Zuri! See you next week," said Kiki. Zuri smiled. She had a friend now.
