AMAL THE AUTHOR
Grade 4 - Level 4
A character, a setting, an event and a solution — Amal writes her own book and reads it to the little ones.

1. Amal the Author. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Amal decided to write a book of her own — a real one, with pages.
3. "Every author needs a character," said Maya. "Someone we truly care about."
4. Amal chose a small grey cat with an enormous curiosity.
5. The setting came next: the market, on the windiest day of the year.
6. Then the event: one big gust took every shopping list into the air!
7. Amal read the problem to the little ones and asked them to predict the solution.
8. "The cat!" they shouted. "The cat will chase the paper!"
9. So the cat chased every list and carried them back, one by one.
10. On the last page, the whole market whispered thank you to one small cat.
11. Amal read her book outside the library, and the children retold it all the way home.
12. Inside the cover she wrote: by Amal — author, Year 4.
