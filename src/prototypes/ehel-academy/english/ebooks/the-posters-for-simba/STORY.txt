THE POSTERS FOR SIMBA
Grade 4 - Level 4
Amal (Grade 4) series, book 5 (Term 2, Unit 5: Action and Movement)

1. The Posters for Simba. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Simba was safe at Talia's, with clean water and food, and he had already stopped shivering.
3. "Someone might be looking for him," said Nora. "Let us make posters and describe him."
4. So they described him carefully: thin, brown, with one ear that never quite stands up.
5. Amal wrote the words. She used every detail, and she checked her spelling twice.
6. Leo drew the picture. It took him three attempts before the ear looked right.
7. Nora read the whole thing back to them before they made a single copy.
8. They put one up at the market, one at the school gate, and one in the bakery window.
9. Three days went by. Nobody came.
10. "Then he has a home already," said Talia. "He has had one since the day you carried him out."
11. Simba was not thin any more. He could run the length of the yard without stopping once.
12. They kept one poster — the picture — and pinned it up in the classroom, where everybody could see him.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: describe, rescue, defend, check, admit, suffer, signal.
Themes: doing the honest thing even when you hope it fails; a home that was already given
Cameos: Talia, Simba and the poster plan all come from the Unit 5 story "The Spiral Cave".
