“Hello!” said a kind lady at the door. “I am your teacher. What is your name?”

“My name is Amal,” she said softly. “I am six years old.”

“Welcome, Amal!” said the teacher. “Come and see our classroom.”

“Look!” said Amal. “A red book! And a blue pencil!”

A boy smiled at her. “Hello, my name is Leo,” he said. “Do you want to be my friend?”

“Yes!” said Amal. She was happy now.

The teacher said, “Let us sing!” All the children sang the alphabet song: “A, B, C, D…” Amal sang too.

“I like my school,” said Amal.

Amal smiled. “I learned new words. I made a friend. And I can sing the alphabet song!”

What happened to the ball after Leo threw it?

answer: It went up into a big tree

Leo threw the ball, it went up, up, up, and landed on a branch.

the text it is asked about
It was a sunny day in the town of Hargeisa. Amal ran outside to play. She had a big red ball.

“Look at my ball!” said Amal. She could bounce it. Bounce, bounce, bounce!

Her friend Samira came to play. “Can you catch the ball?” asked Amal.

“Yes, I can!” said Samira. Amal threw the ball. Samira caught it. “Well done!” said Amal.

Then little Leo came. He was only four. “Can I play too?” asked Leo.

Amal looked at the ball. She wanted to play with Samira. But Leo looked sad.

“Yes!” said Amal. “Let us all play. We can take turns.”

Amal rolled the ball to Leo. Roll, roll, roll! Leo put his two hands on the ball. He was happy.

“Now throw it to me!” said Samira. Leo threw the ball. But — oh no! The ball went up, up, up!

The ball went into a big tree. It was on a branch. “My ball is stuck!” said Amal.

The three friends looked up. How could they get the ball?

“I know!” said Samira. “Let us jump!” They all jumped. Jump, jump, jump! But the ball was too high.

Just then, big brother Adam came. He was tall. “I can help,” said Adam.

Adam shook the branch. Shake, shake, shake! The ball came down. Amal caught it. “Thank you, Adam!” said all the children.

“You shared the ball,” said Adam. “You are good friends.”

Amal, Samira and Leo played all day. They bounced and rolled and caught the ball. It was so much fun!

When the sun went down, Amal said, “Sharing is the best game of all.”
