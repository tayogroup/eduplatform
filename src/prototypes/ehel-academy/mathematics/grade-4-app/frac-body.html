Parts of a Whole

1

More parts, smaller parts

The whole never changes. Cut it into more pieces and each piece must get smaller.

Every size you have tried, one part of each

2

A fraction is a division

Share the cakes fairly. What each child gets is the cakes divided by the children — and that is exactly what the fraction says.

3

A fraction of an amount

A unit fraction is an instruction: make that many equal groups, then take one of them.

4

Same value, different name

Two fractions can look different and be worth exactly the same. Line them up and see.

5

Out of a hundred

A percentage is how many parts in every hundred. The sign % is just shorthand for “out of a hundred”.

6

Which is bigger?

Give them the same bottom number first. Then you are only comparing the top ones. Choose >, < or =.

7

Adding and taking away

Same bottom number means the pieces are the same size, so you just count them. Estimate first, then work it out.
