Reviewed 2026-09-11. Reviewer: Claude (automated measurement, an independent line-by-line content read, and judgement); no human teacher review yet. Scale: 1 Unsatisfactory; 2 Needs major improvement; 3 Acceptable with revisions; 4 Strong; 5 Excellent.

## Summary

The build reaches all 18 Cambridge Primary Global Perspectives 0838 Stage 2 objectives, every quiz key is single and explained, and the progress fix from the Grade 1 review is already live on these pages. The review found two things a learner or a school would feel. First, the opinion step praised any reason that was about the topic, whichever way it argued: 'I think we should recycle more, because it takes time at the end of the day' was accepted as an opinion with reasons, in every grade. Second, a line-by-line read found about 40 content problems, among them a lesson that answered 'ask the teacher' with 'that is somebody else's action' and no reassurance, options to 'cry until somebody gives me one' and 'ask my mum to come', a home activity that had children look inside the recycling bin, a bus driver drawn as an aeroplane pilot seven times, the learner's own scarecrow idea credited to a classmate, and question builders that refused real questions or offered one that is not a question ('Where was the beach from your house?').

All of it is fixed in the build: 71 content edits across the eight lessons and six changes to the shared Global Perspectives kit, each gated and mutation-tested, verified on screen and in the browser with the real platform modules, both gates green on all four grades. It was committed as commit 9fec6d681 and Grades 1 to 4 were redeployed and pushed the same day, so the live pages carry every fix in this report.

Average score **4.2 / 5** across 27 areas; lowest 4 (21 of the 27 areas; none below 4). Two High-priority findings, both fixed and live since commit 9fec6d681.

Change status at 2026-09-11: 16 items need no change or are done in the build, 7 have fixes built with a human or device check still open, 4 are open and not started. The validation fixes are live since commit 9fec6d681; the final pre-pilot review's since 2f1c97bf0; the open-items pass recorded below is in the working tree, not yet committed or deployed.

## What was fixed during this review

Every change below is verified: both gates green on Grades 1 to 4, the mutation harness passing on all four stages, and every lesson of all four grades driven to 100%. The validation fixes are live since commit 9fec6d681 and the final pre-pilot review's since 2f1c97bf0; the last item, the open-items pass, is in the working tree.

- **Opinions (kit, all grades).** Each on-topic reason names the opinions it supports; the page refuses a reason that argues the other way and says why; the builder and the coverage gate refuse an opinion with too few supporting reasons.
- **Safeguarding.** Asking a grown-up is always all right (Lesson 4, five places); no crying or 'ask my mum' options; going to bed on time rather than choosing a bedtime; hands out of the bin; websites with a grown-up beside you.
- **Question builders.** Lesson 1 and 7 endings accept every real question; the crowd is at the slide, as the picture shows; 'Where was the beach from your house?' replaced by 'in your old town' and 'How far'.
- **Facts and credit.** 'The most children' for three of eight; 'knows most about'; the rules sentence the Explain points at; the moon, the map, the running-out paint; the learner's own ideas and job; acacia; Leo's beach.
- **Pictures and words.** A bus for the bus driver (seven places), a pencil for 'sharper', 'queue', 'Grade 1 and Grade 2', 'this year' for 'Stage 2'; the Lesson 8 review's repeated stems reworded.
- **Kit casing.** 'I', titles, acronyms, weekdays and the course's names keep their capital after 'I know that' and 'Now I think'.
- **Decisions on the owner's instruction.** Every 2020-era emoji replaced (look-back rewind, a chair for the step stool); the four grades stay the same length.
- **Final pre-pilot review (2026-09-11, after commit 9fec6d681).** A fresh read found 0 High, 7 Medium, 18 Low; all Medium fixed (mixed opinions need a reason on each side; 'Who would know...?' questions; 'Leo'; the fewest-children item; 'pictures and bars'), with the kit's spoken-template fixes. Details: 'global-perspectives-grades-1-4 pilot readiness review.docx'.
- **Open items after the pilot review (2026-09-11, working tree).** Every lesson opens with 'Before we start' (a recap of the previous lesson and two starting questions, not marked); 2Ea.01 now 16 steps (was 8): Lesson 7 adds an opinion step with two reasons on its own topic (a buddy for a new pupil, moving to a new town, giving a talk), with 'mixed' views that need a reason on each side. Local context: a fisherman who works at sea replaces the aquarium as a source about the sea. The remaining Low wording findings from the final reads are fixed, and the hub's minutes come from a play-through. Verified: both gates green (the gate now also checks the recap against the previous page, refuses a starting question with two keys or one copied from any quiz in the grade), the mutation harness catching every break, all 32 lessons driven to 100% with every step recorded, and the accessibility pass clean on all 566 slides.

## Verdict
