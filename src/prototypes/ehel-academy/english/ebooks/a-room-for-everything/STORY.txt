Grade 2 - Level 2
Zuri series, book 8b (Term 3, Unit 8: Home, Sweet Home)

1. A Room for Everything. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. "Come and see my home," said Kiki. Her home is a tree house, up in the big baobab.
3. The kitchen has a sink, a cooker and a cupboard. The bananas live in the cupboard.
4. The dining room has a table and four chairs. Four, because visitors come.
5. The living room has a soft sofa and a warm rug. This is the best room.
6. The bedroom has a bed, a pillow and a window. The window is for looking at the stars.
7. And the bathroom has a bath and a tap. The tap drips. Kiki says she likes it.
8. "And where do you keep your books?" asked Zuri. Kiki showed her the shelf. Twelve.
9. Then they went to Zuri's home. A burrow has one room, and it is for everything.
10. "Is one room enough?" asked Kiki. "It is enough for me," said Zuri. "It is warm and it is safe."
11. So they drew both homes, side by side, and put them on the wall together.
12. Five rooms or one room. Every home has a place for everything it needs.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: kitchen, dining room, living room, bedroom, bathroom, bed, table, chair, sofa, sink, rug, window, shelf.
Themes: five rooms and one room are both enough; comparing without ranking
Cameos: Kiki's tree house in the baobab and Zuri's burrow, both from Every Home Is Different.
