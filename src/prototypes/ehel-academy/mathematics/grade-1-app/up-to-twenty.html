Numbers to 20

Two numbers. Which one is bigger? Tap it. The number line will help you.

10

Guess, then count

Don't count yet! How many stars do you think there are? Tap your guess.

11

Estimate to 20

How many apples? Slide your guess on the number line and lock it. Then tap each apple to count it into the frames.

12

Adding

Put the two groups together. How many altogether? Tap the answer.

13

Taking away

Tap the counters to cross them out.

14

How many more?

Press Match them up. The ones with no partner are the difference.

15

Through 10

Bigger sums. First tap the answer. Then we go through 10 together, one counter at a time.

16

Making 10

Ten in a row. Tap where to split them. Find 5 different ways to make 10.

17

Doubles

A ladybird has the same spots on both sides. Double means the same again.

18

Doubles to 20

Tap a double to see it in the frames. Find 5 doubles, then use them to add.

19

Halves

Share the counters between two plates, one each. Half means two equal groups.

Tap the pizza to cut it in half.

20

Halves to 20

Pick a number, then press Halve it. Half is the opposite of double. Find 5 halves.

21

Two halves make one whole

Press Put them together and watch.

24

Compare to 20

Two pictures. Does the first one have more, fewer, or the same? Look at the tens first, then the ones.

25

Odd and even

Tap a number. If every counter has a partner, the number is even. If one is left alone, it is odd. Sort 8 numbers.

26

First, second, third

Ten runners in a race. The one nearest the flag is first. Press Next runner and say the places with me.

27

Number patterns

A number pattern follows a rule. Look at the jumps. What is the rule? Tap it, then tap the number that comes next.
