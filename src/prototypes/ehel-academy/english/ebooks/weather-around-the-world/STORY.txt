1. Weather Around the World. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Weather is not the same everywhere. Amal and Nora set out to draw every kind of weather they could find.
3. In some places the morning is foggy and grey, and people walk slowly along the path until the mist begins to lift.
4. In others a hot sun makes people sweat, especially where dry canyons stretch for miles under a clear sky.
5. Near a quiet bay or a green meadow the weather feels gentler — but even a calm place changes quickly once the wind turns.
6. High in the mountains snow falls softly and covers the ground in white.
7. Down by the coast, moisture from the sea keeps the air feeling damp all day.
8. Rain helps plants grow, because plants need moisture. Farmers watch the clouds and hope for a steady shower rather than a flood.
9. Not all weather is gentle. A storm brings rain, but a hurricane brings wind strong enough to bend trees and tear roofs from houses.
10. Sometimes hail rattles down from dark clouds like handfuls of small stones.
11. In open country a tornado can spin across the land faster than almost anything a person has ever seen.
12. Scientists study the wind, the clouds and the temperature every day. Wherever we live, we watch the sky and learn.

Themes: the same sky doing eight different things; watching it and learning
