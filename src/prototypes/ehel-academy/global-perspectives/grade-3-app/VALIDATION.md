Reviewed 2026-09-11. Reviewer: Claude (automated measurement, an independent line-by-line content read, and judgement); no human teacher review yet. Scale: 1 Unsatisfactory; 2 Needs major improvement; 3 Acceptable with revisions; 4 Strong; 5 Excellent.

## Summary

The build reaches all 18 Cambridge Primary Global Perspectives 0838 Stage 3 objectives, every quiz key is single and explained, and the progress fix from the Grade 1 review is already live on these pages. The review found three things a learner or a school would feel. First, safeguarding: the lesson on acting for other people marked 'tell the teacher' as the wrong answer when a new girl eats alone every day, and taught 'other people's problems are for grown-ups to fix' as a mistaken belief. Second, the kit marked right answers wrong: a talk's second middle fact was refused because the talk had one middle slot, and an opinion reason was accepted whichever way it argued. Third, about 30 content problems, among them 'tap water is safe to drink', a river 'higher in winter', a Venn diagram that contradicted its own survey, a school using 'four hundred litres' of water a day, and a board drawn as a tyre.

All of it is fixed in the build: 82 content edits across the eight lessons and six changes to the shared Global Perspectives kit, each gated and mutation-tested, verified on screen and in the browser with the real platform modules, both gates green on all four grades. It was committed as commit 9fec6d681 and Grades 1 to 4 were redeployed and pushed the same day, so the live pages carry every fix in this report.

Average score **4.2 / 5** across 27 areas; lowest 4 (22 of the 27 areas; none below 4). Two High-priority findings, both fixed and live since commit 9fec6d681.

Change status at 2026-09-11: 16 items need no change or are done in the build, 7 have fixes built with a human or device check still open, 4 are open and not started. The validation fixes are live since commit 9fec6d681; the final pre-pilot review's since 2f1c97bf0; the open-items pass recorded below is in the working tree, not yet committed or deployed.

- **Medium.** A teacher's read of the content and keys (areas 1, 4, 10, 19); a screen-reader walk-through (15); two lessons on the school's own devices (21); watching children use it (13, 27); a cross-subject decision on self-hosting the fonts (20).

## What was fixed during this review

Every change below is verified: both gates green on Grades 1 to 4, the mutation harness passing on all four stages, and every lesson of all four grades driven to 100%. The validation fixes are live since commit 9fec6d681 and the final pre-pilot review's since 2f1c97bf0; the last item, the open-items pass, is in the working tree.

- **Talks (kit).** Each talk has two middle slots for its two middle facts; the builder and gate refuse a talk whose cards per part do not match its slots.
- **Opinions (kit).** Reasons name the views they support; a counter-reason is refused with the reason why; for Mr Bello, Mrs Adams and Mr Khan two reasons back 'agree', two back 'disagree', all four 'partly'.
- **Spoken lines (kit).** 'That is a bus, not a car'; 'You are good at'; 'Now I think I can'.
- **Safeguarding.** Telling a teacher praised in Lesson 5 (Explain, activity, quiz, look-back in Lessons 5 and 8); a report-it action for the fountain; tap-water safety; bedtime.
- **Facts.** Rainy season and 'this year'; the Venn banana; 'more than any other snack'; four thousand litres; measuring tools; the pencil case; Nora's two labels; the two look-back beliefs.
- **Pictures and words.** Mr Ali, the kettle, the cup, the board; rainwater tank; foil strips; 'first in the line'; grammar; Grade and 'this year'; nine repeated stems reworded; question builders widened.
- **Decisions on the owner's instruction.** Every 2020-era emoji replaced (look-back rewind, a money bag, a seedling); the park's dog debate kept; the four grades stay the same length.
- **Final pre-pilot review (2026-09-11, after commit 9fec6d681).** A fresh read found 1 High, 8 Medium, 15 Low; the High (a false option called 'true' in the course look-back) and every Medium fixed, with the kit's spoken-template fixes. Details: 'global-perspectives-grades-1-4 pilot readiness review.docx'.
- **Open items after the pilot review (2026-09-11, working tree).** Every lesson opens with 'Before we start' (a recap of the previous lesson and two starting questions, not marked); 3Ea.01 now 16 steps (was 8): Lesson 5 adds an opinion of three people's views on shared problems (Mr Ali on football in the street, Hana on tidying, Sami on litter), two reasons each. Local context: Lesson 1's bath and 'pull the plug' are now a family's water for a day (eighty litres, four jerrycans) and pouring used water away; flapjacks are samosas; the pear is a mango; 'the post' is the water tank. Dogs are on a leash, not a lead (a voice can say 'led'). The remaining Low wording findings from the final reads are fixed, and the hub's minutes come from a play-through. Verified: both gates green (the gate now also checks the recap against the previous page, refuses a starting question with two keys or one copied from any quiz in the grade), the mutation harness catching every break, all 32 lessons driven to 100% with every step recorded, and the accessibility pass clean on all 566 slides.

## Verdict

Grade 3 is sound and well aligned, strong in almost every area, and the final pre-pilot review's fixes are live. The open-items pass adds a recap and a starting check to every lesson, a second Evaluation activity, local details and voice-safe wording; once it is committed and redeployed, which is the owner's decision, Grade 3 is ready for a supervised pilot with a teacher in the room.
