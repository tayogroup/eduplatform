FAR AWAY HOMES
Grade 2 - Level 2
Zuri series, book 8c (Term 3, Unit 8: Home, Sweet Home)

1. Far Away Homes. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Miss Twiga brought a big book of homes. "None of these," she said, "is anywhere near here."
3. An adobe house, where it is hot and dry. Thick mud walls keep the day outside.
4. A stilt house, where the water rises. When the river comes up, the house stays dry.
5. A cave house, cut into the cool rock. Somebody made it, a long time ago.
6. A skyscraper, with more windows than Zuri could count. People live in every one.
7. A nest of twigs, high in the acacia. One bird built it in one week.
8. A hive of wax, humming all day. The bees made every wall of it themselves.
9. And a burrow, under the ground, where the sand stays cool. Zuri knows that one.
10. Different walls. Different roofs. Different doors.
11. But the same thing inside every one - a place to sit, and somebody glad you came.
12. Zuri drew them all into her book, and left a page for the ones she has not seen.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: adobe house, stilt house, cave house, skyscraper, nest, hive, burrow, hut, flat, walls, roof, door.
Themes: a home is shaped by the place it stands in; the same welcome inside every one
Cameos: Miss Twiga's big book of homes; the nest, the hive and the burrow return from Every Home Is Different.
