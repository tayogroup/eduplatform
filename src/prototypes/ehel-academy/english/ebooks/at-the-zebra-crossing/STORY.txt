AT THE ZEBRA CROSSING
Grade 2 - Level 2
Grade 2 shelf - Poem series, book 5 (Term 3, Unit 9: Let's Explore the City!)

1. At the Zebra Crossing. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. A zebra crossing is a safe place to cross the road.
3. At the zebra crossing,
4. look around at city places,
5. look around at city faces.
6. I bend down to tie my laces... Oops!
7. "WALK!" said Nora. "Hurry up! Let's cross!"
8. So they crossed together, quickly and carefully.
9. The cars waited. That is what a zebra crossing is for.
10. On the other side: the shops, and the library right beside them.
11. "Next time," said Nora, "tie your laces BEFORE we cross."
12. At the zebra crossing - look, and look again, and then walk.
