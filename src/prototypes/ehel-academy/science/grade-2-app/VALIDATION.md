# Grade 2 Science (science/grade-2-v2) validation, version 2

Reviewed 2026-09-11. Supersedes version 1, 2026-09-11 (in git history, and kept as its own Word file in OneDrive). Reviewer: Claude (automated measurement, browser driving, an independent content review by a second Claude agent, and judgement); no human teacher review yet. Scale: 1 Unsatisfactory; 2 Needs major improvement; 3 Acceptable with revisions; 4 Strong; 5 Excellent. Build reviewed: commit 0f8bed7f5.

Against the 27-area framework in *science-grade-1-v2 validation.docx*. The Word copy of this report is *science-grade-2-v2 validation report v2 2026-09-11.docx* in the same OneDrive folder; both are generated from the same rows.

## Summary

Second version, after the fix pass the first validation asked for. All 53 findings of the content review are fixed: 20 facts, 8 keys including 7 pictures, 15 wording points, 6 safety points and 4 inclusion points. So are the two kit scenes: sunrise and sunset now sit on opposite sides, and the Arctic has no penguin. Grade 2 now has what Grade 1 has: two sittings of about 20 minutes, a warm-up and a 'Last time' recap on every overview, a reasoning question in every quiz, and no objective with a single teaching step. The two quiz questions copied from Grade 1 are replaced, and the two mis-tagged steps retagged. No step was added, removed or reordered, so every saved place stays valid. All ten lessons were driven to 100% again. Grades 1 to 3 were redeployed as 0f8bed7f5. The average rises from 3.7 to 4.4, with no High finding. What remains needs people: a teacher's read, a screen-reader session, the school's devices, and children timed and watched.

Average score **4.4 / 5** across 27 areas (version 1: 3.7). Lowest score 4, in 17 areas. No High-priority finding.

Change status at 2026-09-11: **16** areas need no change or are done, **6** have their fix live with a person's check still open, **5** are open.

## What changed since version 1

- Sixteen scores rose and none fell. The largest moves: accuracy from 2 to 4, and safeguarding from 3 to 5.
- Fixing a wrong tag exposed a thin objective: once the shadow clock was no longer called a model, 2TWSm.02 had one teaching step. It now has a real physical model, a torch and a pencil in play dough.
- The new sequence guard found emoji the first validation could not see: three black cats and two polar bears in Grade 2, and four polar bears in Grade 4. All are replaced.
