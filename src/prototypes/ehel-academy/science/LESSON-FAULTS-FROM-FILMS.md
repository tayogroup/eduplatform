# Faults the film work found in the LESSONS

Made while writing the unit lecture films for Science Grades 2, 3 and 4
(2026-09-23 and 24). Each film is written by one agent reading its lesson
closely, then reviewed by a second agent that changes nothing and must quote the
lesson or the code for every note. That reading found these, and **none of them
is a film fault** — they are in the lessons and the shared lesson kit, and the
films work around them.

**Nothing here has been fixed.** Four kinds are listed, and they are not equally
urgent:

| kind | what it costs a learner |
| --- | --- |
| a lesson that contradicts itself | the child is taught two opposite things, or marked wrong for the lesson's own rule |
| a picture that cannot show what its words claim | the step's own instruction ("count the legs", "spot what changes") cannot be followed |
| a control that does nothing | the child presses it, hears the sound, and sees no change |
| a Cambridge code claimed and not taught | a coverage figure that is not evidence of teaching |

Every line number below was re-checked by hand against the file, and every
"appears nowhere else" is a whole-file search with its complete output read —
not a filtered one. Where a claim rests on arithmetic (a transform, a viewBox,
a leg count) the arithmetic is shown.
