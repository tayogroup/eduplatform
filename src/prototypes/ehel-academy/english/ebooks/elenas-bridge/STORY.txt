2. "Good morning, Elena. Thank you for talking to our school newspaper today."
3. "What does an engineer do?" "An engineer designs and builds things like roads, bridges and machines."
4. "I am building a new bridge for our town."
5. "That sounds like a big job. How long will it take?" "About six more months, if the weather stays kind to us."
6. "Where do you work?" "Near the river, and sometimes in an office. Today I am standing by the water, checking the plans."
7. "What do you wear to stay safe on the site?" "A bright helmet and strong boots, every single day, without exception."
8. "Why is your work important?" "Because a strong bridge helps people cross the river safely."
9. "You mustn't build in a hurry," she said. "You should plan carefully, and check everything twice."
10. "Thank you, Elena. I can't wait to walk across your bridge when it opens."
11. "Neither can I, Nora. Save me a seat at the opening."
12. Six months later the cones came away, and the whole town walked across together. Nora had saved her a seat.
