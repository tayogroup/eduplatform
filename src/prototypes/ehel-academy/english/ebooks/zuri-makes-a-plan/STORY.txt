ZURI MAKES A PLAN
Grade 2 - Level 2
Zuri series, book 10b (Term 3, Unit 10: My English World (capstone))

1. Zuri Makes a Plan. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. "Make a book called My English World," read Miss Twiga. "You have three weeks."
3. Step one: choose. Zuri chose her year. All of it. She did not want to leave anything out.
4. Step two: plan. Nine pages, one for every unit. She wrote the list on the board.
5. Step three: collect. Zuri already had most of it, in the book she writes in every day.
6. Step four: draw. This was the long step. It was also the best one.
7. Step five: write. One sentence under every drawing. Only one. That is harder than it sounds.
8. Step six: check. Every word, twice, out loud.
9. Kiki checked Zuri's pages and Zuri checked Kiki's. That is faster than checking your own.
10. They found one mistake. Zuri had spelled Wednesday with no d in it. She mended it.
11. Then the plan went up on the wall with every step ticked off.
12. "Now," said Zuri, closing her book, "it is ready."

Unit vocabulary reinforced: plan, choose, collect, draw, write, check, page, step, first, next, last, mistake.
Themes: the plan is the work; checking somebody else's page is easier than checking your own
Cameos: the project brief is the Unit 10 reading's own; Kiki is the second pair of eyes.
