What comes next?

A pattern repeats. Say it out loud, then tap the bead that comes next.

2

The part that repeats

Every pattern has a part that repeats. How many beads are in that part? Tap the number.

3

Missing shapes

A shape has fallen out of the middle. Look at the bead before the hole and the bead after it. Which one belongs?

4

Numbers that repeat

Numbers can repeat too, just like the beads. Say the pattern out loud, then tap the number that belongs.

5

Missing numbers

Some numbers have fallen off the track. Tap a gap, then tap the number that belongs there.

Growing shapes

These patterns grow. Each one adds another row. Count the shapes, then work out how many are in the next one.

Jump patterns

11

3 + ? = 7

A number is missing. Press + to add counters until you reach the answer, and count how many you added.

12

9 − ? = 4

Cross out counters until the right number is left. The crossed-out ones are the missing number.

13

The same on both sides

The = sign means the same on both sides. Press + to add counters to the light side until the scale is level.

14

The number machine

A number machine does the same thing to every number. Look at what goes in and what comes out. What is it doing?
