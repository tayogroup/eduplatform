THE TOWN MEETING
Grade 4 - Level 4
Amal (Grade 4) series, book 3 (Term 2, Unit 4: Community and Communication)

1. The Town Meeting. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. "Good evening, everyone, and thank you for coming to the town hall tonight," said the mayor.
3. "I want to share some information about our new community centre, which opens next month near the market square."
4. "First, there is a reading room, and there are many books already stacked on the new shelves."
5. "Then, there is a small clinic, where a nurse will give health advice every morning."
6. "Finally, there is an open hall for meetings, lessons and community events. This whole centre is a service for all of us."
7. A woman near the front raised her hand. "Will the reading room be open for children after school?"
8. "Yes," the mayor replied. "Every child in the population of our town is welcome, and there is no priority list."
9. "Everyone may come," he said, and for a moment the hall was quiet.
10. An older man called out, "How will we know when it opens?"
11. "Please spread the news, tell your neighbours, and bring your ideas to the next meeting."
12. "Communication is how a community grows strong," said the mayor, "and this centre belongs to every one of you."

Unit vocabulary reinforced: service, information, priority, population, deliver, location, communication.
Themes: a public building that belongs to everybody, with no list at the door
Cameos: the mayor's answers are the Unit 4 listening text; the librarian and Doctor Sarah staff the new rooms.
