THE TENTH ROOM
Grade 4 - Level 4
The Exhibition is packed away, the nine rooms are closed — and Teacher Yasmin has one more room to show the class.

1. The Tenth Room. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. The Exhibition was over. The hall was quiet, and the chairs stood empty.
3. We took the nine rooms down, one poster at a time.
4. "Nine units, nine rooms," said Maya. "It feels strange to close the doors."
5. Teacher Yasmin smiled. "Then come with me. I will show you the tenth room."
6. She led us out through the school gate and into the street.
7. "The tenth room is everywhere," she said. "It is where your English lives now."
8. At the post counter, Omar greeted us in English, and we answered without thinking.
9. Signs, songs, labels at the market — we found we could read them all.
10. "You carry the room with you," said Teacher Yasmin. "It never closes."
11. That evening, Amal opened a brand-new notebook at her desk.
12. On the cover she wrote THE TENTH ROOM — and left every page ready.
