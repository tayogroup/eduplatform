THE DAY THE BRIDGE OPENED
Grade 4 - Level 4
The scaffold comes down, the ribbon goes up, and the whole town walks across Elena's bridge at last.

1. The Day the Bridge Opened. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. For a whole year, Elena and her team built the bridge over our river.
3. Today the scaffold came down. The bridge stood on its own at last.
4. The mayor came with a ribbon, and half the town came along behind.
5. "A bridge is not for looking at," said Elena. "It is for crossing."
6. Amal and Nora crossed first, whereas the grown-ups had to wait their turn.
7. The drums played, the banner waved, and Omar closed the shop for one more hour.
8. Karim crossed with his toolbox. The caretaker crossed with his keys jingling.
9. Grandma Hana walked out slowly and stopped in the middle to watch the water.
10. "I have waited sixty years for this walk," she said.
11. That evening, the new lamps on the bridge glowed for the very first time.
12. Now the two sides of the river are one town, whichever way you cross.
