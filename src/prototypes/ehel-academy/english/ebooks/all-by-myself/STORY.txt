ALL BY MYSELF
Grade 3 - Level 3
Mina wants to do everything by herself - and everybody in the family has a job they do themselves.

1. All by Myself. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. "I can do it all by myself!" said Mina, at breakfast.
3. She poured the milk herself. Some of it went in the cup.
4. Idris makes his bed himself, every morning before school.
5. Adam cooked lunch himself on Saturday. "I taught myself," he said proudly.
6. I tidy my room myself. Nobody has to ask me.
7. "Can you carry the basket yourself?" Mum asked me. "It is heavy."
8. I tried. Then Idris helped, and we carried it ourselves, together.
9. Even the hen finds its own breakfast, all by itself.
10. "Watch me!" said Mina. She put every toy away herself.
11. Dad smiled. "You did that yourselves," he said. "All of you."
12. Some jobs are just for me, myself. The best jobs we do ourselves, together.
