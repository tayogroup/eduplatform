THE MEAL WE MADE
Grade 4 - Level 4
The basket from the farm becomes a family meal, and Amal follows her food from the soil all the way to the plate.

1. The Meal We Made. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. The basket from the farm sat in the kitchen, full of good things.
3. "Tonight we cook it together," said Mum. "Every hand helps."
4. Grandma Hana washed everything twice. "Clean food is safe food," she said.
5. Amal measured the rice, and Mina counted every cup out loud.
6. Dad kept the fire low and steady. The pot began to sing.
7. Soon the kitchen smelled of spice and fresh rice. Everyone's tummy rumbled.
8. They set the table together and put the biggest dish in the middle.
9. "This meal travelled far," said Amal. "From the soil, to the market, to our plate."
10. Everyone ate slowly and happily. Cooking together had made it taste better.
11. Mina wanted only the sweet part. "One handful," smiled Mum, "after the healthy part."
12. That night Amal wrote: food tastes best when you know its whole journey.
