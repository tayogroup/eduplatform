3. Amal wanted that dictionary. She wanted to master new words and improve her own language.
4. She still had homework, chores and her daily reading. "I must find a balance," she told herself.
5. "Can you manage the contest and your schoolwork?" her mother asked. "I will try my best," said Amal, "and I will continue."
6. Many people in the town worked hard, but one stood out: Omar, who ran the market shop and looked after the post counter.
7. After class the next day she walked to the shop with her notebook and asked him for an interview.
8. At home she used every detail. She checked her spelling and kept her handwriting neat, because she wanted the report to be fair and honest.
9. "Take your time," her father said gently. "Do not leave out anything important." "I won't. This report has a purpose."
10. On the morning of the contest it rained hard. "It is cancelled!" shouted Sami. "Nobody will cancel anything," said Teacher Yasmin. "We will hold it in the library."
11. The class sat in a circle and read in turn. Nora forgot two of her lines. Amal waited quietly, and when her turn came she read clearly.
12. "The winner is Amal. Her report was honest, clear and full of meaning." She opened her new dictionary and found the word effort: work done with care.
