# Grade 2 Computing (computing/grade-2-v2) validation

Reviewed 2026-09-11; its fixes were deployed the same day. Reviewer: Claude (automated measurement + judgement); no human teacher review yet.  
Against the 27-area framework in 'computing-grades validation.docx'. Scale: 1 Unsatisfactory; 2 Needs major improvement; 3 Acceptable with revisions; 4 Strong; 5 Excellent.  
What was reviewed: the standalone lesson build that ehel-comp-g02 launches (computing/grade-2-app, served as app/computing/grade-2-v2), not the Word-pack course under computing/grade-2, which the shell serves when no override applies and which declares a different framework.  

The Word report beside the owner's other validation reports (OneDrive: `computing-grade-2-v2 validation report.docx`) carries the same content; both are written from one script.

## Summary

The build teaches all 31 Cambridge Primary Computing 0059 Stage 2 objectives across ten lessons. A check by hand of every objective against the steps that claim it found each one taught through something the child does and nothing from Stage 3. One objective is only partly met: 2MD.03 asks children to compare different ways of collecting data WITH computing devices, and the lesson compares four ways of which only one uses a device. 67 of the 75 teaching steps can tell a child “not quite”, each of the 116 questions has one key and a reason, and every lesson was played to the end in a browser after this review's fixes, with no errors and nothing wider than a phone screen.

Grade 2 already carries the Grade 1 validation's shared fixes, deployed on 2026-09-11. The one that mattered most was checked again here on the live pages: all ten lessons store every step, are recorded complete, and reopen where the learner left them.

Reading every learner-facing line found eight kinds of error, all corrected. The widest spread were claims about data on computers: that it “does not get lost”, “cannot be spilt on” and “takes up no space at all”, in seven places. Lesson 10 taught that telling jokes marks a robot as fictional, when the course's own smart speakers tell jokes, in seven places. The others were a temperature graph that “means the medicine is working”, a loading spinner that “means” no network where it usually does, robot vacuums and delivery robots described as simpler than they are, a tablet on which you “can never get lost”, and a quiz question that was not English. A home project had a grown-up pour hot water with no cup, as a demonstration of a bug; it is now acted out with an empty cup.

The measurements found three things the reading could not. Six sort labels were read aloud by the voice as “jump slash jump”. Three emoji need 2020 or 2021 devices. Five parts of the laptop and tablet drawings were tap targets of 17 to 23 px on a phone, below the 24 px minimum. All three are fixed. Contrast passes everywhere it was measured.

What is left is content work and work no measurement can do. Four objectives rest on a single activity, and 2MD.03 needs a second way of collecting with a device. There is no recap of the lesson before and no warm-up. Two activities repeat Grade 1's toast and sandwich almost word for word. Beyond that, a teacher needs to read the content and the question keys, and someone needs to watch children use it and walk it with a screen reader. This review's fixes were deployed on 2026-09-11 and all ten lessons were checked again on the live pages.

Change status at 2026-09-11, after the deploy: 10 need no change or are done, 4 have their fix deployed with a human check or an owner decision still open, 13 are open and not started.

Average score 4.1 / 5 across 27 areas; lowest 3 (Assessment Balance, Multimedia Quality, Duplication / overlap); 5 areas at 5.

## Before this review

Grade 2 was redeployed on 2026-09-11 with the Grade 1 validation's kit fixes: a finished lesson is stored complete and a reopened lesson opens where it was left (checked on all ten Grade 2 lessons on the live pages in this review), controls are at least 44 px, labels are at least 13 px, the network map no longer cuts off its labels, and the Sort race game asks each sort step's own question. This review is about what is Grade 2's own.

## What was fixed during this review

Committed as `7cc8338cf` on 2026-09-11, pushed, and deployed to Grade 2 the same day. Grades 1, 3 and 4 were rebuilt on the fixed kit with their lesson data unchanged.

- Data on computers: “it does not get lost” is now “it is harder to lose”; “takes up no space at all” is now “fit on one tablet”; “cannot be spilt on” and “does not get wet” now say that the spill only reached the paper, and that a spare copy can be kept (lessons 7 and 8, seven places).
- Robots: a story robot is marked by feelings and finding things funny, not by telling jokes, which smart speakers do (lesson 10, seven places); robot vacuums are “simple” where their whole program is “forward until a bump” (three places); a delivery robot moves “turn by turn”, not “square by square”.
- A temperature graph going down “shows the fever is going down”, not “means the medicine is working”; a spinner that never stops “usually” means no network (three places, as in Grade 1); a tablet's home button lets you “always find your way back” rather than “never get lost”; “Who is a program's bug the fault of?” is now “A program goes wrong. Where is the bug?”.
- Safety: the “Tea for a robot” home project is acted out with an empty cup and no water.
- Speech: six sort labels written “repeat 3 times, jump / jump, jump, jump”, which the voice read with the word “slash”, are now “A: repeat 3 times, jump. B: jump, jump, jump.”.
- Emoji: toothbrush (2020, three uses) became a tooth, a potted plant (2020) a seedling, a heavy equals sign (2021, two uses) a plain “=”.
- Tap targets (shared kit): the laptop's speaker and charging port and the tablet's volume buttons, charging port and speaker grew from 17 to 23 px to 28 px or more on a 375 px screen, into empty space, with no ring reaching another. Only Grade 2 draws these figures.

## What this review could not establish

- No human teacher has read the content or the 116 question keys, and no child has been watched using a lesson.
- No screen reader was used, and nothing was opened on the school's own devices.
- The class chat, hand-up, Join class and Wehel controls were not exercised with a live launch.
- Progress was checked on the live pages with the browser's own store; a real launch, which writes the same events to the school's server, was not observed from here.
- Three shared checkers cannot evaluate a Computing build and are reported as not run: audit-stage-coverage.py reads only the Mathematics frameworks; check-judging.py passes every Computing step; check-answer-keys.py cannot read the question format.
- The learning-time estimates are derived from step kinds, not measured.

## Verdict on Grade 2 Computing

Nothing found stops the children already using Grade 2. The progress defect that cost every learner something was fixed before this review and is confirmed on all ten live lessons. The errors this review found are corrected and live. The build teaches all 31 Stage 2 objectives through activities and holds the Stage 3 line.

It is thinner than the other grades: four objectives rest on one activity, and 2MD.03 is only partly met. That, a recap and a warm-up are the content work left. Before it is called validated rather than measured, a teacher should read the content and the question keys, and somebody should watch a child use a lesson and walk one with a screen reader.
