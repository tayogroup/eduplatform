What this lesson is about

By the end of this lesson you will be able to: Name flat shapes, including quadrilaterals; say whether a shape is regular or irregular; find a line of symmetry on a shape; reflect a shape over a mirror line; measure the perimeter of a shape; work out the area of a shape.
