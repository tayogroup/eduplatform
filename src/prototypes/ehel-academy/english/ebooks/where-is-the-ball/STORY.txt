WHERE IS THE BALL?
Grade 1 - Level 1
Grade 1 shelf - Fable series, book 5 (Term 2, Unit 3: Fun and Games)

1. Where Is the Ball? Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Kiki had a big red ball. Then the ball was gone.
3. "Is it on the bench?" No. A little bird was on the bench.
4. "Is it under the tree?" No. A rabbit was under the tree.
5. "Is it next to the water?" No. A duck was next to the water.
6. "Is it in the grass?" No. A frog was in the grass.
7. The little elephant looked left. "Nothing here."
8. The goat looked right. "Nothing here."
9. Then the chick said, "Peep! Look under me!"
10. The ball was under the chick. The chick was on the ball!
11. Everybody laughed. "You are a very small hill," said Kiki.
12. Then they all played. Bounce, roll, throw, catch!

Unit vocabulary reinforced: on, under, next to, in, left, right, ball, bounce, roll, throw, catch, rabbit, duck, frog.
Themes: a search that teaches where rather than what
Cameos: Kiki, the little elephant, the goat and the chick; the rabbit, duck and frog are Unit 3's own story animals, drawn for the first time here.
