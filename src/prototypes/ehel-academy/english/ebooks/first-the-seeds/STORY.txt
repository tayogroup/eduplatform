FIRST THE SEEDS
Grade 3 - Level 3
Amal series, book 3 of 4, from the listening plan "Planning the Garden" (Term 2, Unit 5: Actions and Activities)

1. First the Seeds. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. "Let's make a plan for our new garden," I said. "What should we do first?"
3. "First, we will search for good seeds," said Leo. So we went to see Omar.
4. "We want strong seeds," he said, "that will grow into healthy plants."
5. "Then we build a small fence, to protect the young leaves."
6. A goat came to test the fence on the very first evening. The fence held.
7. "After that, we water the garden every day, even when it does not rain."
8. Some mornings I did not want to. I went anyway, because the plan said every day.
9. Two whole weeks, and nothing. "The plan did not say hurry," said Leo.
10. Then one green leaf. Then four.
11. "Finally, we celebrate when the first flower grows," said Leo. And it did.
12. First the seeds, then the fence, then the water, and then this. We remembered every step.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: search, build, protect, develop, complete, celebrate, discuss, offer.
Themes: a plan you can say in order is a plan you can finish; waiting is one of the steps
Cameos: Leo's four steps - seeds, fence, water, celebration - are the Unit 5 spoken plan, in its own order.
