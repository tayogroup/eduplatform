THE LETTER FROM LONG AGO
Grade 4 - Level 4
Grandma Hana knows the handwriting on the attic letter — and the telescope has one more sky to show.

1. The Letter from Long Ago. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. The letter from the attic lay open on the table all through dinner.
3. "That is my father's handwriting," said Grandma Hana at last.
4. Long ago, her father watched the stars from this very hill.
5. The letter described a comet he saw the year Hana was born.
6. "He wrote it for whoever found the telescope," she said. "That is you."
7. Amal and Idris polished the brass until it shone like new.
8. That night, they carried the telescope up the hill together.
9. The moon came first, pale and curved, with its quiet craters.
10. Then a planet with rings, hanging in the dark like a bracelet.
11. Grandma Hana looked the longest of all. "Hello, Father," she whispered.
12. They put the letter back in its box — with a new one beside it.
