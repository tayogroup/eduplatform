THE CIRCULAR PLAN
Grade 4 - Level 4
Amal (Grade 4) series, book 4 (Term 2, Unit 4: Community and Communication)

1. The Circular Plan. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Teacher Yasmin wrote the week's challenge on the board: "Create a way to share information with the community."
3. "Do you mean a newspaper?" asked Amal. "Something like that. But you must use what you already know."
4. The students talked at break. "We could build one enormous sign!" said Leo. "People will just walk past it," said Nora.
5. "It has to be circular," Amal added. "That is my rule." "Why circular?" Leo teased. Amal only smiled.
6. After school she went to the town library and looked at the location of every shelf, news board and reading chair.
7. "Good service always starts small," the librarian said kindly.
8. Amal stopped at the school gate. "Everybody passes this spot," she whispered. "This is the right location."
9. The next day they brought cardboard, tape, scissors and markers. Amal drew the circle. Leo wrote the headlines. Nora added a comic.
10. "Check your spelling before you stick anything down," said Teacher Yasmin, "and do not forget to erase the pencil lines."
11. By Friday it stood by the gate. "Turn it," Amal explained, "and read what is on each side."
12. "It is not about how big the quantity is," said Nora. "It is about what people learn." People stopped, spun the circle, and sometimes added a page of their own.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: circular, plain, service, location, quantity, quality, priority, information, fiction, erase.
Themes: a small thing in the right place beats a big thing nobody stops at
Cameos: Leo, Nora and the librarian are all from the Unit 4 story.
