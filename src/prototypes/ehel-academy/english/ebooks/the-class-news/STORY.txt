THE CLASS NEWS
Grade 3 - Level 3
Notebooks out, camera ready: this week Amal's class makes the news, and sends it a long, long way.

1. The Class News. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. "This week," said Teacher Yasmin, "our class will make the news."
3. Maya brought a camera. "A photograph tells a story too," she said.
4. Sami and I walked round the school with a notebook, hunting for news.
5. We wrote about the new books in the library, and Maya took a photograph.
6. Daniel spoke like the man on the radio. "Good morning! Here is the news!"
7. Leo drew a big television out of a box, with a hole for the screen.
8. We read our news through the box, one story at a time.
9. Then Teacher Yasmin helped us send it all by email to another school.
10. "The internet can carry your words a long, long way," she said.
11. Two days later, an email came back, full of their news.
12. Now we make the news every Friday. Bring your notebook!
