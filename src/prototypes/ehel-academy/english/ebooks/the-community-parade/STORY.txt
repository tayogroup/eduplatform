THE COMMUNITY PARADE
Grade 4 - Level 4
Amal series, book 6 (Term 2, Unit 6: People in Society)

1. The Community Parade. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Once a year our town holds a parade for the people who keep it working.
3. The carpenter came first, then the engineer, then the caretaker who unlocks the school every morning at six.
4. Omar closed the shop for one hour - the only hour it closes all year.
5. Maya walked the whole route with her notebook, asking everybody the same question: what do you do?
6. Our neighbours carried a banner. One family arrived here as refugees; the other has farmed the same field for a hundred years.
7. Sami's father is a nurse. Noah's mother is a lawyer. Both were marching.
8. Teacher Yasmin marched with the school. So did every child who wanted to.
9. There was music, and there was too much of it, and nobody minded.
10. At the end, the mayor gave the shortest speech I have ever heard: "Thank you. Go home. Come back next year."
11. Maya's article was one page of names. Just names, and what each person does.
12. I read every one. It took a while. That was rather the point.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: janitor, carpenter, labourer, engineer, merchant, governor, lawyer, artist, photographer, messenger, hero, refugee, immigrant, tenant.
Themes: a town is the people who keep it working; a page of names
Cameos: the parade and the refugee and immigrant neighbours are both from the Unit 6 readings.
