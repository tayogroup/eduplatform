What the app assesses, and what it does not Every step states whether the platform can assess it or whether an assessor signs it off. The two modules differ honestly: in Shapes and Measurements 63% is assessed here, because a reading or a calculation is right or it is not. In Carpentry only 29% is — sawing to a line is judged on the work, not on a screen. Neither module claims to deliver a Kenyan Level 4 unit; both name the units they feed.
Not yet trade-reviewed The performance criteria and the teaching were written from published curricula and have not been read by a practising tradesperson or a TVET assessor. That review comes before any of this is put in front of a learner.
