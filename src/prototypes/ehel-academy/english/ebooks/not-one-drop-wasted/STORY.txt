1. Not One Drop Wasted. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. The rain did not come. The well was getting low.
3. The grass was dry. Duku was thirsty.
4. Musa filled his pot to the top. Then he spilled half of it.
5. "Do not waste it," said Koko. "The well is low."
6. So Musa carried his pot slowly. Not one drop fell.
7. The goat washed her feet in the river instead.
8. Kiki caught the drips from the roof in a little cup.
9. The chicks drank from the cup, one at a time.
10. For eight days, nobody wasted a single drop.
11. Then the sky went grey. Drip. Drip. Rain!
12. Everybody danced in the puddles. The well was full again.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: water, well, low, empty, waste, wasted, drip, drop, carried, thirsty, rain, puddle.
Themes: a shortage everybody solves at once, in small ways
Cameos: Musa, Duku, Koko, Kiki and the goat; the well is The Well in the Village's.
