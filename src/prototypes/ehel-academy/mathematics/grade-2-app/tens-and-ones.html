A quick look

Small amounts you can see without counting. Big ones you guess, then count to check.

Numbers in words

A number has a name as well as a numeral. 42 is forty-two.

4

Build it with tens and ones

Add tens and ones. A ten is a stick of ten ones.

5

Which is more?

Look at the tens first. Only when the tens are the same do the ones decide.

6

First, second, third

One, two, three say how many. First, second, third say which place.

7

Round to the nearest 10

Which ten is nearer? Tap it.

8

Odd or even?

Put the counters in pairs. If one is left on its own, the number is odd.

9

Make 20, make 100

Which number finishes it off? The bar shows how much is still missing.

10

Adding two-digit numbers

Add the tens, then add the ones. Put the two answers together.

11

Taking away by jumping back

Jump back the tens first, then the ones.

12

One undoes the other

Adding and taking away undo each other. That is how you check an answer.

13

Arrays: 2s, 5s and 10s

Rows of dots make an array. Change the rows and what is in each row.

14

Know them by heart

The 1, 2, 5 and 10 times tables. Answer from memory if you can.

15

Sharing and grouping

Share 12 sweets between 3 friends: one each, round and round.
