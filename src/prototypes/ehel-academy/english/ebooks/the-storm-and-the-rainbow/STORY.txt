THE STORM AND THE RAINBOW
Grade 3 - Level 3
Thunder like a giant drum, lightning at the window - and then, over the whole town, a rainbow.

1. The Storm and the Rainbow. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. The morning was warm, but the sky grew cloudy and dark.
3. "A storm is coming," said Mum. "Everybody inside."
4. The thunder banged like a giant drum. Mina held my hand.
5. Lightning lit the whole room white for a second.
6. "Thunder is only a sound," said Dad. "It cannot hurt you."
7. We watched the rain from the window. The street turned into little rivers.
8. Grandma Hana made warm tea, and the storm slowly walked away.
9. Then the sun came out - and Mina shouted, "LOOK!"
10. A rainbow stood over the town, from the market to the school.
11. We counted its colours from the doorway, every single one.
12. "Every season brings its own sky," said Grandma Hana. "This one brought us a rainbow."
