MUSA AND THE WEATHER
Grade 1 - Level 1
Musa the zebra watches the weather change from sunny to cloudy, windy and rainy, and loves every kind.

1. Musa and the Weather. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Musa looks at the sky every day.
3. Today it is sunny. It is hot!
4. Musa rests under the shady tree.
5. Now it is cloudy. Where is the sun?
6. Now it is windy. Whoosh!
7. The kite dances in the windy sky.
8. Now it is rainy. Drip, drop, drip!
9. Musa waits under the big tree.
10. The chicks splash in the puddle!
11. The rain stops. A rainbow comes!
12. Sunny, windy, rainy - Musa loves them all!
