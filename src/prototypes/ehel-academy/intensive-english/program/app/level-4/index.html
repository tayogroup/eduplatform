Ehel Academy · Intensive English

Level 4 — Advanced

By the end: Can understand the main ideas of complex speech and writing on familiar and less familiar topics, take part in conversations, meetings and debates fluently enough that neither side is strained, and write clear, detailed emails, reports and opinion pieces that weigh advantages and disadvantages.

B212 lessonsweeks 40–48 of the programme3 days per lessonabout 84 hours

Part 1 · Speaking with confidence

1Conversations that flowYou will be able to start, keep going and steer a natural conversation with people you don’t know well. 2Saying exactly what you meanYou will be able to work out new words from their parts, and describe things precisely. 3Telling it wellYou will be able to tell a detailed account, and report fairly what others said and meant. 4If things had been differentYou will be able to talk about past decisions, regrets and what might have happened.
Then: Part 1 review & check (2 days)

Part 2 · Information and ideas

5The news, and how far to trust itYou will be able to follow news stories and judge how reliable they are. 6Facts, figures and trendsYou will be able to describe numbers and trends accurately, and say what they do and don’t show. 7Causes, effects and careful claimsYou will be able to discuss causes and effects in health, the environment or society without overstating. 8Writing that flowsYou will be able to write clear, connected emails and short essays that are easy to follow.
Then: Part 2 review & check (2 days)

Part 3 · Persuading and deciding

9Making a caseYou will be able to argue for a position with evidence, and answer objections. 10Negotiating and solving problemsYou will be able to negotiate, set conditions and reach an agreement both sides accept. 11Formal writingYou will be able to write formal letters, emails and short reports in the right register. 12Presenting and leading a discussionYou will be able to give a clear presentation, handle questions and plan your English after the course.
Then: B2 review & check (2 days) · end of programme
