5. At break time he stood in the corner, holding his bag.
6. I remembered exactly how that feels. So I walked directly over.

9. By Friday, Theo was probably the noisiest one at our table.
10. "You did what I did," said Nora quietly, smiling.
11. Kindness is a thing you carry. I learned that from her.
12. Now Theo waits by the gate, watching for the next new pupil.
