2. Teacher Yasmin brought a bright box into class. "This is our Box of Ideas," she said.
3. "Every child will add one idea. It can be a dream, a memory, or a hope."
4. The whole room went quiet. An idea is easy to have and hard to write down.
5. Nora wrote about a library with no walls at all. That was her dream.
6. Idris wrote about the smell of rain on hot ground. That was his memory.
7. I could not choose. I had three ideas, and not one of them felt sincere enough.
8. Sami wrote one line: "I hope my sister gets well." Nobody said anything for a moment.
9. Teacher Yasmin said, "Feelings are not good or bad. Sadness is not a mistake. It is information."
10. So I wrote what I actually felt, which was that I was scared about the contest next week.
11. We read them out loud, and nobody laughed. Every single idea was allowed.
12. An idea is small until you say it. Then it belongs to everybody, and it is not heavy any more.
