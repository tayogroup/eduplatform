THE CARETAKER'S KEYS
Grade 4 - Level 4
Amal (Grade 4) series, book 5 (Term 2, Unit 6: People in Society)

1. The Caretaker's Keys. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. The caretaker reaches the school before anybody else, while the corridor is still dark.
3. He carries a ring of keys, and he knows which key belongs to which door without looking.
4. He unlocks the gate first, so that no child ever waits outside in the rain.
5. Then he sweeps the corridor from one end to the other. For eleven years, the same corridor.
6. He wipes the board, straightens the chairs, and picks up the paper yesterday's class dropped.
7. By the time the first bell rings, the school looks as if nobody had ever made a mess in it.
8. Amal had walked past him a hundred times. On the day of the parade, she finally stopped.
9. "Thank you," she said. "I saw your job on a sign this morning, and I had never said it before."
10. The caretaker leaned on his broom. "Nobody notices when a floor is clean," he said. "They only notice when it is not."
11. "I noticed," said Amal, and she meant it.
12. Heroes do not always wear capes, she wrote in her journal that night. Sometimes they carry brooms.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: caretaker, hero, respect, article, personal.
