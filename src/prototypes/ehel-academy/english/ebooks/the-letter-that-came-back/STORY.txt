THE LETTER THAT CAME BACK
Grade 4 - Level 4
A letter stamped 'wrong address' comes back to Omar's post counter, and he and Amal will not rest until it finds its person.

1. The Letter That Came Back. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. On Monday a letter came back to the post counter. Across it was stamped: wrong address.
3. "Somebody wrote this with care," said Omar. "And somebody else is waiting for it."
4. Amal copied the name into her notebook. Nobody at the market knew it.
5. Grandmother Salma studied the envelope. "The number is smudged," she said, "but the street is ours."
6. Omar closed the counter early. "A letter can wait," he said, "but not for ever."
7. They asked at every door along the road. People shook their heads kindly.
8. At the last house they found Theo. His family had just moved to town.
9. "That is our name!" said Theo. "Our post still goes to the old house."
10. Theo ran inside with the letter. Through the window came a happy shout.
11. "Thank you for not giving up," said Theo. Omar smiled. "Every letter is waiting for somebody."
12. Back at the counter, Amal wrote one line in her notebook: every letter is a person.
