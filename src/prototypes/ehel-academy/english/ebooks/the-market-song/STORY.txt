THE MARKET SONG
Grade 4 - Level 4
Amal (Grade 4) series, book 5 (Term 1, Unit 3: Food and Health)

1. The Market Song. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. On Saturday the whole village goes to the market, and the stalls sing before the people do.
3. Grandma Hana gave Amal a basket and one instruction: "Bring back only what is fresh."
4. "Rice and lamb and fresh green peas — gather them with care, if you please!"
5. Amal chose the rice first, and let a handful run through her fingers to check it was dry and clean.
6. Omar weighed out the lamb. "This came in this morning," he said. "Smell it, and you will know."
7. The peas were bright green and firm. Amal put back the soft ones without being told.
8. "Wash them well and cook them slow — healthy food helps children grow!"
9. At home she washed every piece under clean water, because dust and pesticide can hide on the skin.
10. Grandma Hana let her stir the pot. "Slowly," she said. "Good food is not in a hurry."
11. They ate together at the table, and Amal named every item in her list, with a comma between each one.
12. That night Amal sang the market song to herself. It was not a poem for school. It was a rule she could remember.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: rice, lamb, fresh, gather, bakery, pesticide, comma, labour.
Themes: a rhyme that turns out to be a rule you can shop by
