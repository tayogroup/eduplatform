THE DAY THE FIRE BELL RANG
Grade 2 - Level 2
Zuri series, book 2b (Term 1, Unit 2: Good Neighbours and Jobs)

1. The Day the Fire Bell Rang. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. The bell rang in the middle of the lesson. It was not the lesson bell. It was the fire bell.
3. The fire engine came down Warta Street. It was red, and it was very, very loud.
4. The firefighter had a helmet, boots and gloves. "This is my equipment," she said.
5. The ladder went up the wall of the shop. Up, up, up, all the way to the roof.
6. Then the hose sent water right up after it. Zuri had never seen water go so high.
7. The little fire was out. Nobody was hurt. The shopkeeper said thank you six times.
8. "Hold this," said the firefighter. Zuri held the helmet. It was heavier than her book.
9. Zuri wrote it all down. Helmet. Boots. Gloves. Ladder. Hose. And one red fire engine.
10. Back at school, Miss Twiga asked what each thing was for. Zuri knew every answer.
11. Then Zuri painted the fire engine. She made it as red as she possibly could.
12. "When I am big," said Zuri, "I will help too." "Me too," said Kiki.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: firefighter, helmet, boots, gloves, ladder, hose, equipment, rescuing, heavy, safe.
Themes: equipment is not a costume; wanting the job in your turn
Cameos: the window cleaner monkey up the ladder; the shopkeeper hen; Miss Twiga's follow-up lesson.
