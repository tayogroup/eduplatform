TWO NEIGHBOURS
Grade 4 - Level 4
Amal (Grade 4) series, book 3 (Term 2, Unit 6: People in Society)

1. Two Neighbours. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Theo and his family are refugees. They left their home because it was not safe.
3. They travelled a long way, crossing three borders and sleeping in strange rooms, before they reached our town.
4. Now Theo lives at the end of our street, in a small house with a garden full of tomato plants.
5. He waters them every evening after school.
6. Next door lives Omar, who came here as an immigrant.
7. He moved to our town to open a market stall and build a better life.
8. He wanted his children to grow up somewhere calm and safe, with good schools and kind neighbours.
9. Every morning, before the sun is fully up, Omar arranges baskets of mangoes and bags of spices.
10. He always saves the sweetest fruit for the children on his street.
11. "A refugee runs from danger. An immigrant walks towards a dream," says Theo's mother. "We should welcome both."
12. Theo says he is learning to feel at home here, one football match and one new word at a time.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: refugee, immigrant, tenant, neighbour, merchant, personal, hero.
Themes: one family ran from danger, one walked towards a dream; both are welcome
Cameos: Theo, his mother and Omar are the Unit 6 reading's own; the tomato garden is hers too.
