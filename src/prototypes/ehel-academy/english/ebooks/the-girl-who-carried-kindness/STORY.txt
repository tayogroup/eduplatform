THE GIRL WHO CARRIED KINDNESS
Grade 3 - Level 3
Amal series, book 6 (Term 2, Unit 6: Describing People and Things)

1. The Girl Who Carried Kindness. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. My cousin Noah is my favourite person in the family. He is kind and honest, and he never says one thing while thinking another.
3. Nora is my favourite person outside it. She is friendly and calm, even when everybody else is busy.
4. There are two roads to school. One is short and rough. The other is long and smooth.
5. I always take the short one, because I am always late. Nora always takes the long one.
6. One morning I found out why. A junior student walks that way, and Nora walks beside her.
7. "Is that not a lot of extra walking?" I asked. "It is," said Nora. "But she is small, and the road is busy."
8. I thought about that all day. Being clever is not the same thing as being kind.
9. The next morning I was careless and left late again. But this time I took the long road.
10. Nora was already there. We walked together, all three of us, and we were not late at all.
11. Later that day, Nora and I sat together under the tall tree. Nothing needed to be said.
12. Kind is not a thing you are. It is a thing you carry, and you can pick it up any morning you like.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: kind, friendly, honest, kindness, care, busy, calm, able, careless, popular, rough, tough, similar, alive, favourite, extra.
Themes: kindness as a choice you make again each morning, not a character trait
Cameos: cousin Noah and the two roads to school, both from the Unit 6 readings.
