THE BENCH IN THE YARD
Grade 3 - Level 3
The old bench wobbles, so Amal, Sami and Dad mend it step by step.

1. The Bench in the Yard. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. The old bench in the yard wobbled. Nobody sat on it any more.
3. "Let us mend it," said Sami. "First, we need a plan."
4. Omar gave us a strong flat stone from behind his shop.
5. Then Dad brought his toolbox after work.
6. Sami held the bench steady, and I slid the stone under the short leg.
7. After that, Dad tightened every screw, one by one.
8. Then we washed the bench and let the sun dry it.
9. Finally we tested it. Sami sat down first, very slowly.
10. It did not wobble. It did not creak. It just held.
11. Now the little ones sit there every break time.
12. A yard with a good bench is a friendlier yard.
