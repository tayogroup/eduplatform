THE FROZEN POND
Grade 3 - Level 3
Dad wakes Amal early, and the mountain pond has turned to ice.

1. The Frozen Pond. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. One cold morning, Dad woke me early. "Bring your jacket," he said.
3. We set off up the quiet mountain trail.
4. The air grew cooler with every step. My breath made little clouds.
5. "What is the temperature?" I asked. "Maybe two degrees," said Dad.
6. Near the top we found the little pond. It had frozen in the night.
7. The water was a smooth, shining sheet of ice.
8. I knelt down and knocked on it gently. It was hard, like glass.
9. "Do not step on it," said Dad. "Thin ice cannot hold you."
10. We watched the sun climb, and the frost began to shine.
11. On the way down, the ice had turned back to water.
12. The weather can change in one morning. I saw it happen.
