1. The Spelling Contest. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Our class was getting ready for the spelling contest. Teacher Yasmin gave us a practice list on Monday.
3. The list had thirty words on it. Some of them were easy. Some were not easy at all.
4. Nora studied at the library after school. I studied at home with Adam.
5. I lost my eraser on Tuesday, so Idris lent me his. A good brother is a good supply.
6. On Wednesday we wrote a short report about spelling rules and read it out to the class.
7. Teacher Yasmin taught us a rule: "i before e, most of the time." Rules have exceptions.
8. On Thursday I got three words wrong. I wanted to give up.
9. Grandma Hana said, "An author does not write a book in one night. Study a little, every day."
10. So I studied the details. Just five words each evening, until I knew every one of them.
11. On Friday the contest began. When my word came, I closed my eyes and spelled it slowly.
12. I did not win. But I knew every word on my card, and Teacher Yasmin said that is what learning looks like.
