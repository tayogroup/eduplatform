THE WELL IN THE VILLAGE
Grade 1 - Level 1
Amal (Grade 1) series, book 8 (Term 3, Unit 8: Wonderful Water)

1. The Well in the Village. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Every morning, Amal walked to the well with Adam. They carried an empty pot.
3. "Fill it slowly," said Adam. "Water is precious. We must not waste one drop."
4. Amal helped pull the rope. Up came the pot, full and heavy. "We did it!"
5. At home, Mama used the water. "We use water to cook," she said.
6. "And we use water to wash," said Amal. She washed her hands until they were clean.
7. "And we use water to drink," said little Hodan. She drank a big cup and smiled.
8. One day, the land was very dry. No rain came. The grass was brown.
9. "The well is getting low," said Adam. "We must share and be careful."
10. Then the sky went grey. Big clouds came. Drip... drip... drip. "Rain!"
11. The children ran outside and danced in the rain. The rain filled the well.
12. "How lucky we are to have water," said Amal. "Water is wonderful."

Unit vocabulary reinforced: water, well, rain, drop, pot, wet, dry, clean, drink, wash, cook, grow, share, river, cloud, grey.
Themes: sharing a scarce thing; the relief when the rain finally comes
Cameos: Adam carries the pot and little Hodan drinks the first cup, both from the Unit 8 reading.
