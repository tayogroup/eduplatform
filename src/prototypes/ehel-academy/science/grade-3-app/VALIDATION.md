Second version, after the fix pass the first validation asked for. ## Appendix B: the second review's findings and decisions (all fixed)

### Kit (already FIXED in lesson-kit, do not edit the kit)
- magnetPoles: the flipped magnet drew S facing S while the voice said "north faces north". Now the left magnet is S|N; the right starts S|N (N facing S: attract) and flips to N|S (N facing N: repel).
- moonPhases: day labels are now 1, 5, 8, 12, 16, 19, 23, 27, 30 (a cycle of about 29 and a half days), not 1 to 25.
- measure(): a step may now set "dim": "tall" or "deep" (default "long") for the spoken line "X is N centimetres tall/deep".
- askQuestion(): a step may now set "findOuts": a list with ONE follow-up per question (same shape as "findOut": {"ask", "opts", "why"}), in the same order as "questions". The builder checks the count and that each has one right option. "findOut" stays as the fallback and must remain.
- FIGURES.organs: the heart now sits between the lungs.
- experiment(): "your prediction WAS what happened" is now "your prediction was right after all".
- blockGraph(): the button now says "+ 1 mark" (singular); a step may set "unitOne" to override.
- SIMS.separate: the salt button no longer says "then taste"; the voice says "Only with a grown-up, taste one drop: salty!".
