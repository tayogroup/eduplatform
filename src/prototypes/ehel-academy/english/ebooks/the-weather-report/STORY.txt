THE WEATHER REPORT
Grade 4 - Level 4
Amal (Grade 4) series, book 4 (Term 1, Unit 2: Nature & Weather)

1. The Weather Report. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. The weather desk stands in a small room beside the lake. Every morning somebody sits down and tells the whole town what the sky is doing.
3. Today Teacher Yasmin's class had been invited to watch. Amal was allowed to hold the script.
4. "Good morning, listeners. It is foggy near the lake this morning, so please walk slowly and take care on the path until the mist clears."
5. "By midday the sun will come out, and it will be warm and pleasant for anyone visiting the meadow or the bay."
6. "The breeze may pick up a little near the water," she said, "so hold on to your hat."
7. "But the weather will not stay calm all day. Tonight a storm may bring rain and hail. Bring your washing in early and shut your windows tight."
8. "If you hear thunder, go inside and stay calm — and never shelter under a tall tree during a storm."
9. Amal looked up from the script. That last line was the one her class had learned only the week before.
10. "Tomorrow should be calmer, with light cloud in the morning and clearer skies by the afternoon."
11. "Farmers near the canyon fields will be glad of a little extra moisture after such a dry week. That is all from the weather desk today."
12. That night the storm came exactly as the report had said. Amal shut her window, and she was not frightened at all.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: foggy, storm, hail, moisture, surface, canyon, meadow, bay, calm.
Themes: a forecast is advice, and advice is only useful if you act on it
Cameos: the broadcast is the Unit 2 listening text; Amal holds the script.
