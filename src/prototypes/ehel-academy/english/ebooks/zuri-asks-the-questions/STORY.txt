3. "What do you do?" Zuri asked the bus driver. "I am driving," he said. "All day long."
4. "And what do you do?" "I am cleaning," said the window cleaner, from the top of his ladder.
5. "I am selling," said the shopkeeper. "Fruit today. Bread tomorrow."
6. "I am growing," said the farmer. "Everything you eat starts here, in this field."
7. "I am helping," said the doctor. "People come here when they feel poorly."
8. "I am teaching," said Miss Twiga. "That is a job too, you know."
9. "I am rescuing," said the firefighter. "Mostly I am checking that nothing needs rescuing."
10. Zuri read her notepad back. Driving. Cleaning. Selling. Growing. Helping. Teaching. Rescuing.
11. Then she wrote the whole report on the class board, in her very best letters.
12. "Seven jobs," said Zuri, "and every one of them helps somebody on our street."
