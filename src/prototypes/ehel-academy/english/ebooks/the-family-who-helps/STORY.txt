THE FAMILY WHO HELPS
Grade 3 - Level 3
Amal series, book 1 (Term 1, Unit 1: All About Me and My Family)

1. The Family Who Helps. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. My name is Amal. I am eight years old, and I live with my family in a small house near the town centre.
3. There are a lot of us. Adam is my older brother, Idris is younger, and Mina is the littlest of all.
4. Grandma Hana lives with us too. At night she tells us stories - some of them true, some brand new.
5. Every evening we eat together. Dad asks each of us to name one good thing that happened that day.
6. It is my duty to keep my room tidy. It is Adam's duty to help Idris with his reading.
7. Mum says a family is a place where everybody is needed. Even Mina has a job: she puts her toys away.
8. At school we show respect too. We listen when other people talk, even when we are bursting to speak.
9. In a public place we walk calmly. In our own rooms at home we can be as noisy as we like.
10. Nora and I walk the junior students to their classrooms. It can feel confusing to be new at a big school.
11. Teacher Yasmin says good listening means "I care what you think." I am practising every day.
12. My family helps me, and I help them. That is what makes our house feel warm.

Themes: a household where everybody is needed; respect as small daily acts
Cameos: Adam, Idris, Mina and Grandma Hana from the Unit 1 readings; Teacher Yasmin and Nora at school.
