THE DAY OF KIND WORDS
Grade 4 - Level 4
A new football brings out jealousy on the school yard, until Sami and Adam learn what teamwork and one kind word can do.

1. The Day of Kind Words. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Sami brought a new football to school. Adam wanted it at once, and felt jealous.
3. "May I play?" asked Adam. Sami kept the ball close. "Maybe later."
4. Adam stood by the bench, annoyed and a little miserable.
5. Nora came over. "A thoughtful word costs nothing," she said kindly.
6. At break, Sami's game went wrong. Nobody would pass, and everybody felt cross.
7. "A ball is no fun alone," said Amal. "Teamwork makes the game."
8. Sami thought about it. Then he rolled the ball across to Adam.
9. "Two captains, one team," said Sami. Adam grinned, and the jealous feeling melted away.
10. They picked the teams fairly and played until the bell rang.
11. "That was generous," said Nora. Sami smiled. "Kindness is easy to share."
12. Adam walked home grateful and peaceful. One kind word had turned the whole day round.
