THE ANTS AND THE BIG CRUMB
Grade 2 - Level 2
Zuri series, book 6c (Term 2, Unit 6: All About Bugs)

1. The Ants and the Big Crumb. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Zuri dropped one crumb of her bread. She was going to pick it up. She never got the chance.
3. An ant found it. One ant, out of the whole garden, and it took less than a minute.
4. The ant pushed. The crumb did not move. An ant is strong, but a crumb is very big.
5. So the ant ran all the way back to the anthill. It did not give up. It went for help.
6. Two ants. They pushed together. Still too heavy.
7. Then ten ants came out of the anthill at once.
8. And up it went. Ten ants, one crumb, and no fuss at all.
9. The line crawled all the way across the flat stone.
10. Over the log. Under the big leaf. Round the plant instead of through it.
11. And in it went, right into the anthill, where Zuri could not follow.
12. "A little and a little," wrote Zuri, "makes a lot. Ask any ant."

Unit vocabulary reinforced: crawl, carry, push, lift, collect, ant, anthill, crumb, heavy, together, over, under, into.
Themes: one is not enough and ten is; going for help is not giving up
Cameos: the anthill and the marching line are the Six-Leg Club's own; the crumb is Zuri's breakfast.
