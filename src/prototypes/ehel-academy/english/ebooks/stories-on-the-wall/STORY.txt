STORIES ON THE WALL
Grade 3 - Level 3
The new reading wall is finished — now it needs its stories.

1. Stories on the Wall. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. The new wall stood straight behind the garden. It was strong, but it was empty.
3. "A reading wall needs stories," said Teacher Yasmin. "Let us fill it up."
4. First, Amal pinned up her poem about the garden birds.
5. Then Leo painted his blue waterfall beside it.
6. Nora copied out her best page in her neatest writing.
7. Soon the wall was full of colour, from the middle to both sides.
8. On Friday the whole class came outside to read the wall.
9. Even the goat came back. It did not eat a single page.
10. "The wall protects our garden," said Amal. "And now it feeds us stories."
11. Grandma Hana came to read the poems, and she stayed until sunset.
12. Our garden grows flowers on one side and stories on the other.
