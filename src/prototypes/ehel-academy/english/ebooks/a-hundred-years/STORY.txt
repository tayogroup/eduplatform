A HUNDRED YEARS
Grade 3 - Level 3
Grandma Hana opens her old brown photo box, and fifty years - half a century - come out of it.

1. A Hundred Years. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. In the evening, Grandma Hana opened her old brown box.
3. Inside were photographs from long ago, small and grey.
4. "This is my school," she said. "That was fifty years in the past."
5. "Half a century!" I counted on my fingers. "Five whole decades!"
6. In the photograph, the town had no big road and no clock tower.
7. "Was your school ancient?" asked Idris. Grandma laughed. "It was modern then!"
8. "One day, our photographs will look old too," I said.
9. "What will the future bring?" asked Idris. "Flying buses?"
10. Grandma Hana said, "A century is a hundred years, and it goes by day by day."
11. So we took a new photograph, all of us together, for the family box.
12. Somebody in the future will find it, and say: look how young Grandma was!
