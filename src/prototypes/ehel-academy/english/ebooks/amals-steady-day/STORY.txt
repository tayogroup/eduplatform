2. Amal wakes up early, before anybody else in the house is ready.
3. First she helps her mother sweep the floor. It is a small task, but small tasks show real effort.
4. Then she boils the water for the morning tea, standing well back from the hot pot.
5. She folds her school clothes neatly and packs her bag. She checks twice that her reading book is inside.
6. Amal walks to school with Maya. They talk about their homework the whole way, and the road is never long.
7. She likes to arrive early. There is time to greet Teacher Yasmin and put her books away calmly, instead of rushing.
8. In class there is a daily reading time. For a few quiet minutes Amal loses herself in a story.
9. At break she plays with Sami and Theo. She never forgets that the bell will ring soon.
10. After school come the chores, and then the homework. Amal does both before she allows herself to rest.
11. In the evening she keeps a balance. There is still a little time to draw, or to read something just for fun.
12. "If I use my time well, I can do it all," Amal says. She goes to bed proud of a steady, usual day.
