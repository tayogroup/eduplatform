8. "Well done, Nora," said Teacher Yasmin. "A metre is always the same length."
9. There are one hundred centimetres in one metre. The floor was three metres long.
10. Sami carried the heavy basket. Maya helped, so the load was fair for both.
11. "I am the tallest, so I sit first!" said Theo. "Being tall does not mean you go first," said Amal.
12. "A fair measure is the same for everyone," said Teacher Yasmin. "And so is a kind heart."
