THE STORM AND THE SCIENCE TENT
Grade 4 - Level 4
Amal series, book 2 (Term 1, Unit 2: Nature & Weather)

1. The Storm and the Science Tent. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Teacher Yasmin planned a science fair, and the whole class built a tent on the village field.
3. Nora's project was the weather. Mine was moisture - how the air holds water you cannot see.
4. In the morning the sky was clear. By midday there were clouds the colour of iron.
5. We taped down our posters. The temperature dropped, and the air smelled of rain.
6. Then the storm arrived. Hail rattled on the canvas like a drum, and everybody ran for the tent.
7. Lightning split the sky. Nora counted the seconds until the thunder, the way her project said to.
8. "Six seconds," she said. "The storm is two kilometres away." Nobody laughed at the weather project after that.
9. We waited it out together, twenty of us under one roof, dripping and delighted.
10. When it passed, the field was a lake, and every poster on the outside table was ruined.
11. Nora's poster survived, because she had brought a plastic sleeve. Preparation is not luck.
12. The judges gave her first place. "The weather did the demonstration," said Teacher Yasmin.

Themes: preparation is not luck; a demonstration the weather gave for free
