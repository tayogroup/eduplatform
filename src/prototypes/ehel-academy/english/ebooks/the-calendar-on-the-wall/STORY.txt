THE CALENDAR ON THE WALL
Grade 3 - Level 3
Amal series, book 3 (Term 1, Unit 3: Time and Daily Life)

1. The Calendar on the Wall. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Our class made a calendar and hung it on the wall. It had all twelve months, from January to December.
3. January, February, March. I wrote the first three months in my very best handwriting.
4. April, May, June. Nora drew rain on one month and sunshine on the next.
5. July, August, September. Idris drew a kite on every single page, because he likes kites.
6. October, November, December. Mina coloured the very last month completely purple.
7. Teacher Yasmin said, "A year has twelve months. A day has twenty-four hours. Use them well."
8. Every morning I get up at six. I eat at seven, and by eight o'clock I am at school.
9. Yesterday I finished my homework early. Today I am helping Mina with hers.
10. Tomorrow the vacation begins, and I will read all the books I have been saving up.
11. Grandma Hana laughed. "A century is a hundred years," she said, "and it still goes by."
12. On the last page I wrote a poem: "The months go fast, the hours too - so do the thing you mean to do."

Unit vocabulary reinforced: January to December, calendar, hour, century, vacation, future, soon, sooner.
Themes: a year is a thing you can hold; using the hours you have
Cameos: the poem on the last page is the one Amal writes in the Unit 3 reading.
