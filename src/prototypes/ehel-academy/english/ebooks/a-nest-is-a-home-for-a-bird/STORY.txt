A NEST IS A HOME FOR A BIRD
Grade 2 - Level 2
Grade 2 shelf - Poem series, book 5 (Term 3, Unit 8: Home, Sweet Home)

1. A Nest Is a Home for a Bird. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. A nest is a home for a bird.
3. A hive is a home for a bee.
4. A hole is a home for a rabbit.
5. And a house is a home for me.
6. The bird built her nest out of grass and twigs, all by herself.
7. The bees made their hive out of wax. Listen - you can hear them humming.
8. The rabbit dug her hole in the soft soil, deep and warm.
9. Some people live in a house. Some live high up in a block of flats.
10. "And which one is best?" asked Amal. "The one you come home to," said Mum.
11. That night the bird came back to her nest, exactly as Amal came back to hers.
12. A nest, a hive, a hole - and a house. Home, sweet home.
