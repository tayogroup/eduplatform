SOMEBODY, NOBODY, EVERYBODY
Grade 3 - Level 3
Kind little notes keep appearing in class, and nobody knows who is leaving them.

1. Somebody, Nobody, Everybody. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. On Monday there was something new on my desk: a small folded note.
3. It said: "Your reading made me smile." Nobody had signed it.
4. "Somebody left it," said Nora. "But who?"
5. On Tuesday Idris found one too: "Your kite drawing is wonderful."
6. By Thursday nearly everybody had found one. And nobody had seen anything.
7. We asked everybody. "Was it you?" "Not me." "Was it you?" "Not me."
8. "Anybody could have written them," I said. "The letters tell us nothing."
9. Then Maya noticed something: the notes came on days somebody arrived early.
10. So on Friday we came early ourselves, and waited quietly by the door.
11. It was Theo, with one last note in his hand.
12. "It was nothing," said Theo. "It was something," said everybody at once.
