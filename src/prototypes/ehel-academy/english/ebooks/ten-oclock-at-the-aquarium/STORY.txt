TEN O'CLOCK AT THE AQUARIUM
Grade 2 - Level 2
Zuri series, book 9b (Term 3, Unit 9: Let's Explore the City!)

1. Ten O'Clock at the Aquarium. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. The schedule on the wall said what would happen and when. Zuri read all of it twice.
3. Ten o'clock: the octopus. Eight arms, and every one of them is clever.
4. Eleven o'clock: the penguins. On land they are slow. In the water they are the fastest thing there.
5. Twelve o'clock: the turtle is fed. The turtle is old, and slow, and does not care.
6. One o'clock: the shark. It is huge. It is behind very thick glass. Zuri checked.
7. Kiki did not like the shark. So Zuri stood next to her until it swam away.
8. Two o'clock: a hundred small fish, all turning at exactly the same moment.
9. Three o'clock: the talk. "The sea is a home too," said Miss Twiga. "Do not drop anything into it."
10. Zuri copied the whole schedule into her book, so she could do it all again one day.
11. Then out into the city again - loud, bright and dry.
12. "Amazing," said Zuri. It was the best word she had, and it was not quite big enough.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: aquarium, schedule, o'clock, octopus, penguin, turtle, shark, fish, clever, fast, slow, huge, scary, beautiful.
Themes: a timetable is a promise you can read; standing next to a frightened friend
Cameos: the aquarium is the Unit 9 reading's own. Kiki is the one who is frightened - and it is not treated as funny.
