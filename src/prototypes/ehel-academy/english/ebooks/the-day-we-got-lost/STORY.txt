THE DAY WE GOT LOST
Grade 4 - Level 4
Amal series, book 9 (Term 3, Unit 9: Places, People, and Plans)

1. The Day We Got Lost. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Our class took the train to the capital. It was four hours, and I did not sleep for one of them.
3. The station was the biggest room I had ever stood in.
4. Teacher Yasmin gave each pair a map and a meeting time. "Do not be late. Do not go alone."
5. Nora and I found the museum, the mall, and a restaurant that sold nothing we recognised.
6. Then we took one wrong turn, and then another, and the street names stopped matching the map.
7. We were lost. Properly lost, in a city of two million people.
8. Nora did not panic. She found a signpost, then a street name, then the same name on our map.
9. We walked back the way the map said, not the way we felt like going. Those are rarely the same.
10. We reached the meeting point four minutes early. Teacher Yasmin never knew.
11. On the train home Nora fell asleep against the window and I kept the map.
12. I still have it. There is a small pencil cross where we worked out where we were.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: tourism, airport, station, railroad, capital, nation, museum, mall, restaurant, entrance, hallway, elevator, arrive, horizon.
Themes: going the way the map says rather than the way you feel like going
Cameos: the trip to the capital is the Unit 9 readings' own.
