KIKI MAKES MUSIC
Grade 1 - Level 1
Grade 1 shelf - Fable series, book 5 (Term 3, Unit 6: My Five Senses)

1. Kiki Makes Music. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Kiki found a drum under the big tree.
3. Boom, boom! The drum was very loud.
4. The little elephant covered her ears. "Too loud!"
5. So Kiki played quietly. Tap, tap, tap.
6. "That is better," said the elephant. "Now I can hear the birds."
7. The ostrich brought a violin. It made a thin, sweet sound.
8. Miss Twiga pressed the keys with her nose. Ding, ding!
9. Loud drum. Quiet violin. Louder keyboard!
10. They played together. Boom, tap, ding!
11. Musa came running. "I can hear you from the hill!"
12. "Then come and play," said Kiki. And everybody did.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: loud, quiet, louder, quieter, sweet, hear, ears, drum, violin, piano.
Themes: loud is not the same as good; playing so somebody else can still hear
Cameos: Kiki, the little elephant, the ostrich, Miss Twiga and Musa; the three instruments are the ones the Unit 6 Music Man song names.
