THE WALL BEHIND THE GARDEN
Grade 3 - Level 3
Amal series, book 5 (Term 2, Unit 5: Actions and Activities)

1. The Wall Behind the Garden. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. On Saturday our family had a busy day. Mum said we should work together before the sun got too hot.
3. First we cleaned the yard. Dad swept the path while I picked up the loose leaves.
4. Then we discussed the garden. Adam wanted maize. Mina wanted flowers. We decided to plant both.
5. But we could not plant anything yet, because the old wall behind the garden had fallen down.
6. So we built it again, stone by stone. Idris carried, Adam lifted, and I fitted the stones together.
7. Nora came to help. Omar brought us water, and Grandma Hana told us where the wall used to end.
8. By noon we had a heap of broken stones to remove. It was hot, and my arms ached.
9. "Nearly done," said Dad. "A wall protects a garden. A garden feeds a family."
10. In the afternoon we completed it. The wall stood straight, and the garden was safe.
11. Then we planted: maize along one side and flowers along the other, because that is what we agreed.
12. That evening we celebrated with sweet tea. It happened because we did it together.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: discuss, listen, offer, build, complete, remove, develop, protect, travel, escape, search, celebrate, happen.
Themes: work shared out; a result you can point at
Cameos: the wall behind the garden is the Unit 5 reading's own; Nora, Omar and Grandma Hana all help.
