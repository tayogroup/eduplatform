THE CHAMPIONS' LESSON
Grade 3 - Level 3
After the contest, the Grammar Champions get a new job: teaching prepositions to the junior class.

1. The Champions' Lesson. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. After the contest, Teacher Yasmin had one more idea.
3. "The junior class needs help with prepositions. Champions, will you teach them?"
4. We met in the library to get ready. Daniel drew three big cartoons.
5. Nora wrote IN, ON and UNDER at the top of a poster.
6. I practised speaking slowly and clearly, the way Teacher Yasmin does.
7. The next day, the junior class sat in rows and looked up at us.
8. "The book is ON the desk," I said, and Daniel put it there.
9. "Now the book is UNDER the desk!" The little ones laughed and said it with us.
10. A small girl put her pencil IN the cup, all by herself. Everybody cheered.
11. "Teaching is learning twice," said Teacher Yasmin from the door.
12. The Grammar Champions did not win a prize that day. We won something better.
