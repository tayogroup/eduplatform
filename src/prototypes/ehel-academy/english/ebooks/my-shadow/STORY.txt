MY SHADOW
Grade 2 - Level 2
Grade 2 shelf - Poem series, book 5 (Term 2, Unit 4: The Big Sky)

1. My Shadow, by Robert Louis Stevenson. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. I have a little shadow that goes in and out with me,
3. and what can be the use of him is more than I can see.
4. He is very, very like me from the heels up to the head,
5. Nora has one too. Two girls, two shadows, side by side.
6. At midday the sun is high, and the little shadow hides under her feet.
7. "Where did he go?" said Amal. "He is here. He is just very small."
8. In the evening the sun sinks low, and the shadow grows long again.
9. Longer than the tree. Longer than the wall. Longest of all at bedtime.
10. And I see him jump before me, when I jump into my bed.
11. Then the light goes out, and the little shadow goes to sleep too.
12. Good night, little shadow. See you in the morning.
