THE MEASURING DAY
Grade 4 - Level 4
Teacher Yasmin's class measures everything — doors, pencils, mangoes and a litre of water — and learns to trust its own numbers.

1. The Measuring Day. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. "Today we measure everything," said Teacher Yasmin. "Length, weight — and water too."
3. Amal's team measured the classroom door. Just over two metres tall!
4. Nora measured her pencil: sixteen centimetres, sharpened twice.
5. Which is heavier, a mango or a feather? The scale answered before anyone could argue.
6. "What weighs more — a kilogram of rice, or a kilogram of feathers?" Half the class fell for it.
7. A litre of water filled the bottle to the very top. Not one drop more would fit.
8. In the yard they paced the playground: forty-two steps for Adam, fifty for Amal. "Steps are not centimetres!" laughed Nora.
9. Back inside, Nora made a chart to compare every result.
10. One problem was left to solve: who is taller, Adam or Leo? The metre stick decided — by one centimetre.
11. "You completed every measurement," said Teacher Yasmin. "Now you can trust your numbers."
12. At home Amal measured everything — the table, the door, even the cat from next door.
