Reviewed 2026-09-11. Reviewer: Claude (automated measurement, an independent line-by-line content read, and judgement); no human teacher review yet. Scale: 1 Unsatisfactory; 2 Needs major improvement; 3 Acceptable with revisions; 4 Strong; 5 Excellent.

## Summary

The build reaches all 18 Cambridge Primary Global Perspectives 0838 Stage 4 objectives, every quiz key is single and explained, and the progress fix from the Grade 1 review is already live on these pages. The review found three things a learner or a school would feel. First, safeguarding: telling the teacher that a classmate cannot see the board was marked as doing nothing, and the rule was repeated in five more places. Second, the course assumed a northern, banana-importing town: bananas 'do not grow in our town' while apples came from a farm 'two miles up the road', radiators and heating, a sleep answer that added up to eleven hours in the eight-to-ten row. Third, about 45 other problems, among them the kit's opinion and talk defects, two different people called Omar, a supermarket leaflet credited to a 'building company', a tin can labelled 'a crisp packet', and question builders that refused 'How does a banana travel to reach us?'.

All of it is fixed in the build: 117 content edits across the eight lessons and six changes to the shared Global Perspectives kit, each gated and mutation-tested, verified on screen and in the browser with the real platform modules, both gates green on all four grades. It was committed as commit 9fec6d681 and Grades 1 to 4 were redeployed and pushed the same day, so the live pages carry every fix in this report.

Average score **4.2 / 5** across 27 areas; lowest 3 (Assessment Balance). Two High-priority findings, both fixed and live since commit 9fec6d681.

Change status at 2026-09-11: 14 items need no change or are done in the build, 5 have fixes built with a human or device check still open, 8 are open and not started. The validation fixes are live since commit 9fec6d681; the final pre-pilot review's fixes are in the working tree, not yet committed or deployed.

## Required changes, by priority

- **High.** Question quality (area 10) and safeguarding and local context (area 20). Both fixed in the build and verified on screen. Both are live since commit 9fec6d681.
- **Medium.** A teacher's read of the content and keys (areas 1, 4, 10, 19); a screen-reader walk-through (15); two lessons on the school's own devices (21); watching children use it (13, 27); a cross-subject decision on self-hosting the fonts (20).
- **Low.** A second application step for 4Ea.01 (3); a recap frame (9); a diagnostic opener (11); timing two children (17).

## What was fixed during this review

Every change below is in the working tree and verified: both gates green on Grades 1 to 4, every inline script parsing, the mutation harness passing on all four stages, and every lesson of all four grades driven to 100%. All but the last item are live since commit 9fec6d681; the last is in the working tree.

- **Kit.** Two middle slots per talk and a gate that holds cards to slots; opinion reasons that name the views they support; 'You are good at'; a real singular in the observe correction; capitals kept after 'I know that' and 'Now I think'.
- **Safeguarding.** Telling the teacher about Hana is a right answer; the rule and look-back say telling a grown-up counts.
- **Local context.** Local bananas and imported apples; kilometres; the equator; air conditioning; a sleep answer that adds up.
- **People and names.** Yusuf the classmate, Mr Omar the shopkeeper, Omar Salim; Dana from the supermarket company; 'Plant', not 'Sow'.
- **Keys and explanations.** Question builders widened in Lessons 1 and 7; genuinely off-topic reasons; the Venn items; the window seat; 'you decided it was not your problem'; the Lesson 8 talk card; nine repeated stems reworded.
- **Pictures and words.** Plastic cups, a tin can and a sweet wrapper; queue, supermarket, 'ask with', 'the fire alarm went off for a drill'; Grade for Year.
- **Decisions on the owner's instruction.** 'Reading a comic' replaces 'listening to music'; every 2020-era emoji replaced (look-back rewind, a seat for the window seat, wind for open windows, a no-entry sign, name labels); the four grades stay the same length.
- **Final pre-pilot review (2026-09-11, after commit 9fec6d681).** A fresh read found 0 High, 7 Medium, 15 Low; all Medium fixed ('half of the children we asked'; neutral question-builder feedback; the course look-back's kinds of activity; 'my' in look-backs), with the kit's spoken-template fixes. Details: 'global-perspectives-grades-1-4 pilot readiness review.docx'.
