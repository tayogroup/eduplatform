AMAL AND THE LITTLE HEN
Grade 1 - Level 1
Amal (Grade 1) series, book 5 (Term 2, Unit 5: On the Farm)

1. Amal and the Little Hen. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Amal loved the farm. "Come," said Ayeeyo. "There is much work to do today."
3. First they went to see the cow. "Good morning, cow!" The big cow said, "Moo!"
4. The sheep said, "Baa!" The goat wanted to eat Amal's hat. Amal laughed.
5. "Now we must feed the hens," said Ayeeyo. She gave Amal a bowl of seed.
6. The little hen looked hungry. "I will feed her," said Amal.
7. She threw the seed on the ground. "Cluck, cluck!" said the happy hen.
8. Then Amal saw something warm in the straw. It was a little white egg!
9. "Well done, Amal. When you are kind to the animals, they are kind to you."
10. In the field, Amal planted a tiny seed. "With water and sun, it will grow."
11. Adam drove past on the tractor. "Hard work, little sister!" he called.
12. That night Amal ate warm bread and drank fresh milk. "I love the farm."

Unit vocabulary reinforced: cow, hen, chick, sheep, goat, moo, baa, cluck, farm, barn, field, seed, egg, tractor, feeding, planting, growing, milk, bread.
Themes: kindness to animals comes back to you; everything on a farm grows
Cameos: Ayeeyo keeps the farm in the Unit 5 reading; Adam drives the tractor there too.
