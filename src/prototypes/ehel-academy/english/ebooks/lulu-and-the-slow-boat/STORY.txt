LULU AND THE SLOW BOAT
Grade 1 - Level 1
Grade 1 shelf - Fable series, book 5 (Term 3, Unit 7: Let's Go!)

1. Lulu and the Slow Boat. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Lulu the little bird can fly fast. Whoosh!
3. Kiki went to the lake in a boat. The boat was slow.
4. "Come with me," said Kiki. "We can go together."
5. "No," said Lulu. "I am fast. I will get there first."
6. Lulu flew high above the acacia trees.
7. Then the wind came. It was strong. Lulu had to stop and wait.
8. The little boat floated on. Slow, slow, slow.
9. When Lulu got to the other side, Kiki was already there.
10. "You are slow," said Lulu, "but you did not stop."
11. "Slow is fine," said Kiki. "Come and sit down."
12. So they went home together, slowly, and watched the sun go down.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: fast, slow, fly, float, boat, wind, stop, go, travel, together.
Themes: fast that has to stop against slow that does not
Cameos: Lulu from Lulu Says Let's Go, and Kiki in the boat from Lulu and the Wonderful Water.
