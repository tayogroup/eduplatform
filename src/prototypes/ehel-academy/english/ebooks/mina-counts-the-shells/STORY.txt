MINA COUNTS THE SHELLS
Grade 3 - Level 3
Mina says the beach must hold a million shells, so Amal shows her how to measure instead of count.

1. Mina Counts the Shells. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. On Saturday I took my little sister Mina to the beach.
3. "Look at all the shells!" Mina shouted. "There must be a million!"
4. "That is what I said," I told her. "Then we measured. I will show you how."
5. We marked one square on the sand, with string and four corner stones.
6. Mina counted the shells inside the square. There were fifty-two.
7. "Now we measure the beach," I said. "Walk, and count your steps."
8. Mina counted her steps all the way to the big rocks.
9. Then we did the multiplication together, there on the sand.
10. "Thousands and thousands!" said Mina. "But not quite a million."
11. "You did not count every shell," I said. "You measured. Measuring is faster."
12. Mina packed six shells to take home. "These ones," she said, "I counted."
