A WALK AROUND TOWN
Grade 1 - Level 1
Amal (Grade 1) series, book 9 (Term 3, Unit 9: City Places)

1. A Walk Around Town. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. First they came to the big road. The traffic lights were red. "Red means stop."
3. Then the light was green. "Green means go!" said Amal, and they walked across.
4. "Look, Mum! There is my school." Adam waved from the gate. "Hello, Amal!"
5. Next to the school was the market. It was very busy.
6. "Good morning," said Omar the shopkeeper. "Would you like a mango?"
7. "Yes, please. Thank you!" said Amal. "You are a polite girl," said Omar.
8. They walked past the hospital. "Faduma the doctor works here," said Mum.
9. Near the park, Leo dropped his paper. "Please put it in the bin," said Amal.
10. "Sorry," said Leo, and he put the paper in the bin. Now the park was clean.
11. Last of all came the library. It was quiet. Ayeeyo was there. "Come, my dear."
12. "Red means stop, green means go - and we keep our town clean. I love my town!"

Unit vocabulary reinforced: school, market, hospital, library, park, road, shop, bin, teacher, doctor, shopkeeper, neighbour, red, green, stop, go, clean, busy, quiet, polite.
Themes: a town is the people who help in it; everyone keeps it clean
Cameos: Omar the shopkeeper, Faduma the doctor and Leo are all named in the Unit 9 reading; Ayeeyo is waiting in the library.
