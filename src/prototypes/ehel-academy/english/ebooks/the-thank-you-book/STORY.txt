THE THANK-YOU BOOK
Grade 3 - Level 3
The class makes a secret book for Teacher Yasmin, with a title, a beginning and an ending.

1. The Thank-You Book. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. "Teacher Yasmin taught us all year," said Nora. "We should thank her."
3. "Let's make a book," I said. "A book needs a title, a beginning and an ending."
4. Leo chose the title, and wrote it out in his very best letters.
5. Sami wrote about the garden. Adam wrote a poem, four lines long.
6. Maya drew the Box of Ideas, right across the biggest page.
7. "Every tale needs a problem," said Leo. "Our problem was fitting it all in."
8. We put the pages in order: a beginning, a middle and an ending.
9. On the last page we wrote our message, with every name underneath.
10. We left it on her desk and waited, quiet as mice.
11. She read it slowly. At the ending, she smiled her biggest smile.
12. "You used your own words," she said. "That is the best thank-you there is."
