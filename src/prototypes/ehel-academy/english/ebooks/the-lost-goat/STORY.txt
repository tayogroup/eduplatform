5. "I must find her before dark," he said quietly to himself.

8. Then he proceeded slowly along the lane behind the bakery, listening carefully for any sound.
9. Suddenly he heard a soft sound, like a gentle bleat coming from behind some crates.
10. There she was, calmly chewing on a piece of straw.
11. Adam ran to rescue her. "I will always defend you," he said. "You gave me such a fright."
12. He carried her home along the quiet lanes, promising himself he would check the rope twice from now on.
