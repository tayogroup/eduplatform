2. Amal sat quietly at the kitchen table, staring at her spelling list.
3. Tomorrow was her big spelling test, and she felt nervous every time she looked at the long words.
4. Her thoughts were anxious ones. What if I forget the words? What if my mind goes blank in front of the whole class?
5. Her older brother Adam came in and saw her worried face.
6. "Don't be so hard on yourself," he said kindly, pulling out the chair beside her. "You have studied every day this week."
7. "I have felt nervous before a test too, and it was fine in the end."
8. "Really?" Amal asked, looking doubtful. "You always seem so calm." "Everybody feels nervous sometimes."
9. "The trick is not to let the worry win," said Adam. Amal was still doubtful, but she felt a little braver.
10. After dinner Mum brought her warm milk. "Be polite to your worry," she said. "Listen to it calmly, then tell it to go to sleep."
11. Amal laughed, and the tight feeling in her chest loosened a little. She read her list one more time.
12. The next morning she walked into the classroom with the words still fresh in her mind. When the test was over, she had remembered every single one.
