A HAT FOR THE SCARECROW
Grade 1 - Level 1
The wind takes the scarecrow's old hat, and Duku, Koko and Gigi bring it back.

1. A Hat for the Scarecrow. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. The scarecrow stood in the green garden.
3. One day the wind blew and blew.
4. The old hat flew up and away!
5. "Oh no!" said Duku. "Our hat!"
6. Duku ran after the hat. Run, run!
7. Gigi looked in the tall grass.
8. Koko found the hat by the fence!
9. They carried the hat back home.
10. Duku put the hat back on. There!
11. "Good as new!" said the happy friends.
12. The scarecrow kept the garden safe again.
