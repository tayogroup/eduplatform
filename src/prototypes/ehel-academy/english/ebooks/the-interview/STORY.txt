THE INTERVIEW
Grade 3 - Level 3
Amal series, book 3 of 4, from the listening text "Amal Talks About Her Family" (Term 1, Unit 1: All About Me and My Family)

1. The Interview. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Teacher Yasmin made an interview corner: one chair, one microphone, and one question at a time.
3. "Hello, Amal. Can you tell us a little about yourself?" My mouth went completely dry.
4. "My name is Amal, and I am a student at Ehel Academy." For a moment that was all I had.
5. "Who do you live with at home?" asked Maya. That question was easier.
6. My parents, my two brothers, my little sister, and my grandmother. All of us, in one house.
7. "Our house is small," I said, "but it is always full of noise and laughter."
8. Then she asked, "Who are your role models?" and I did not know what to answer.
9. So I said what my mother actually does. She is patient with everybody, even at the end of the day.
10. And what my father actually does. He listens carefully before he speaks, every time.
11. "What is your duty at home?" "To honour them," I said, "and to be a good student."
12. "Thank you for talking with us, Amal." "Thank you," I said, "for listening to my story."

Unit vocabulary reinforced: family, parent, student, role model, respect, honour, duty.
Themes: a question you cannot answer in the abstract; answering it by describing what people actually do
Cameos: Maya asks the questions; the answers are Amal's own from the Unit 1 interview.
