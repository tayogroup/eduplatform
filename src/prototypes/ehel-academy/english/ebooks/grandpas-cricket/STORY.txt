2. Nora was helping Grandpa water the tomato plants.
3. "Grandpa, what is that in your pocket?" asked Nora.
4. He opened his hand. A small brown cricket sat on his palm.
5. The cricket rubbed its wings together and made a soft, clicking chirp.
6. "Can I keep it as a pet? I love the chirping sound!" said Nora.
7. "You may - but you must take good care of it. First: crickets need to stay warm."
8. "Second: they eat small pieces of fruit and vegetables."
9. "Third: a shallow dish of water. And air holes in the lid, always."
10. Nora put the jar somewhere warm, away from the open window.
11. That evening the cricket chirped, and Nora knew it was happy.
12. "A pet is not a toy," said Grandpa. "It is somebody who needs you every day."
