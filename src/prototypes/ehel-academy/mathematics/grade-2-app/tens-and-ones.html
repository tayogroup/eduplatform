Tens and Ones

What this lesson is about

By the end of this lesson you will be able to: Count up to 100 objects; say how many you see without counting them; know what each digit means in a 2-digit number; round a 2-digit number to the nearest 10; use arrays to show multiplication; use sharing and grouping to work out a division.

Numbers to 100

For the grown-up: what to have to hand

About 100 small things that are all the same - dried beans, bottle tops or pasta - and a hundred square if you can print one. Making real piles of ten is what makes tens and ones mean something.
