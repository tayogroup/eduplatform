Grade 3 - Level 3
Amal series, book 2 of 4, from the story "Amal's Year of Words" (Term 3, Unit 10: My Year of Words (capstone))

1. The Green Folder. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. It was the last Monday of Year 3. Teacher Yasmin put one large green folder on every desk.
3. "Inside this folder is your whole year," she said. "Choose the work that shows how far you have come."
4. On the very first page I found my family tree from Unit 1, with Mina drawn in the corner.
5. Behind it lay my calendar chart. January blue, February green, March grey for the heavy rains.
6. Under the chart was my report about the county hospital, where Doctor Sarah answered every question.
7. Then my poster about climate and weather, with the temperature written down for a whole week.
8. Next came my page of big numbers, all the way up to a million.
9. "You spelt million with two letters l," said Nora. "I remember, because I spelt it wrongly first."
10. At the back was my kindness jar page, with one honest sentence written on it.
11. "In January you could hardly write one paragraph," said Grandma Hana. "Now you write a whole page."
12. On the last sheet I wrote my goal for Grade 4, and then I read it back twice.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: one page per unit: family, calendar, hospital, climate, temperature, million, kindness.
Themes: seeing a year from the outside, in one folder
Cameos: the family tree, the calendar chart, the hospital report, the climate poster, the million page and the kindness jar are the six pages Unit 10 names.
