Grade 1 Mathematics

Five lessons, one for each part of the maths you learn this year. Pick the one you want — you can come back and change your mind.

Number

Up to Twenty

Count to 20 and back, read and write the numbers, order and compare them, estimate, add and take away, doubles and halves, odd and even, and money.

29 steps · check · stickersStart Patterns

What Comes Next

Find the part that repeats, fill the missing shape and the missing number, count on in jumps, balance both sides, and make a pattern of your own.

14 steps · check · stickersStart Shape, measure and time

Shapes and Sizes

Name flat and solid shapes and sort them, compare length, weight and how much a jug holds, say where something is, and read the days, the months and the clock.

14 steps · check · stickersStart Statistics

Asking and Sorting

Ask everyone a question, write the answers in a list and a table, build a block graph and a pictogram, sort into hoops and a Carroll diagram, then say what the data shows.

11 steps · check · stickersStart Fractions

Halves and Wholes

Tell two equal parts from two unequal ones, colour one half of a shape, share a group of things fairly, halve a number, and put two halves back together to make one whole.
