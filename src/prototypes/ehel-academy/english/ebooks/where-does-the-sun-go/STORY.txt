WHERE DOES THE SUN GO?
Grade 2 - Level 2
Zuri series, book 4c (Term 2, Unit 4: The Big Sky)

1. Where Does the Sun Go? Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Zuri wakes up when the light reaches her burrow. That moment is called sunrise.
3. In the morning the sun is low. Everything has a long, long shadow.
4. At midday the sun is right at the top of the sky. Now the shadow is tiny.
5. In the evening the sun is low again - but this time on the other side.
6. Then sunset. The sky turns orange and pink, and the birds all go home.
7. "Where does the sun go?" Zuri asked. "Nowhere," said Miss Twiga. "That is the surprise."
8. "The sun stays still. We turn. The Earth is turning under your feet right now."
9. At night the moon takes the sun's place, and the stars come out behind it.
10. "So somewhere far away," said Zuri, "somebody is watching the sun come up right now."
11. Zuri went to sleep. The Earth kept turning. It does not need anybody to watch it.
12. And in the morning the sun was back. Day, night, day, night, all the way round.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: sunrise, morning, midday, evening, sunset, night, moon, star, shadow, long, short, high, low, turn.
Themes: the answer is not where the sun goes but what WE do; day and night as one motion
Cameos: Miss Twiga's ball-as-the-Earth explanation is the Unit 4 listening's own.
