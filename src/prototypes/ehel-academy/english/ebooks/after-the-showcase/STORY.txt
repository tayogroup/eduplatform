AFTER THE SHOWCASE
Grade 3 - Level 3
The showcase is over, the garden is quiet, and Amal carries her year home in a green folder.

1. After the Showcase. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. The showcase was over, and the garden was quiet at last.
3. We took the bunting down and folded it, corner to corner.
4. We carried the chairs inside, counting as we went.
5. Teacher Yasmin gave each of us our folder. "Take your year home."
6. At home, Grandma Hana read my booklet, page by page.
7. Mina pointed at the million page. "One day I will count that far."
8. "You will," I said. "And I will show you where to start."
9. Dad hung my kindness page on the wall, right by the door.
10. Mum read my goal for Grade 4 out loud: a long chapter book, all by myself.
11. "Then you will need this," said Grandma Hana, and reached up to the shelf.
12. Year 3 is finished. The reading is not. I opened the first page.
