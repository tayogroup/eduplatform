VISITORS' DAY AT SCHOOL
Grade 4 - Level 4
An engineer, a doctor, a police officer, a mechanic and the mayor visit the class, and every guest has a job to tell about.

1. Visitors' Day at School. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Teacher Yasmin pinned up a notice: Visitors' Day on Friday. Every guest has a job to tell us about.
3. The classroom became a little hall, with chairs in rows for the audience.
4. Elena the engineer showed her yellow helmet. "I make things stand up safely," she said.
5. Doctor Sarah came next. "My job is keeping every member of this town well."
6. Officer Rami showed how he stops a whole road with one raised hand.
7. Karim held up his tools. "When something breaks, a mechanic asks why."
8. Then the mayor arrived — the most surprising guest of all.
9. "A town is people," he told the audience. "Every job you heard today serves someone."
10. Sami asked every visitor the same question: "Is your job hard?" Every visitor said yes — and smiled.
11. Afterwards Amal drew every guest for the class wall, like an artist at work.
12. "Which job will you choose?" asked Teacher Yasmin. The whole class answered at once.
