SAMI’S SECOND SHOW
Grade 4 - Level 4
Sami said his lines once and survived — now Teacher Yasmin asks him to do it all again, for the little ones.

1. Sami’s Second Show. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. After the play, Sami could not stop smiling for a whole week.
3. Then Teacher Yasmin asked, "Will you perform it again, for the little ones?"
4. The old worry came back at once. Sami sat very still at his desk.
5. "You have done it once," said Amal. "Your voice knows the way now."
6. They practised once in the yard, just to wake the lines up.
7. The little ones filled the front row and would not sit still.
8. Sami stepped out. This time his voice was steady from the first word.
9. When the queen lost her crown, the little ones gasped out loud.
10. When it was found again, they clapped until the curtains shook.
11. "Being brave twice is easier than once," said Sami. "But only a little."
12. Teacher Yasmin pinned up a new sign: THE SAME PLAY, EVEN BETTER.
