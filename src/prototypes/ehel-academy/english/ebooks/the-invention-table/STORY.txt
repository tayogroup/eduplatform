THE INVENTION TABLE
Grade 4 - Level 4
A rain roof for the hens, a bell for the book cart, a rack for the letters — Invention Day comes to school.

1. The Invention Table. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. "Invention Day is on Friday," said Teacher Yasmin. "Bring an idea that helps somebody."
3. All week, Amal paid attention to every squeak and drip at home.
4. Idris built a little rain roof for the hens — protection from the storms.
5. Nora invented a bookmark that could never fall out of its book.
6. Sami fixed a bell to the library cart, so you hear the books coming.
7. Amal made a letter rack for Omar's counter, with a slot for every street.
8. Each child wrote a description: what it is, what it does, who it helps.
9. On Friday, the invention table was the most popular place in the whole school.
10. Omar read Amal's description twice, then carried the rack straight to his counter.
11. "One invention will go to the national fair," said Teacher Yasmin.
12. It was the rain roof — and the hens, said Amal, deserved a medal too.
