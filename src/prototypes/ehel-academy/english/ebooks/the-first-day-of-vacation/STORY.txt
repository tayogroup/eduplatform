THE FIRST DAY OF VACATION
Grade 3 - Level 3
No school bell, no line at the gate - but Amal's eyes still open at six o'clock, right on time.

1. The First Day of Vacation. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. The vacation began today. No school bell. No line at the gate.
3. But at six o'clock my eyes still opened, right on time.
4. "A routine is hard to switch off," laughed Mum.
5. At seven we ate breakfast slowly. Nobody had to hurry.
6. At eight I opened the first of my saved-up books.
7. I read in the garden all morning, with the hens for company.
8. At one o'clock Sami came round. We ate lunch under the big tree.
9. In the afternoon we drew a calendar of the vacation, day by day.
10. Swimming on Monday. The market on Friday. Books in between.
11. "Even a vacation fits better in order," I told Grandma Hana. She laughed.
12. Tomorrow I will wake at six again. But I will not get up until seven.
