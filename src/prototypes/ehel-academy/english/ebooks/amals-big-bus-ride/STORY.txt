AMAL'S BIG BUS RIDE
Grade 1 - Level 1
Amal (Grade 1) series, book 7 (Term 3, Unit 7: Let's Go!)

1. Amal's Big Bus Ride. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. "How will we get there?" asked Amal. "First we walk. Then we go by bus."
3. Amal and Mum walked down the road. They walked past the market.
4. They walked to the bus stop and waited.
5. Soon a big bus came. It was yellow and red. "Beep, beep!" went the bus.
6. "Find a seat. Then sit down," said Mum. Amal found a seat by the window.
7. The wheels on the bus went round and round.
8. "Look, Mum! A boy on a bicycle!" The boy waved. His name was Adam.
9. "And look - a red car!" The car went fast. The bus went slow and bumpy.
10. Amal saw a girl named Samira, going to school. Samira waved too.
11. The bus drove past the shops. It drove past the library. It drove to the sea.
12. "Amal!" called Grandmother, by the little blue boat. "You came to see me!"

Unit vocabulary reinforced: walk, bus, car, bicycle, boat, wheels, seat, drive, ride, float, road, town, sea, fast, slow, sit down.
Themes: a journey told by what goes past the window
Cameos: Adam on his bicycle and Samira on her way to school both wave from the Unit 7 reading.
