1

Facing and turning

The four cardinal points go round in order: north, east, south, west. A quarter turn clockwise takes you to the next one.

2

The eight points

Between each pair sits an ordinal point, and its name is simply both names joined: north and east make north-east, written NE.

3

Following a route

A route is moves one after another. Do the first, then the second, then tap the square you land on.

4

Along first, then up

The first number is along, the second is up. Swap them and you are somewhere else entirely. Tap the point the label names.

5

The corners of a shape

A shape sits on the grid and every corner has its own pair. Read the circled one: along first, then up.

6

When the mirror is the edge

Here the mirror line runs along the edge of the shape. Nothing has any distance to cross, so the reflection touches the original and the whole picture doubles.
