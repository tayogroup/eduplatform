THE SPIRAL CAVE
Grade 4 - Level 4
Amal series, book 5 (Term 2, Unit 5: Action and Movement)

1. The Spiral Cave. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. It was race day at the village field, and my brother Adam was the fastest runner in town.
3. I am not fast. But I can keep going, which is a different thing.
4. Halfway through the race, Idris shouted. The goat had escaped again.
5. Adam stopped running. He was winning, and he stopped.
6. We followed the goat up the hill, past the mountain trail, until it disappeared into a hole in the rock.
7. Inside was a cave we had never seen: cool, dark, and coiled like a shell.
8. Adam went first, and made us walk slowly. "Never rush in a place you cannot see," he said.
9. The goat was at the centre of the spiral, perfectly calm, chewing something it should not have.
10. Idris carried it out. It weighed more than he did, and he refused to admit that.
11. We got back to the field long after the race had finished. Nobody had won it.
12. "You lost the race," I told Adam. "I rescued a goat," he said. "Ask me which one I will remember."

Unit vocabulary reinforced: gallop, accelerate, proceed, spiral, rate, peek, gaze, signal, squeeze, pressure, rescue, defend, prevent, admit.
Themes: stopping when you are winning; what you actually remember afterwards
Cameos: the village race, the lost goat and the spiral cave are the Unit 5 readings' own.
