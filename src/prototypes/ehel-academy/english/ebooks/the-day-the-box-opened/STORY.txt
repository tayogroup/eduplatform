THE DAY THE BOX OPENED
Grade 3 - Level 3
On the last morning of term, Teacher Yasmin opens the Box of Ideas - and one idea gets built.

1. The Day the Box Opened. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. The Box of Ideas sat on the desk all term, filling up slowly.
3. On the last morning, Teacher Yasmin lifted the lid. "Time to open it."
4. She read the ideas out, one by one, in her gentle voice.
5. Sami's flying idea made everybody look towards the sea.
6. Nora's dream came out too: a library with no walls at all.
7. "Could we build it?" asked Leo. "Not with bricks. With a rug and a shelf, under the tree."
8. So we carried the books out into the playground.
9. We read under the tree until the bell went.
10. Amal put the empty box back on the desk. "It is not empty," said Teacher Yasmin.
11. "It is waiting for next year's ideas."
12. An idea in a box is a seed. An idea out loud is a garden.
