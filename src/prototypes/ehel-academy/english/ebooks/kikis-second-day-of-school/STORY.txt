KIKI'S SECOND DAY OF SCHOOL
Grade 1 - Level 1
Kiki goes back to school and helps a shy new pupil find a friend.

1. Kiki's Second Day of School. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. It was Kiki's second day of school.
3. Musa waved. "Good luck again, Kiki!"
4. A new pupil stood by the school.
5. The little goat felt very shy.
6. "Hello!" said Kiki. "Come with me."
7. They sat on the bench together.
8. Miss Twiga taught them a new song.
9. They counted one, two, three, four!
10. At playtime they shared a sweet mango.
11. Ring, ring! The bell rang. Home time!
12. "School is fun with friends," said Kiki.
