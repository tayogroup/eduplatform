# Archived: the earlier Intensive English Level 1 (A1 + A2)

These twenty authored units were Level 1 from 2026-08-01 to 2026-09-11: an
adult course, A1 in Units 0-9 and A2 in Units 10-19, compressed from the school
English Grades 1-4 and aligned to Cambridge 0058.

They were replaced on 2026-09-11 when the owner re-levelled the course on
Cambridge Primary English as a Second Language 0057: Level 1 is now Stages 1-3,
Pre-A1 to A1, age-neutral, and Level 2 will be Stages 4-6, A1 to A2.

They are kept, not deleted, for two reasons:

- **Their A2 half (Units 10-19) is material the rebuilt Level 2 can draw on**,
  since the new Level 2 ends at A2.
- **They were reviewed and signed off** (script review and curriculum sign-off,
  2026-08-01), so they are the record of what learners were taught until the
  new course ships, and a known-good reference for field shapes.

The builder does not read this folder. `tools/build-intensive-units.js` reads
`../authored/` only.
