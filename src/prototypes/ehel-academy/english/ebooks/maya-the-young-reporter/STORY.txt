1. Maya the Young Reporter. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Maya is nine years old, and she wants to be a scientist one day.
3. Right now her biggest challenge is the class newspaper she writes every week for Teacher Yasmin's class.
4. Every Monday she opens her notebook, taps her pencil, and thinks of a good headline before she starts.
5. She must find real information about her town and share it clearly, so that nobody is left confused.
6. Last week she learned that the population of her neighbourhood was growing, because new families had come to open shops near the market.
7. "That is important news," said Teacher Yasmin. "Write it clearly, and do not judge the new families."
8. "Welcome them," she said, "and ask them what they hope to discover here."
9. Maya wrote her report neatly, choosing each word with care.
10. She erased every mistake before she stuck the pages onto the notice board, because a good reporter checks her own work twice.
11. Sami leaned over her desk. "A newspaper written by a nine-year-old?" he laughed.
12. Then he sat down and read it, word by word. "This is high-quality work," he said. "Can I help you next week? I have an idea too."
