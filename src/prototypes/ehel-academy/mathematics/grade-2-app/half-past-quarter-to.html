Half Past, Quarter To

1

The two hands

The short hand shows the hour. The long hand shows the minutes. Move them and watch.
short = hours long = minutes

2

Quarter past, quarter to

A quarter of the way round is quarter past. Three quarters round is quarter to the NEXT hour.

3

Counting round in fives

Count round in fives. On the right it is past. On the left it is to.

4

To the very minute

Every little mark is 1 minute. Find the nearest number, then count on.

5

Clock face and digital

Match the clock face to the digital time.

6

am, pm and the 24-hour clock

Before noon is am. After noon is pm. A 24-hour clock keeps counting: 1 pm is 13:00.

7

Seconds to years

Learn the ones that never change, then answer.

8

Days, months, calendar

Seven days, always the same order. Twelve months in a year. A calendar puts a month in rows of seven.

9

A time, or how long?

A time says when. An interval says how long. Same clock, different question.

10

Under a second

A second splits as well. 0.1 s is a tenth, 0.01 s is a hundredth. Races are won in these.
0.00

11

Time in two places

Noon does not happen everywhere at once. Each city keeps its own clock.

12

Hours as decimals

The decimal is a share of the hour, not the minutes. Half an hour is 0.5 h, not 0.30 h.
