1. Sami's First Story. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Sami had promised Maya an idea for a story, and now it was Monday.
3. "What is your story about?" Maya asked, opening her notebook. Sami went quiet.
4. "I do not know yet," he admitted. "Teasing you was easier than writing."
5. "Then start where I start," said Maya. "Find real information, and share it clearly."
6. They walked to the market together, and Sami counted the stalls: nineteen, and four of them new.
7. He asked Karim the carpenter how long he had mended stalls in the square. "Eleven years," said Karim. Sami wrote it down.
8. "That is a fact," said Maya. "A fact is where a story begins."
9. Sami wrote his first draft that evening. It had a good idea in it and eleven spelling mistakes.
10. He erased every one of them, because a good reporter checks his own work twice.
11. "Nineteen stalls, and four of them new," read his headline. Teacher Yasmin read it twice and nodded.
12. They stuck it on the notice board side by side. "Next week," said Sami, "I will find the story myself."

Unit vocabulary reinforced: information, quality, discover, challenge, erase, tease, population.
Themes: a fact is where a story begins; keeping a promise you made as a joke
Cameos: Karim the carpenter answers the question; the promise itself is Sami's last line in the Unit 4 reading.
