THE SHINY BEETLE
Grade 3 - Level 3
Noah knows where the small creatures live — if you walk gently.

1. The Shiny Beetle. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. My cousin Noah knows where all the small creatures live.
3. "Walk gently," he said. "Noisy feet send them under cover."
4. We searched the field first, in the warm grass.
5. A shiny beetle marched past my shoe, heading north.
6. We followed it carefully, past the fence and down the hill.
7. At the lake, we counted the little water birds.
8. Mina lifted a big leaf, gently, and found a sleeping snail.
9. "Do not collect them," said Noah. "Their home is here."
10. So we drew them in our notebooks instead.
11. The beetle turned east, into the forest shade, and was gone.
12. We walked home west, into the low sun, feeling lucky.
