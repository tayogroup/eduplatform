THE SIX-LEG CLUB
Grade 2 - Level 2
Zuri series, book 6 (Term 2, Unit 6: All About Bugs)

1. The Six-Leg Club. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. In the garden, Zuri found a tiny world. "Come and look!" she called.
3. A butterfly with big orange wings sat on a leaf.
4. "Six legs, three body parts and two antennae," said Kiki. "That is an insect."
5. An ant crawled under the stone. Then another. Then a hundred more!
6. They marched in a line to the anthill, collecting seeds as they went.
7. A bee flew above the flowers. Buzz, buzz. She was collecting sweet nectar.
8. A cricket chirped between two blades of grass. Chirp! Chirp!
9. A spider was spinning a web in front of the gate. Round and round and round.
10. "A spider has eight legs," said Miss Twiga. "So a spider is not an insect."
11. Under a wet log they found a worm. No legs at all!
12. Zuri drew them all in her book. "The Six-Leg Club," she wrote. "And friends."

Unit vocabulary reinforced: butterfly, cricket, ant, bee, spider, worm, insect, legs, wings, antennae, anthill, web, on, under, in, between, above, in front of, fly, jump, crawl, spin, chirp, collect.
Themes: looking closely; a rule that decides what counts as an insect
Cameos: Kiki names the rule; Miss Twiga rules the spider out of the club.
