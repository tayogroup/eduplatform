7. In the kitchen there was a sink and a table. In the dining room, four chairs.
8. In the living room there was a soft sofa and a warm red rug.
9. In the bedroom there was a bed by the window. In the bathroom, water and soap.
10. Kiki made her bed and tidied her room. Zuri swept the floor and set the table.
11. Miss Twiga showed them homes far away: an adobe house, a stilt house, a cave house and a tall skyscraper.
