2. "Adam, why does the sun disappear every night? Does it go to sleep?"
3. "The sun never sleeps," said Adam. "Let me show you something."
4. He fetched a torch. "This torch is the sun," he said, and switched it on.
5. "And this ball is our planet, Earth. Watch this little mark I have drawn on it."
6. He held the torch still and turned the ball slowly in front of it.
7. "When the mark faces the torch, that part of Earth has light. That is day."
8. Adam turned the ball further. Slowly the mark turned away from the light.
9. "Now the mark faces away. No light. That is night!" said Amal.
10. "It takes one whole day and one whole night for Earth to turn all the way round."
11. That night Amal looked up. Somewhere, it was already morning.
12. And in the morning the sun rose again, exactly as Adam had said it would.
