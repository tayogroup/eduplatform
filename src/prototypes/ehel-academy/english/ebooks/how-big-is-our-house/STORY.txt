HOW BIG IS OUR HOUSE?
Grade 3 - Level 3
Mina wants to know how big their house is, so she and Amal measure it - gate, wall, room and all.

1. How Big Is Our House? Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. "How big is our house?" asked Mina one morning. "Let us measure it and see," I said.
3. We started at the gate. From the gate to the door was twenty steps.
4. The garden wall was six metres long. Mina held one end of the string.
5. Inside, we measured the big room, wall to wall: four metres.
6. On the table, the bottle was taller than the bowl. We lined them up.
7. We weighed the basket on the scales, mangoes and all.
8. Mina measured me against the door frame and made a small mark.
9. Then I measured Mina. She had grown two centimetres since her birthday.
10. Mum baked bread while we worked, and the whole house smelled warm.
11. We packed our numbers into my notebook, room by room.
12. "Now we know how big our house is," said Mina. "Big enough," said Mum.
