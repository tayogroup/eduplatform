SIMBA'S NEW HOME
Grade 4 - Level 4
Simba the rescued dog grows strong at Talia's, learns to run and fetch on the village field, and finds where he belongs.

1. Simba's New Home. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Simba lived with Talia now. He was still thin, and still a little shy.
3. He ate slowly and slept near the door, as if he might have to run.
4. Talia fed him well and talked to him softly every day.
5. Amal, Nora and Leo visited after school. Simba's tail wagged like a flag.
6. "He needs to run," said Talia. "A strong dog is a moving dog."
7. They took him to the village field. Simba trotted, then ran!
8. He raced Adam along the fence and very nearly won.
9. Leo threw the ball, and Simba brought it back proudly every time.
10. Gradually his ribs disappeared, and his coat began to shine.
11. "You rescued him," said Talia. Amal shook her head. "He was brave. We only helped."
12. At sunset, Simba sat by Talia's door. Home at last.
