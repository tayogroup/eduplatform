Ehel Academy · Intensive English

Starter — First Steps

By the end: Can greet people and give personal details, count and deal with prices, name everyday things, people, places, food, clothes and work, say what they need and where it hurts, and ask the way, in short phrases.

Pre-A110 lessonsweeks 1–7 of the programme3 days per lessonabout 68 hours

Part 1 · Hello

1HelloYou will be able to greet people, say and spell your name, and ask for someone’s name. 2NumbersYou will be able to count to 20, and say your age, a phone number and a price. 3People and familyYou will be able to say who people are, and talk about your family.
Then: Part 1 review & check (1 day)

Part 2 · Things and places

4Things around meYou will be able to name everyday things, their colours and sizes, and ask for what you need. 5At homeYou will be able to say where you live, what is in your home and where things are. 6Food and drinkYou will be able to say what you like and want, order in a café and ask the price. 7Clothes and weatherYou will be able to say what you are wearing and what the weather is like.
Then: Part 2 review & check (1 day)

Part 3 · Out in the world

8My body, my healthYou will be able to say how you feel and where it hurts, and ask for help. 9In townYou will be able to ask where a place is and understand simple directions. 10Work and my dayYou will be able to say what you do, when you work, and what you do every day.
Then: Starter review & check (2 days)
