Mathematics · Grade 4

Shape, Space and Place

1

The faces of a solid

Every flat face of a solid is a 2D shape you already know. Pick a solid and take it apart.

2

Fold it up: nets

A net is a solid opened out flat. Fold it up in your head. What does it make?

3

Every line of symmetry

Test each line. If folding along it makes the halves match exactly, it is a line of symmetry. Find all of them.

4

Reflect it in the mirror

Each square has a partner the same distance on the other side of the mirror line. Tap the squares to build the reflection.

5

Putting shapes together

Join shapes and you make new ones. Some shapes tessellate — they tile a floor with no gaps at all.

6

Area without counting

Count the squares once. Then find the rule that gives the same answer every time without counting.

7

An odd shape on a grid

An odd shape has no formula. Count the whole squares, then treat each part square as about a half.

8

Reading between the marks

When the pointer sits between two marks, the fraction of the way across tells you the reading.

9

Which way from here?

Four cardinal points, and four ordinal ones in between. Follow the instruction and see where you land.
