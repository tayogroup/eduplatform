Ehel Academy · Intensive English

Level 3 — Independent English

By the end: Can follow the main points of clear speech on familiar matters, deal with most situations at work or while travelling, tell a story, report what others said, and write connected text giving reasons and explanations.

B112 lessonsweeks 29–40 of the programme4 days per lessonabout 108 hours

Part 1 · My story

1My life storyYou will be able to tell the story of your life, and talk about past habits and turning points. 2Experience and changeYou will be able to connect the past to now, and describe how things have changed. 3Describing preciselyYou will be able to describe people, places and things in detail. 4Telling a story wellYou will be able to tell a story with the background, the events in order and how people reacted.
Then: Part 1 review & check (2 days)

Part 2 · Getting things done at B1

5What if?You will be able to talk about plans that depend on something, and give advice. 6How it is doneYou will be able to describe processes and services, and understand official notices. 7Passing on messagesYou will be able to report what people said, asked and wanted. 8Work and careersYou will be able to talk about your experience and skills, and handle a job interview.
Then: Part 2 review & check (2 days)

Part 3 · Views and problems

9Opinions and disagreementYou will be able to agree and disagree politely, and give your reasons. 10Getting alongYou will be able to use everyday phrasal verbs and verb patterns in conversation and email. 11Guessing and regretYou will be able to talk about imaginary situations, guess what happened and talk about regrets. 12Money, services and complaintsYou will be able to complain and get a result, then plan your next steps in English.
Then: B1 review & check (2 days)
