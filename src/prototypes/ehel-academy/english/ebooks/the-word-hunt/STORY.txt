THE WORD HUNT
Grade 2 - Level 2
Zuri series, book 1b (Term 1, Unit 1: Welcome and Calendar)

1. The Word Hunt. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. "Here is your hunt," said Miss Twiga. "Find ten English words. They can be anywhere."
3. The first word was on the chalkboard. Zuri read it out loud. B-O-O-K. Book!
4. The second word was on a book. The third word was right beside it, on the same page.
5. The fourth word was on the tablet. Zuri touched it, and the tablet said it back to her.
6. The fifth, sixth and seventh words were on the colour chart. Red. Blue. Green.
7. "Yellow and pink!" said Kiki. That made nine. One more word to find.
8. So Zuri counted the books on the shelf. One, two, three - twelve books, and twelve words.
9. Outside there were words too. A word on the side of the bus. Zuri did not know that one.
10. A word on the market sign. "Fruit," read Zuri. "I know that word. I like fruit."
11. That night, at her burrow, Zuri wrote every word into her book. Twelve of them.
12. "Twelve words," said Zuri. "Tomorrow I will find twelve more." "Then I am coming too," said Kiki.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: book, tablet, chart, picture, word, red, blue, green, yellow, pink, one to twelve, spell.
Themes: English is not only in the lesson; noticing is a skill you can practise
Cameos: Miss Twiga sets the hunt; Kiki finds the last two; the shopkeeper hen at her stall.
