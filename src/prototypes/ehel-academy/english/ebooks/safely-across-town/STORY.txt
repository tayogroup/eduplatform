SAFELY ACROSS TOWN
Grade 4 - Level 4
Nora must carry a message across three big roads to the clinic, and Officer Rami shows her how a nervous walker becomes a confident one.

1. Safely Across Town. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Nora had to carry a message across town to the clinic. She felt nervous.
3. "Three big roads," she whispered. "And I always muddle the way."
4. Officer Rami stood at the first crossing. "Safety is a habit," he said. "Learn it once, use it daily."
5. "Stop at the kerb. Look both ways. Listen. Then walk — never run."
6. Nora crossed the first road safely. Her heart still thumped.
7. At the second road she waited calmly for the bus to pass.
8. A lorry rumbled past. Nora stood well back, patient and careful.
9. By the third road she was not nervous any more. She was careful — and confident.
10. At the clinic, Doctor Sarah took the message. "You came the safe way. Sensible girl!"
11. On the way home the roads felt smaller. Being careful had made her brave.
12. "Safely there and safely back!" called Officer Rami. Nora waved, hopeful and proud.
