What this is, and what it is not These two modules are foundation work. They are not units of the Kenyan Carpentry & Joinery Level 4 qualification — all eight of those units assume the learner already has tools and joints. Every criterion here names the Level 4 units it feeds instead of claiming to deliver them. Each step also states whether the app can assess it or whether an assessor signs it off at the bench: 29% is assessed here, 45% here and at the bench, and 26% at the bench only.
Not yet trade-reviewed The performance criteria and the teaching were written from published curricula and have not been read by a practising carpenter or a TVET assessor. That review comes before any of this is put in front of a learner.

Built on the Ehel Academy lesson platform. Content follows the Sokoine University of Agriculture workshop manual for carpentry and joinery; the Level 4 unit codes cited are from the Kenya TVET CDACC curriculum, referenced only to show where this work leads. UK / East African terminology throughout. Every drawing is vector, drawn in code — nothing here is a photograph.
