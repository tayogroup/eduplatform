A SHELF FOR THE TOWN
Grade 4 - Level 4
After Maya's article, the town meeting agrees to build a bookshelf in the hall — one book from every family, and a headline to finish.

1. A Shelf for the Town. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. After Maya's article, the whole town kept talking about books.
3. At the next meeting the mayor stood up. "A cart is a start," he said. "Our hall has room for a shelf."
4. Hands went up all over the hall. It was an agreement: everyone would bring one book.
5. Omar brought a book of letters. Teacher Yasmin brought three dictionaries.
6. The librarian sorted them by colour first, then by author, laughing at herself.
7. Karim mended an old shelf until it stood proud and straight.
8. Maya wrote it all down for the paper: "A town that reads together."
9. On opening day, bunting hung across the hall and every chair was taken.
10. Amal read the first story aloud to the little ones at the front.
11. The cart still comes on Thursdays. Now the shelf and the cart share the work.
12. Maya's next headline said: one shelf, one town, many readers.
