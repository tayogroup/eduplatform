1. The Night Amal Counted the Stars. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Amal woke early. The morning sky was pale and pink, and the sun was low.
3. She ran out to feed the goats. On the sandy ground she saw a long, thin shape.
4. "Look, Adam! My shadow is so long this morning!"
5. By midday the sun was high. Her shadow was short. It hid right under her feet!
6. "The sun did not move by itself," said Adam. "Our Earth is turning, round and round."
7. A cloud drifted over the sun. Her shadow disappeared. "No light, no shadow!"
8. In the evening the sky turned orange. "Come and sit with me," said Grandma Hana.
9. "Grandma," she whispered, "there are so many stars!"
10. "One, two, three, four..." But every time she looked, she found more.
11. "The moon has no light of its own," said Grandma Hana. "The sun shines on it."
12. "The sky is full of wonderful things," said Amal. "I never really looked up before."
