NINE ROOMS
Grade 4 - Level 4
Amal series, book 10 (Term 3, Unit 10: My English Voice (capstone))

1. Nine Rooms. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. It was the last week of Year 4, and Teacher Yasmin gave us the Exhibition.
3. "Nine units," she said. "Think of them as nine rooms. Show me what is in each one."
4. In the first room: Omar at the post counter, and a letter that made somebody feel remembered.
5. In the second: a storm, a tent, and a girl who brought a plastic sleeve.
6. In the third: a field, a market, a kitchen, and everything in between.
7. In the fourth: a library that arrives on Thursdays with one squeaky wheel.
8. In the fifth: a race nobody won, and a goat worth more than winning. In the sixth: a parade, and a page of names.
9. In the seventh: a curtain opening, and Sami saying every line.
10. In the eighth: a telescope, an attic, and a great-uncle who learned the stars from a book.
11. In the ninth: a map with a pencil cross on it.
12. On Exhibition evening I walked people through all nine rooms. It took a while. I did not hurry once.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: one room for each of Units 1-9: mail, moisture, rice, service, spiral, carpenter, terrified, equipment, station.
Themes: walking somebody else through a year you have had
Cameos: the Year 4 Exhibition and the poem "Nine Rooms" are both from the Unit 10 readings.
