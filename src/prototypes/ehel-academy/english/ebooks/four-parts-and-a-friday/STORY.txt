FOUR PARTS AND A FRIDAY
Grade 4 - Level 4
Amal (Grade 4) series, book 3 (Term 3, Unit 10: My English Voice (capstone))

1. Four Parts and a Friday. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. The Year 4 Exhibition closes your English year. It has four parts, and you complete them in order.
3. Part 1: choose your six pages. Take them from at least five different units.
4. Your set must show reading, writing, speaking and new words.
5. Part 2: build your display board. Write two paragraphs on every page.
6. Add one picture and one clear label to each page, and print your name and your class on the front.
7. Part 3: prepare your talk. It lasts two minutes.
8. Open with your name, present three pages, then answer two questions from the audience.
9. Part 4: write your reflection. Name one thing that was genuinely hard, one thing that improved, and one goal for Grade 5.
10. Pages chosen by Tuesday. Board finished by Thursday. Talk rehearsed with a partner by Thursday evening.
11. The Exhibition opens on Friday at five o'clock in the school hall. Sixteen marks for the board, sixteen for the talk, sixteen for the draft, sixteen for the reflection.
12. One rule matters more than the rest: every page must be your own work, written in your own English voice.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: the brief's own words: pages, board, paragraph, label, talk, reflection, draft.
Themes: a big task read as four small ones with dates on them
Cameos: every deadline and mark on these pages is the Unit 10 project brief's own.
