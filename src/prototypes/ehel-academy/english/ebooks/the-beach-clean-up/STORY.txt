THE BEACH CLEAN-UP
Grade 3 - Level 3
Three bins, one morning, and a beach left clean behind them.

1. The Beach Clean-Up. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. On Saturday our class returned to the beach with three big bins.
3. The tide had left rubbish all along the sand.
4. "Sort as you go," said Teacher Yasmin. "Metal, glass and plastic."
5. Leo found a metal tin. It went into the first bin.
6. Nora found a glass jar. She carried it carefully with both hands.
7. I collected plastic bottle tops until my bag felt heavy.
8. A small crab watched us from between the wet stones.
9. We worked from the rocks to the water and back again.
10. By noon the sand was clean and safe for small feet.
11. "The sea gives us so much," said Teacher Yasmin. "Today we gave something back."
12. We left only footprints, and the waves smoothed even those away.
