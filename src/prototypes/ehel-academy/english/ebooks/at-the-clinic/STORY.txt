2. "Good morning, Amal. How do you feel today?" asked Doctor Sarah.
3. "Not very well. My stomach hurts, and I feel a little dizzy."
4. "I am sorry to hear that. Can you tell me what you ate?"

6. "Was the meat fresh?" "No. It smelled strange. But I was very hungry, so I ate it anyway."
7. "That was not a wise choice," said Doctor Sarah. "When meat is not fresh, chemicals can build up inside it."
8. "What should I do now?" "Drink plenty of clean water, and rest at home today instead of going to school."
9. "You must be more careful. Old food might make you very sick. Always check your food first."
10. "I will remember that, Doctor Sarah. Thank you for helping me."
11. "You are welcome. Tell your grandmother to gather only fresh food from the market from now on."
12. Amal walked home slowly, with a bottle of clean water and one clear rule to keep.
