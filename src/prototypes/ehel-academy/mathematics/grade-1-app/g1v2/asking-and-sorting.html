Asking and Sorting

1

What this lesson is about

By the end of this lesson you will be able to: Ask everyone a question and collect the answers; put answers into a list and a table; build a block graph and read it; read a pictogram; sort things on a venn diagram or a carroll diagram; say what the answers tell us.

Ask everyone

For the grown-up: what to have to hand

A handful of things to sort into two groups - buttons or bottle tops in two colours - a loop of string for a sorting hoop, and paper to make a tally on while asking everyone at home a question.
