THE CHICK AND THE DUCK
Grade 1 - Level 1
Little Pip meets a duck at the pond and learns that chicks run while ducks swim.

1. The Chick and the Duck. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Little Pip stayed close to Koko now.
3. One day Pip met a duck.
4. The duck went in. Splash, splash!
5. "Come in, Pip!" said the duck.
6. "No, no! Chicks do not swim!"
7. Pip hopped along the side. Hop, hop!
8. The duck swam and Pip ran.
9. They played all the sunny morning.
10. Koko called. "Home time, little Pip!"
11. "Goodbye, duck! See you tomorrow!"
12. Pip told Koko all about it.
