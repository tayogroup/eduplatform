Grade 1 English Review Desk

Every item in the Grade 1 English course that does not carry a curriculum reviewer's approval, with the text to read and — where it was rewritten on 10 September 2026 — the version it replaced.

Nothing on this page changes the course. A verdict recorded here is a note of what you decided. Writing Approved – curriculum reviewer onto an item in the course files is a separate, deliberate step, and it is only ever done from what you record here.

RewrittenThe item was already in the course and its wording changed on 10 September. Read the two columns and decide whether the change is sound. Fastest lane.
NewThe item did not exist before 10 September. Nobody has read it. Read it cold.
StandingUnchanged, and never approved by a reviewer. The oldest backlog, and the largest.
