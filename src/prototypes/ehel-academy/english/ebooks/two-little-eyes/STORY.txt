TWO LITTLE EYES
Grade 1 - Level 1
Grade 1 shelf - Rhyme series, book 3 (Term 3, Unit 6: My Five Senses)

1. Two Little Eyes. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Amal says a poem about her senses. Point as you say it.
3. Two little eyes to see all around.
4. Two little ears to hear each sound.
5. One little nose to smell what's sweet.
6. One little mouth that likes to eat.
7. "Which sense is not in the poem?" asked Ayeeyo.
8. Amal thought. Then she wiggled her fingers. "Touch!"
9. "Yes," said Ayeeyo. "Five senses, not four."
10. See, hear, smell, taste, touch.
11. Amal said the poem again and touched Ayeeyo's soft shawl.
12. "Now I have them all," she said.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: see, hear, smell, taste, touch, eyes, ears, nose, mouth, sweet, soft.
Themes: a poem that names four senses, and the child who finds the fifth
Cameos: the poem and the question at the end of it are both the Unit 6 reading's own; Ayeeyo asks it.
