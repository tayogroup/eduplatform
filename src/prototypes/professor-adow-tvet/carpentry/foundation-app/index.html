Carpentry Foundation

The hand tools of the trade, how they are kept and how a workshop is kept safe, and how a rough board is brought to a straight, square, finished size. Everything here is what the joint lessons assume you already have.

Where this leadsThis is foundation work. It is not a unit of the Kenyan Carpentry & Joinery Level 4 qualification — all eight of those units assume you already have tools and joints. Preparing stock to size is behind every one of them, and most directly behind Door and Window Frames (0732 251 02D), Timber Furniture Items (0732 351 06D) and Timber Floors and Framed Structures (0732 351 07D).

Lessons

Preparing Timber to Size
Group the hand tools by what they do, name the parts of a smoothing plane, take four damaged tools out of service, choose timber for a job, and take a rough-sawn board to a straight, square, finished size. · 10 steps
