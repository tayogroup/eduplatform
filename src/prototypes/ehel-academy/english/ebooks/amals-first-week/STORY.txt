AMAL'S FIRST WEEK
Grade 2 - Level 2
Grade 2 shelf - Story series, book 4 (Term 1, Unit 1: Welcome and Calendar)

1. Amal's First Week. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. It was Amal's first day at a new school. She held her mother's hand and looked at the big blue door.
3. "Good morning. Welcome!" said a kind teacher. "My name is Teacher Yasmin. What is your name?"
4. "My name is Amal. A-M-A-L," she said. She felt a little braver now.
5. Inside, there was a big calendar on the wall, and a chart of all the colours.
6. "Hello! My name is Leo," said a boy. "Today is Saturday. You can be my partner!"
7. The whole class sang. "Saturday, Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday!"
8. "I like drawing," said Amal. "I like football," said Leo. "And mangoes!"
9. On Monday she learned her colours. On Tuesday she counted to twelve.
10. On Thursday a new girl came. She stood by the door and looked shy.
11. "Hello. My name is Amal. You can be my partner today." "My name is Nora," she said.
12. "I have two friends now," Amal told her mother. "And I know all the days of the week!"

Themes: Amal starts a new school, learns the days of the week, and on Thursday does for Nora what Leo did for her
