THE PUPPY HIDES
Grade 1 - Level 1
A little puppy hides, and Amal and Adam look under, in, on and behind everything.

1. The Puppy Hides. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. A little puppy came to play.
3. "Let us play hide and seek!"
4. The puppy ran away to hide.
5. Is he under the bed? No!
6. Is he in the basket? No!
7. Is he on the mat? No!
8. Is he behind the door? No!
9. Then they heard a little sound.
10. The puppy was under the big tree!
11. "We found you!" laughed Amal.
12. The happy puppy jumped up and down.
