UP WITH THE SUN
Grade 3 - Level 3
Idris wants to win the big race, so he trains every morning: wake up, stretch, run, climb - and keep well.

1. Up with the Sun. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. The school games were coming, and I wanted to win the big race.
3. "A healthy body starts with sleep," said Dad. So I went to bed early.
4. Firstly, I wake up when the sun does, while the town is still asleep.
5. Secondly, I stretch and exercise in the yard. Ten jumps, ten hops.
6. Then Theo and I run to the old tree and back. Every day a little faster.
7. We climb the low wall at the schoolyard - carefully, away from danger.
8. Mina exercises with me too. Three jumps, then a rest.
9. One morning I dropped my water bottle and it broke. Mum found me another.
10. On sports day I was wide awake and ready. My legs felt strong, not weak.
11. Finally, the race! I ran my fastest ever, and this year I came second.
12. "Second is a win too," said Dad, "when last year you came fifth."
