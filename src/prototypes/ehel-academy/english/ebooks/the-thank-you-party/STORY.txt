THE THANK-YOU PARTY
Grade 3 - Level 3
The neighbours help Amal's family all year. So the family throws a party in the garden to say thank you.

1. The Thank-You Party. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. "Our neighbours help us all year," said Mum. "Let us thank them."
3. We decided to invite them to a small party in our garden.
4. I wrote the invitations in my very best handwriting.
5. Adam carried the chairs. Idris swept the path. Mina picked the flowers.
6. Grandma Hana cooked, and the whole street could smell it.
7. At four o'clock the neighbours came, and Omar came too, with mangoes.
8. "Thank you for the party," they said. "Thank you for being kind neighbours," said Dad.
9. We were polite. We passed the food and let our guests choose first.
10. Mina used her small voice, and everybody heard her anyway.
11. When the sun went down, we waved goodbye at the gate.
12. I felt glad and thankful. A promise to say thank you is easy to keep.
