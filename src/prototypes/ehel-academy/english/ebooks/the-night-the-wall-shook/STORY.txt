THE NIGHT THE WALL SHOOK
Grade 3 - Level 3
Amal series, book 4 of 4, from the listening recount "What Happened?" (Term 2, Unit 5: Actions and Activities)

1. The Night the Wall Shook. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. "Let me tell you what happened yesterday," said Sami.
3. "The wind blew very hard, and the wall began to shake because of it."
4. "I was standing near the garden when I first heard the creaking sound."
5. "What did you do?" "We ran to hold the frame."
6. "Leo grabbed one side, and I grabbed the other."
7. "We held on tightly until the wind slowed down again."
8. "Did the wall fall down?" "No. But we did not complete the support in time."
9. "The wooden frame still needs more stones underneath it."
10. "What will you do differently?" "Add more stones. Then it will be much stronger."
11. "Can I help you fix it after school?" "Yes, please. Two pairs of hands finish faster than one."
12. We finished on the Thursday. The next wind came, and the wall did not move at all.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: happen, complete, build, protect, remove, discuss, offer, listen.
Themes: finishing the part nobody sees; the repair is the story, not the rescue
Cameos: Sami and Leo holding the frame, and the support they did not complete, are both from the Unit 5 recount.
