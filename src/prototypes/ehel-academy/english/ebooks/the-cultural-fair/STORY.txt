THE CULTURAL FAIR
Grade 4 - Level 4
Amal (Grade 4) series, book 5 (Term 3, Unit 7: Emotions, Behaviour, and Identity)

1. The Cultural Fair. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. "How do you feel today, Nora?" asked Maya. "I feel curious. We are visiting a cultural fair tomorrow."
3. "Have you ever been to one?" "Yes. I went last year, and I felt so proud to show my family's food."
4. "Why are you curious?" "Because I have never tried food from so many different places before."
5. "What did your family bring to the fair?" "A big tray of rice and spiced chicken."
6. "My little brother helped carry the plates, even though he felt shy at first."
7. "Were you nervous standing behind the table?" "A little, at the start."
8. "But everyone was so polite, and lots of people asked kind questions about our food."
9. "By the end I wasn't nervous at all." "I hope I feel that brave tomorrow."
10. The next day the hall was full of tables, and Nora tried five new foods without once feeling shy.
11. "Don't be shy about asking people questions," Maya had said. "Most families are proud to talk about where their food comes from."
12. Nora asked eleven of them, and wrote every answer down. Curious, it turned out, was a good way to feel.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: curious, proud, nervous, shy, polite, generous, ethnic.
Themes: curiosity as a way of being brave in a room full of strangers
Cameos: the conversation is the Unit 7 dialogue "A Chat About Feelings"; the fair is the one they are discussing.
