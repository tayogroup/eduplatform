THE LIBRARY THAT CAME BY CART
Grade 4 - Level 4
Amal series, book 4 (Term 2, Unit 4: Community and Communication)

1. The Library That Came by Cart. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Our town has no library building. What it has is a cart.
3. Every Thursday it arrives, painted blue, with two shelves of books and one squeaky wheel.
4. Teacher Yasmin says a library is a service, not a building. The cart proves her right.
5. Maya writes for the town paper. She is twelve, and she has a notebook for everything.
6. She interviewed Omar about how many people ask him to read to them. The number surprised the whole town.
7. So Maya wrote an article: "A town that wants to read." It was printed on the front page.
8. At the town meeting, people argued about priorities. A road, a roof, or a room full of books?
9. Little Mina asked why we could not simply keep the cart. That got the biggest laugh of the evening.
10. In the end the vote was for a community centre, with one room for the books.
11. The cart still comes on Thursdays. Nobody wanted to give it up.
12. Maya kept the front page. "Information," she says, "is just knowledge that somebody bothered to share."

Unit vocabulary reinforced: service, deliver, information, thought, discover, knowledge, challenge, priority, quality, judge, location, population.
Themes: a library is a service, not a building; sharing what you find out
Cameos: the travelling library, Maya the young reporter and the town meeting are all Unit 4's own.
