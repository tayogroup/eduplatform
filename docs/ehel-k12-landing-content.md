## 2. Why Families Choose Ehel K-12

*(Four to six short differentiator cards — the "why us, not just any online school" section.)*

**Live classes, not video libraries.**
Every class runs in real time with a real teacher your child can talk to, ask questions, and be seen by. If a lesson isn't landing, the teacher adjusts on the spot — something a pre-recorded course can never do.

**Placed properly before day one.**
No child starts in the wrong seat. Every student goes through a short placement step so their first class actually matches where they are — not just their age or grade label.

**Small classes, real attention.**
Choose the group size that fits your child, from one-to-one to a small cohort. Nobody gets lost in a lecture hall of thirty video tiles.

**Teachers who are actually vetted.**
Every teacher on the platform goes through a review and approval process before they're allowed in front of students — not just an application form.

**Parents stay informed, not locked out.**
Parent and guardian accounts are built in from day one: attendance, progress, and communication are visible to the people responsible for the child, not buried behind a login only the student holds.

**Safety and consent, handled properly.**
Live classes follow clear consent and recording policies agreed with parents up front, so there's no ambiguity about what's recorded, why, and who can see it.

**Built around your schedule, not a fixed timetable.**
Families pick from real weekly time slots across time zones, so a class time in your evening doesn't mean a teacher awake at 3 a.m. — or a schedule that only works for one part of the world.

## 3. What a Family Actually Gets

*(Concrete, non-vague description of the mechanics — this is the section that separates "marketing" from "informational.")*

- **A structured intake, not a signup form.** We ask about the student's current level, curriculum background, and learning goals before placing them — because "grade 4" means something different at every school they may be coming from.
- **A weekly rhythm, not a one-off booking.** Families choose how many sessions per week and which days/times work, and that becomes the student's regular schedule.
- **A named teacher, not a rotating pool.** Once placed, students keep a consistent teacher for continuity — the same person who taught last week's lesson is the one building on it this week.
- **A visible paper trail for parents.** Enrollment status, consent records, and communication all sit in one place a parent can actually see, instead of scattered emails and screenshots.
- **A path that starts small.** Enrollment requests don't lock a family into anything — the school reviews placement and confirms the best class options before anything is finalized.

## 5. For Parents

**Headline:** Everything you need to say yes with confidence.

We know handing your child's education to an online program is a real decision. That's why enrollment starts with a proper conversation — about your child's level, your schedule, your concerns — before we ever put them in front of a class. You'll always know who is teaching your child, when, what's being recorded and why, and how to reach us. This isn't a black box.

**Common questions parents ask:**

- **How do you make sure the teacher is qualified?** Every teacher goes through a vetting and approval process before being allowed to teach — this isn't self-service.
- **What if my child is placed at the wrong level?** Placement happens before enrollment is finalized specifically to avoid this — and the school reviews every request rather than auto-assigning.
- **Is class time recorded?** Recording and consent are opt-in and explained clearly — you decide what you're comfortable with, and it's documented, not assumed.
- **What if our time zone makes this hard?** Scheduling is built around real weekly availability across time zones, not a single fixed timetable designed for one region.
- **Can I stay involved without hovering?** Parent/guardian access is part of the account structure from the start — you're not shut out and you're not required to sit in every class either.

## 6. How It Works

1. **Tell us about your student.** A short form covering the student's background, current level, and what you're hoping to get out of it — for parent and student alike.
2. **We review and place.** Ehel K-12 reviews the request and confirms the right class, teacher fit, and schedule — not an automatic sign-up.
3. **Confirm the schedule and consent.** Weekly session count, time zone, availability, and the consent details that matter to your family.
4. **Start class.** Your child joins live sessions with a real teacher, at the level and pace that's actually right for them.
