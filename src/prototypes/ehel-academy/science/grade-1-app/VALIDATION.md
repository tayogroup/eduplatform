Reviewed 2026-09-11. Supersedes version 2, 2026-09-11 (in git history, and kept as its own Word file in OneDrive). Reviewer: Claude (automated measurement, browser driving, an independent content review by a second Claude agent, and judgement); no human teacher review yet. Against the 27-area framework in *science-grade-1-v2 validation.docx*. The Word copy of this report is *science-grade-1-v2 validation report v3 2026-09-11.docx* beside that file in OneDrive; both are generated from the same rows.

## Summary

Third version, written after the open issues from version 2 were worked through the same day. Every open item that did not need a person was fixed. An independent content review found 60 things to change in the Grade 1 text: 23 facts, 11 answer keys, 14 wording points and 12 home-safety points. The most serious were a battery described as 'electricity in a box', an alive test that plants fail, steel spoons and brass keys said to stick to magnets, and a kitten in a feely bag. All 60 are applied. Each Grade 1 lesson now comes in two sittings of about 20 to 25 minutes with a break card between, and opens with a recap of the lesson before and a two-question warm-up. Each quiz gained a reasoning question. No Grade 1 objective is now reached by only one teaching step. Pictures from Emoji 13 and later are drawn by the kit itself, so older devices no longer show empty boxes, and the builder refuses any page that would carry one. Grade 2 lesson 6 has its own simulations instead of replaying Grade 1's. Grade 4 now has no objective reached by only one teaching step. The shared lesson check can now see a missing header bar. All eight Grade 1 lessons were driven to 100% in a browser, and Grades 1 to 3 were pushed and redeployed as 924e54d4b. The average rises from 4.1 to 4.4. What remains needs people: a teacher's read, a screen-reader session, the school's own devices, and children timed and watched.

Average score **4.4 / 5** across 27 areas (version 2: 4.1). Lowest score 4, in 15 areas. No High-priority finding.

Change status at 2026-09-11: **16** areas need no change or are done, **5** have their fix live with a person's check still open, **6** are open.

## What changed since version 2

- Nine scores rose: content depth (3), progression (7), balance across grades (8), lesson structure (9), assessment balance (11), learning time (17), multimedia (18), learner progress (22) and duplication (26). None fell.
- The content review found errors that versions 1 and 2 scored past: 23 factual points and 12 home-safety points. Versions 1 and 2 rated accuracy and safeguarding on a read by the same reviewer who wrote the text; a second reader found what the first did not.
- The balance figure in version 2 was misleading. It divided steps by objectives, but Grade 4 steps each teach more objectives. Counted per objective, Grade 1 has about 3.9 teaching steps and Grade 4 about 3.3.
- Version 2's 'Decide whether Science world shows before it has content' is closed: the owner asked for Science world as a placeholder.

## What was fixed during this review

- Content review: 60 changes to the Grade 1 text across all eight lessons - 23 facts, 11 answer keys, 14 wording points and 12 home-safety points. No step was added, removed or reordered, so every learner's saved place is still valid.
- Two sittings: each Grade 1 lesson is cut at the step nearest its halfway point, about 20 to 25 minutes each. A break card offers 'Stop for today' or 'Keep going', and progress means a child who stops starts there next time. The hub shows both times.
- The overview now opens with 'Last time', a recap of the lesson before, and a two-question warm-up 'What do you already know?' that is never marked. The builder refuses a warm-up that repeats a quiz question.
- One reasoning question added to each Grade 1 quiz (72 items, from 64). Two items added so 1Cp.01 has a second teaching step, and 1ESs.01 tagged on the demo that teaches it.
- Pictures: every Emoji 13+ glyph on a page is swapped at build time for a drawing of the same object, and the builder refuses a page that still carries one. Seven pictures that showed the wrong thing were redrawn or replaced, including a sheep as a baby goat, a pot plant as roots, a feather as a chrysalis, and a boomerang as an elastic band.
- Grade 2 lesson 6: its push experiment measures three pushes on a toy car, so the 'medium push' row records something the child saw. Its shape experiment uses a sponge, a paperclip and a wooden block.
- Grade 4: three new practice steps (safety with electricity, energy technology near you, backing a point with science), so no Grade 4 objective has a single teaching step.
- The shared lesson check now fails a page missing its header bar, for builds whose config opts in. It was mutation-tested and is opted in for all four Science grades.

## Verdict

Grade 1 is fit to teach, and the version on learners' screens now matches this report. The next steps are the four human checks, and they cannot be closed by anything more done here.
