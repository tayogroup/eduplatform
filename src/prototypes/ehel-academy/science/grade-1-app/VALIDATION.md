# Grade 1 Science (science/grade-1-v2) validation, version 2

Reviewed 2026-09-11. Supersedes version 1, 2026-09-10 (in git history, and kept as its own Word file in OneDrive). Reviewer: Claude (automated measurement, browser driving and judgement); no human teacher review yet. Scale: 1 Unsatisfactory; 2 Needs major improvement; 3 Acceptable with revisions; 4 Strong; 5 Excellent. Build reviewed: commit 742eca118.

Against the 27-area framework in *science-grade-1-v2 validation.docx*. The Word copy of this report is *science-grade-1-v2 validation report v2 2026-09-11.docx* beside that file in OneDrive; both are generated from the same rows.

## Summary

Second validation of Grade 1 Science, one day after the first. Since version 1 every lesson gained a seven-part unit shell (what this unit is about, unit lecture, science words, games, things to do at home, Science world, student resources), and Grades 2 to 4 now exist, so progression and balance can be judged against real neighbours for the first time. All 35 Stage 1 objectives are still reached, all eight lessons were driven to 100% in a browser, and the reading level fell to Flesch-Kincaid grade 2.2. This round found and fixed four defects: the Quick quiz game rehearsed the lesson's own end-quiz questions; two steps marked themselves done when drawn rather than when visited; the game and resource overlays were not proper dialogs for keyboard users; and lesson 8 called a star 'a huge ball of fire', a known misconception (Grades 3 and 4 had the same fault as 'burning gas'). The fixes are built into Grades 1 to 4 and committed as 742eca118, but none is live until the Science builds are pushed and redeployed. The main new concern is length: the shell took a Grade 1 lesson to 40 to 45 minutes, longer than any lesson in Grades 2 to 4, so area 17 drops to 3. The average stays at 4.1. There is no High-priority finding. A human teacher read is still the one thing between this build and formal sign-off.

Average score **4.1 / 5** across 27 areas (version 1: 4.1). Lowest score 3, in 4 areas: Balance Across Grades, Assessment Balance, Learning-Time Estimates, Multimedia Quality. No High-priority finding.

Change status at 2026-09-11: **10** areas need no change or are done, **7** have this round's fix built and committed but not yet live, **10** are open.

## What changed since version 1

- Area 17, Learning-Time Estimates, fell from 4 to 3: the unit shell lengthened every Grade 1 lesson to 40 to 45 minutes.
- Area 24, Teacher and Parent Support, rose from 4 to 5: three home projects and a student resources section now sit in every lesson.
- Areas 7 and 8 were re-scored against the real Grades 2 to 4 instead of being left open. Their scores did not move, but their evidence is now measured.
- Version 1's only High-priority finding, resume on reopen, is live. The shared tool has since been hardened so that opening a lesson cannot overwrite the saved place.
- Two corrections to version 1. It counted six glyphs needing 2020-era emoji support; there are eight. Its '90 quiz items' was 64 end-quiz items plus 26 practice questions.

## What was fixed during this review

- The Quick quiz game drew its rounds from the lesson's end quiz, so a child could rehearse the exact assessment questions minutes before meeting them. It now uses the practice questions only, and the builder refuses any game round that repeats a quiz question. Grades 1 to 4.
- The unit overview and Science world marked themselves done as soon as the page drew them. They now tick when the child reaches them. Grades 1 to 4.
- The game and resource overlays were plain boxes: Tab walked out behind them and closing left focus nowhere. They are now dialogs that keep Tab inside and return focus to the button that opened them. Grades 1 to 4.
- 'A huge ball of fire' (Grade 1 lesson 8, five places) and 'burning gas' (Grade 3 lesson 13, Grade 4 lesson 12) now read 'a huge ball of hot, glowing gas'.
- The builder's new refusal found two Grade 2 practice questions that were word for word quiz questions, in lessons 4 and 7. Both replaced.
- All four grades rebuilt through the shared pipeline with both gates green, and committed as 742eca118. Not yet pushed or deployed.

## Verdict

Grade 1 remains fit to teach. The next steps are to push and redeploy so this round's fixes go live, and to decide how to shorten Grade 1 sittings before the term timetable is set. Grade 4, which is built but not shipped, already carries the same fixes.
