Grade 2 - Level 2
Zuri series, book 10 (Term 3, Unit 10: My English World (capstone))

1. Zuri's Book of the Year. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. It was the last week of Year 2. "Make a book about your year," said Miss Twiga.
3. On the first page Zuri wrote her name, and spelled it: Z-U-R-I.
4. On the second page she drew her neighbours: the bus driver, the firefighter and the reporter.
5. On the third page: a head, two arms, two hands and ten fingers. "This is me!"
6. On the fourth page she drew the sun, the light and her own long shadow.
7. On the fifth page she counted in tens: ten, twenty, thirty - one hundred.
8. On the sixth page: a butterfly, a cricket and one busy little ant.
9. On the seventh page: planting, watering and picking up litter.
10. On the eighth page: a house, a flat and a hut. Every home is different.
11. On the ninth page: the library, the shopping centre and the underground.
12. On the last page Zuri drew all her friends. "Goodbye, Year 2," she said. "Hello, Year 3!"

Unit vocabulary reinforced: one page for each of Units 1-9: name, neighbour, head, shadow, ten, butterfly, planting, house, library.
Themes: gathering a year up; looking forward to the next one
Cameos: the whole cast — Musa, Kiki, Duku, Lulu, Miss Twiga, the elephant and the ostrich.
