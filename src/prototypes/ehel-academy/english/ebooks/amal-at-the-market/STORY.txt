Grade 1 - Level 1
Amal (Grade 1) series, book 6 (Term 2, Unit 6: My Five Senses)

1. Amal at the Market. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. The market was big and busy. "Come, Amal. Use your five senses today!"
3. First, Amal used her eyes to see. Red tomatoes. Yellow bananas. Green mangoes.
4. Next she used her ears to hear. "Fresh fish!" called a man. A goat said, "Baa!"
5. Then she used her nose to smell. She smelled warm bread.
6. She smelled sweet flowers. She smelled hot spices from a big pot.
7. A kind man named Omar gave Amal a piece of ripe mango.
8. She used her tongue to taste it. "It is sweet and juicy!" said Amal.
9. Last, she used her hands to touch. She touched a soft cloth.
10. She touched a smooth stone. It felt hard and cold.
11. "Look, Ayeeyo! This melon is big. But that melon is bigger!"
12. "I am thankful for my eyes, my ears, my nose, my tongue and my hands!"

Themes: one busy place, met five different ways; being thankful for the senses
