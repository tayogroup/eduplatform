VEGETABLES FOR THE POT
Grade 1 - Level 1
Amal and Ayeeyo pick carrots, potatoes, onions, beans and a tomato for the soup pot.

1. Vegetables for the Pot. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. "Come, Amal," said Ayeeyo. "Soup today!"
3. They went to the little garden.
4. Amal pulled up two orange carrots.
5. Ayeeyo dug up three brown potatoes.
6. Amal found one round onion.
7. They picked long green beans too.
8. One red tomato went in the basket.
9. They washed everything at the well.
10. Into the big pot it all went!
11. The soup smelled so good. Mmm!
12. "Yum!" said everyone at dinner time.
