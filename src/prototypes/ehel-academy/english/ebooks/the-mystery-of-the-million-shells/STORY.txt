Grade 3 - Level 3
Amal series, book 8 (Term 3, Unit 8: Numbers, Shapes, and Measurement)

1. The Mystery of the Million Shells. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Teacher Yasmin asked us a question. "How many shells are on our beach? Give me a number."
3. "A hundred," said Idris. "A thousand," said Nora. "A million," I said, because it was the biggest number I knew.
4. "Good," she said. "Now prove it."
5. So we measured one square metre of sand with a straight tape, and counted the shells inside it.
6. There were sixty-four. Sixty-four shells in one single square metre.
7. Then we measured the beach itself: two hundred metres long, and thirty metres wide.
8. Multiplication is faster than counting. Two hundred times thirty is six thousand square metres.
9. Six thousand times sixty-four. Adam did the addition twice, to check the fact.
10. Three hundred and eighty-four thousand. Not a million - but much closer to a million than to a hundred.
11. "You did not count them," said Teacher Yasmin. "You measured. That is what maths is for."
12. I looked at the whole beach and, for the very first time, I could see the size of it.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: number, million, fact, pattern, straight, addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, measure, size, metre, height, weight, distance.
Themes: measuring instead of counting; the size of a big number
