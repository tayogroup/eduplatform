FROM COAST TO FOREST
Grade 3 - Level 3
Amal series, book 7 (Term 3, Unit 7: Nature and the Environment)

1. From Coast to Forest. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Teacher Yasmin planned a trip. "We will start at the coast," she said, "and finish on the mountain."
3. We packed water and hats. The weather can change from morning to night, even when the climate stays the same.
4. At the beach the sunshine warmed the sand. The sea went out and came back, out and back.
5. Nora had never seen the sea before. "Have you ever tasted it?" she asked. She tasted it once, and only once.
6. We found metal bottle tops in the sand and carried them away in a bag.
7. Then the forest. Under the trees the temperature dropped, and it was suddenly cool and green.
8. "Everything here is matter," said Teacher Yasmin. "And everything here uses energy. Even the trees."
9. We have seen a beetle carry a leaf twice its size. We have heard a bird we could not find.
10. The trail went up. Higher and higher, until the trees were below us instead of above us.
11. Near the top, the water in our bottles was so cold it almost froze. We could see the whole coast.
12. "One planet," said Teacher Yasmin. "Coast, forest and mountain, all in one morning." I have never forgotten it.
