THE WEATHER BOOK
Grade 4 - Level 4
Amal and Nora build their own weather book — contents, headings, diagrams, charts and one honest correction at a time.

1. The Weather Book. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. "Let us make our own weather book," said Nora. "One chapter for every kind of sky."
3. Amal wrote the contents page first. A good heading tells you where to look.
4. The textbook showed a diagram of clouds. They copied it carefully and added labels.
5. On the tablet, Nora found a rainfall chart and read the numbers aloud.
6. Amal made a mistake in her diagram. "A correction is not a failure," said Nora.
7. Every afternoon they checked the real sky against their book.
8. Grey days, breezy days, one sudden shower — every kind of sky filled a page.
9. "A book is a tool," said Mum, turning the pages. "And this one is well made."
10. At school, Teacher Yasmin read their favourite chapter to the whole class.
11. "May I borrow it?" asked Sami. Soon the weather book had a queue.
12. On the last page Nora wrote one result: weather happens everywhere. Keep watching.
