AFTER THE TRIP
Grade 3 - Level 3
Back from the coast, the class builds a nature table to keep the day.

1. After the Trip. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. The day after the trip, our classroom smelled of sea and leaves.
3. "Let us build a nature table," said Teacher Yasmin.
4. Amal laid out the shells. Nora added a smooth grey stone.
5. Leo pinned up the map and marked our route: coast, forest, mountain.
6. We wrote our poem in big letters above the table.
7. Sami drew the little crab that ran over the teacher's shoe.
8. "What was the temperature in the forest?" asked Nora. "Twenty-two degrees!"
9. The younger pupils came to see our table, two by two.
10. I told them the trees looked tiny below us, like moss.
11. "Have you ever seen the sea?" I asked Mina. "Not yet," she said.
12. "Then one day we will show you," said Teacher Yasmin. And we will.
