1. The Science Fair Poster. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Amal and Nora had one afternoon left to finish their science-fair poster.
3. "Look at our poster," said Amal. "A tornado spins faster than any other storm."
4. "Yes," said Nora, "and a hurricane is stronger than a normal storm. The volcano is the most powerful of all."
5. "I still love the canyon picture best," Amal said. "It shows just how deep a canyon can be — deeper even than the lake."
6. "True. But look at our moisture chart. It shows how rain forms over the bay and drifts across the meadow."
7. "Do you remember when hail fell on the school roof last week? It rattled like tiny stones for ten whole minutes."
8. "I do. My little brother roamed around the garden afterwards, collecting the biggest pieces before they melted."
9. "We should tell visitors that a storm can be dangerous," said Amal, "but that we can stay safe if we watch the sky."
10. "Good idea. Let us practise our safety facts once more before the judges arrive."
11. They said them twice through, and the second time neither girl needed to look.
12. When the judges came, the two friends did not read their poster aloud. They explained it, and that was better.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: tornado, hurricane, volcano, canyon, moisture, bay, meadow, hail, roam.
Themes: practising until you can explain it instead of read it
