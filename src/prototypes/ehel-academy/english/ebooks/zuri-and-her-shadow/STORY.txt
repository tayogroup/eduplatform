ZURI AND HER SHADOW
Grade 2 - Level 2
Zuri series, book 4 (Term 2, Unit 4: The Big Sky)

1. Zuri and Her Shadow. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Early in the morning the sun came up. Zuri saw a long, dark shape beside her.
3. "Who are you?" she asked. The shape said nothing at all. It was her shadow.
4. The shadow copied everything. Zuri hopped. The shadow hopped too.
5. At midday the sun was high. Zuri's shadow was very short. "Where did you go?"
6. Miss Twiga explained. "The sun is the light. You block the light. That makes a shadow."
7. In the evening the sun went low. The shadow grew long again, all the way to the house.
8. At sunset the sky turned orange and pink. Then the shadow slipped away.
9. Night came. The sky was dark. Zuri looked up at the moon and the stars.
10. "Where is my shadow now?" she asked. "Waiting," said Mama. "It waits for the light."
11. The next morning was cloudy and grey. No sun, no shadow. But Zuri waited.
12. At last the clouds moved. Sunrise! "Good morning, shadow," said Zuri. "You came back."

Unit vocabulary reinforced: shadow, light, sun, block, sky, morning, midday, evening, sunrise, sunset, moon, star, cloud, day, night, long, short, high, low, bright, dark, grey, cloudy, sunny.
Themes: noticing something ordinary and asking why; a whole day and night
Cameos: Miss Twiga explains the light; Zuri's mama at the burrow.
