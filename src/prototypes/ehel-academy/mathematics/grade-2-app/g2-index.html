Grade 2 Mathematics

Nine lessons, one for each part of the maths you learn this year. Pick the one you want — you can come back and change your mind.

Number

Tens and Ones

Every number to 100 and what its digits mean. See a small amount at a glance and estimate a big one, read numbers as words, compare and order them, say which place in a line, round to the nearest ten, then add, take away, share and make rows.

16 steps · check · stickersStart Money

Coins and Change

Know the coins and notes, count what is in the purse, make an exact amount and then make it with the fewest pieces, work out what a basket costs, count the change, and decide which of two prices is better value.

9 steps · check · stickersStart Fractions

Fair Shares

Equal parts before anything else. What the top and bottom numbers mean, a fraction of a group, two names for the same amount, which fraction is bigger, where they sit on a number line, adding them, and going past one whole.

9 steps · check · stickersStart Patterns

Patterns That Grow

Find the part that repeats and mend a pattern with a hole in it. Then patterns that grow rather than repeat: find the rule, fill the missing number, predict far ahead without drawing it, and make one that shrinks.

9 steps · check · stickersStart Shape and space

Sides and Corners

Count sides and corners and name the shape, solve shape riddles, fold for a line of symmetry, meet the solid shapes, find the middle of a circle, spot shapes hiding in real things, turn a shape all the way round, test a right angle, and see which shapes tile.

13 steps · check · stickersStart Position and direction

Which Way From Here

Say where something is and name a square on a grid. Give a route and follow one, use north, south, east and west, work out whose left is whose, then flip a shape over a mirror line, slide it and turn it about a corner.

13 steps · check · stickersStart Measurement

How Much, How Long

Compare lengths, measure with cubes and then with a ruler, and draw a line to the length you are asked for. Choose the right unit, balance the scales, read grams and kilograms, millilitres and litres, and read a value that falls between two marks.

11 steps · check · stickersStart Time

Half Past, Quarter To

What the two hands do, quarter past and quarter to, counting round the clock in fives, and reading it to the very minute on a clock face and a digital one. Then the units from seconds to years, and reading a real calendar month.

14 steps · check · stickersStart Statistics and probability

Count It, Chart It

Sort things into groups, keep a tally, read a pictogram where one picture stands for two, build a block graph and read a bar chart with a scale. Then how likely something is, what is in the bag, whether a game is fair, and telling a real pattern from a random one.

For the grown-up. These nine lessons cover all 48 objectives of Cambridge Primary Mathematics Stage 2 — number, money, fractions, geometry and measure, and statistics and probability. Cards that share a colour share a strand: shape sits with position, and measurement with time.

Two of them keep going past Stage 2. Half Past, Quarter To and Which Way From Here were written as whole-topic lessons, so after the Stage 2 work they carry on into the 24-hour clock, timetables and coordinates that cross zero. A Grade 2 child can stop at the check and has still met everything the stage asks for; the later steps are there for when they are ready.
