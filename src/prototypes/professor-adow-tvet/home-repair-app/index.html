Home Repair

Repairs in a finished building, done without making the damage worse. The module opens with wall tiling: taking a broken tile out of a wall that is already grouted and putting a new one back so that nobody can tell which one it was.

Replace a broken wall tile

Replace a Broken Tile

Take one broken tile out of a finished, grouted wall and put a new one back flush with its neighbours — masking and drilling to control the break, raking the joints first, chipping from the centre out, clearing the bed flat, then bedding, spacing, grouting and polishing.

8 steps · about 30 min · checkStart
