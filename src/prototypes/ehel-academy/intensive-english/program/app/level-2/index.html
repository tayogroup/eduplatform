Ehel Academy · Intensive English

Level 2 — Getting Things Done

By the end: Can deal with everyday transactions at work, in shops, offices and on journeys; describe a routine, a past event and an experience in connected sentences; say how long something has been true; explain how something works; ask politely and give an opinion with a reason; and write short messages, notes and emails.

A212 lessonsweeks 18–29 of the programme4 days per lessonabout 108 hours

Part 1 · Work and daily business

1My work and my weekYou will be able to describe your job, your week and what you have to do. 2Hosts and guestsYou will be able to offer, invite, give instructions and check what someone means. 3Getting things doneYou will be able to say what you have and haven’t done, and deal with offices, forms and repairs. 4Plans and arrangementsYou will be able to arrange, change and predict plans.
Then: Part 1 review & check (2 days)

Part 2 · Problems and choices

5When things go wrongYou will be able to tell what happened and what was going on at the time. 6Shopping and servicesYou will be able to compare options, choose one and ask for what you need. 7Home and health over timeYou will be able to say how long something has been true. 8Feelings and reactionsYou will be able to say how things are and how they make you feel.
Then: Part 2 review & check (2 days)

Part 3 · Talking with people

9Polite requests and opinionsYou will be able to ask politely, agree and disagree, and give an opinion with a reason. 10How things work, and the rulesYou will be able to explain how something works, and understand rules and safety notices. 11Travel and timetablesYou will be able to read a timetable, book a journey and deal with a delay. 12Keeping a conversation goingYou will be able to ask follow-up questions, make suggestions and talk about your experiences.
Then: A2 review & check (2 days)
