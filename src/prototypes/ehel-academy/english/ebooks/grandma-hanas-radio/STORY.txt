GRANDMA HANA'S RADIO
Grade 3 - Level 3
The rain comes down, the lights go out - and Grandma Hana's little old radio saves the evening.

1. Grandma Hana's Radio. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. One evening, the rain came down and the lights went out.
3. No television. No lamp for reading. Mina began to worry.
4. Then Grandma Hana brought out a small old radio.
5. "This radio is older than your dad," she said, and turned the little wheel.
6. A voice came out, telling a story about a clever fisherman.
7. We sat close together in the dark and listened to every word.
8. "When I was young," said Grandma Hana, "the radio was our television."
9. The story ended. So Grandma Hana told the next one herself.
10. Hers was even better. It had a storm, a boat, and a very brave hen.
11. When the lights came back on, nobody turned the television on.
12. Some stories need no screen at all. Just a voice, and people listening.
