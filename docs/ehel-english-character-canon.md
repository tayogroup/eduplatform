# Ehel Academy English — Course-Wide Character Canon

Supersedes `ehel-grade1-character-canon.md`, which predates the religion-neutrality
decision and still names Ishmael, Bilal, Hodan, a mosque and a Somali village.

Ehel Academy is **not** a religious school. It respects both Christian and Islamic
families, so the cast carries no faith marking either way. Names come from
[`ehel-neutral-name-dictionary.md`](ehel-neutral-name-dictionary.md); this file
fixes who each one *is*.

## The rule

**One name = one person = one role, across every grade.**

The defect this prevents is real and was widespread before the rebuild: Amal was
the protagonist in nine units and a grandmother in a tenth; Adam was the older
brother in Units 6 and 9 and the younger brother in Unit 8; three different names
held the teacher role in a single grade; and a character called Samira changed
name to Isaac mid-paragraph in 126 passages across Grades 2–8.

A child reads a whole year of these passages in sequence. The cast has to hold.

## Per-unit story characters

The cast above is the **recurring** one — Amal and her circle, and the standing
community adults a child meets again and again across the year. They hold their
role for the whole course.

A single unit's story (a desert expedition, an ancient legend, a courtroom
drama) often needs one-off characters that never reappear. Those may take any
name from the neutral dictionary's secular list, subject to two rules only:

1. **Within the unit, one name = one person = one role** — the collision that
   keeps recurring in the shipped files (a hero renamed mid-story, a leader
   flipping between two names) is the thing to prevent.
2. **Never reuse a recurring-cast name for a one-off** — a story lawyer must not
   be "Amal" or "Doctor Sarah", because those names already belong to someone.

A one-off name may be reused for a *different* one-off role in another unit (a
reader does not expect the Unit 4 explorer to be the Unit 6 journalist), so
these are **not** pinned to a single role course-wide. Names seen in this use so
far: Zara (legend heroine, G5 U4), Amara / Tariq / Mama Dina (careers and
expedition roles, G6). Keep them internally consistent within their own unit.

## Do not use

Faith-marked, or burnt by a prior collision:

`Allah` · `Muhammad` · `Ishmael` · `Isaac` · `Ibrahim` · `Abraham` · `Yusuf` ·
`Musa` · `Aisha` · `Fatima` · `Bilal` · `Jesus` · `Maryam` · `Sagal` · `Amina` ·
`Hodan` · `Liban` · `Halima` · `Nasra` · `Idil` · `Abdi` · `Malik` ·
`Samira` · `Selma`

Samira and Selma are not faith-marked but were both used as the protagonist's
alias in the pre-rebuild content; reusing either re-opens the ambiguity.

Settings follow the same rule — no mosque, church, madrasa or Sunday school, no
Ramadan, Eid, Christmas or Easter, and no prayer as a plot beat. A market, a
school yard, a clinic, a river, a bus, a library all work and carry no marking.

## Introducing a new name

Don't, unless a genuinely new role appears. If one does: take it from the neutral
dictionary's secular list, check it appears **nowhere** in the grade first, and add
it here with its role before shipping.
