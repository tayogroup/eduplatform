Grade 4 - Level 4
Amal series, book 7 (Term 3, Unit 7: Emotions, Behaviour, and Identity)

1. The Day of the Play. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Our class was putting on a play, and Teacher Yasmin gave Sami the biggest part.
3. Sami is the shyest person I know. He went white when she read out his name.
4. "I cannot do it," he told me. "Everybody will look at me."
5. We practised in the yard, just the two of us, until he could say his lines to a tree.
6. Then to me. Then to Nora. Then to three people, which was harder than one.
7. "Nervous is not the same as unable," said Teacher Yasmin. "It only feels the same."
8. On the night, the hall was full. Behind the curtain Sami was terrified, and so was I.
9. The curtain opened.
10. He said every line. He said them well, and he said them loudly, and he did not stop once.
11. Afterwards he sat down very suddenly and laughed until he had to hold onto a chair.
12. Being brave is not feeling calm. It is doing the thing while you do not.
