A GARDEN BY THE METRE
Grade 3 - Level 3
Amal and Leo measure the new garden so every row comes out equal.

1. A Garden by the Metre. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. "Our plan must be correct," said Leo. "So we will measure everything."
3. The garden was four metres wide. We measured it twice to be sure.
4. Every seed row had to be equal: one metre, then one metre again.
5. The holes were more difficult. They must not be too deep.
6. Mina put a flat stone in the centre, to mark the middle.
7. We planted maize on one side and flowers on the other side.
8. The watering was simple: one full can for every row.
9. Leo copied the plan onto paper and ticked each finished row.
10. One row leaned to one side. We measured it again and fixed it.
11. Teacher Yasmin checked our work. "Correct," she said. "A garden by the metre."
12. Now the plants grow in equal rows. Our simple plan worked.
