THE TRAIN HOME
Grade 4 - Level 4
Four hours of window: the capital, the factories, the fields — and at the end of the line, home, which fits exactly.

1. The Train Home. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. The trip was over. Our class stood on the platform, tired and happy.
3. Teacher Yasmin counted us twice. Nobody was lost this time.
4. The train pulled out, and the capital slid slowly past the window.
5. First the tall buildings went by, then the factories, then the last lorries.
6. Then came the countryside: fields, fences and one slow, shining river.
7. The wheels sang the same song for a hundred kilometres.
8. "Goat!" called Amal, and everybody looked too late to see it.
9. The sun went down behind the hills, and the sky turned to gold.
10. At last our own small station rose up out of the dark.
11. Mum and Dad were waving under the lamp before the train even stopped.
12. "The capital was enormous," Amal told them. "But home fits exactly."
