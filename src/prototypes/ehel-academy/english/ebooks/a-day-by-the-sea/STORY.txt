A DAY BY THE SEA
Grade 1 - Level 1
After the big bus ride, Amal spends a day at the sea with Grandmother, watching boats float, planes fly and a fish jump.

1. A Day by the Sea. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Amal is at the sea with Grandmother.
3. A little blue boat floats by.
4. "A boat has sails," says Grandmother.
5. A little fish jumps. Splash!
6. A plane flies high above.
7. "Hello, plane!" waves Amal.
8. A helicopter goes by. It is loud!
9. Adam comes on his bicycle.
10. "Time to go home now," says Grandmother.
11. They go home on the big bus.
12. "What a lovely day!" says Amal.
