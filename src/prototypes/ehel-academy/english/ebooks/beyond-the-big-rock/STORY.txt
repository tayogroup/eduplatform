BEYOND THE BIG ROCK
Grade 3 - Level 3
A treasure hunt at the coast, where every clue is a where-word: beneath the stone, beyond the rock, within the flags.

1. Beyond the Big Rock. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. On Saturday, Teacher Yasmin took us to the coast for a treasure hunt.
3. "Every clue hides near the big rock," she said. "Read them carefully."
4. The first clue said: LOOK BENEATH THE FLAT STONE.
5. Beneath the stone lay a shell with a number two painted on it.
6. The second clue said: WALK BEYOND THE BIG ROCK.
7. Beyond the rock, the sand was covered with shells - shells throughout!
8. "Stay within the flags," called Teacher Yasmin, "and away from the waves."
9. Everyone found a clue except Sami. So Nora shared hers, and they read it together.
10. The last clue sent us back via the shore path, straight to the basket.
11. Within the basket: mangoes, and a small book for every one of us.
12. The best treasure of all? Words that tell you exactly where to look.
