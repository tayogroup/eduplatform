9. We bought sweet mangoes and ate them sitting beside a small garden.
10. At the college gate my brother Adam met us. "I study health and safety here," he said.
11. On the way back I saw a sign. "That is the border," said Teacher Yasmin. "One county ends, and another begins."
12. Back at school we wrote it all down. Our village is small. The county is big. Both of them are ours.
