DUKU PLAYS HIDE-AND-SEEK
Grade 1 - Level 1
The chicks hide behind, under and inside things on the farm, Kiki hides up in the tree, and Duku finds them all.

1. Duku Plays Hide-and-Seek. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. "Let's play hide-and-seek!" says Kiki.
3. Duku closes his eyes and counts.
4. One chick hides behind the haystack.
5. One chick hides under the bench.
6. One chick hides inside the barn.
7. Kiki hides up in the tall tree.
8. Duku looks near. Duku looks far.
9. He finds the chick behind the haystack!
10. He finds the chicks under and inside!
11. "Where is Kiki?" "Up here, above you!"
12. "Found you all!" laughs Duku.
