Letters & Sounds

By the end: Can read and write short regular words sound by sound, read the commonest irregular words, and read a simple sentence aloud.

Literacy12 lessonsalongside Starterabout 30 hours

Part 1 · First sounds and letters

1Sounds in words; holding a penYou will be able to hear the sounds in a word, and hold a pen to make lines and circles. 2s a t n i pYou will be able to read and write your first words. 3o g d m c kYou will be able to read and write words with six more letters. 4r e u h b l fYou will be able to read and write words with seven more letters.
Then: Part 1 check

Part 2 · All the letters

5ck ll ff ss · j v w x · y z quYou will be able to read every single letter of the alphabet. 6Letter names; spelling your nameYou will be able to say the 26 letter names and spell your name aloud. 7Two consonants togetherYou will be able to read words with two consonants side by side. 8ng nk th sh chYou will be able to read five pairs of letters that make one sound.
Then: Part 2 check

Part 3 · Long sounds and whole sentences

9Long vowels: ai ee ie oa ooYou will be able to read long vowel sounds written with two letters. 10ar or ir er ow oi ear air · ur aw ore ou oyYou will be able to read the vowel sounds that are neither short nor long. 11One sound, other spellingsYou will be able to read a sound that has more than one spelling. 12ph and wh, soft c and g; a whole sentenceYou will be able to read a whole sentence, including the common words phonics cannot sound out.
Then: Reading check

Bridge lessons

For learners who already read another language in these letters. They can take these two instead of the twelve lessons.

AEnglish letters, different soundsFor learners who already read another language written in Latin letters, such as Somali: letter names versus sounds, the five short vowels, and th, sh and ch. BOne sound, many spellingsai, a_e and ay; ee and ea; ie, i_e and igh; oa, o_e and ow.
