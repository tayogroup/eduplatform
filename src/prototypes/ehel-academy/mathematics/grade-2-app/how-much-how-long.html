How Much, How Long

1

What this lesson is about

By the end of this lesson you will be able to: Compare two lengths and say which is longer; measure with the same unit every time; measure a length in whole centimetres with a ruler; choose the right unit for what i am measuring; estimate the mass of an object before measuring it; read scales marked in twos, fives and tens.
