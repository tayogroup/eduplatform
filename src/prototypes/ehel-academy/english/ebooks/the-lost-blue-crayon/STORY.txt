1. The Lost Blue Crayon. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. At the tree school, Kiki had ten crayons.
3. Today she had nine. The blue one was gone.
4. "Do not be sad," said Miss Twiga. "We can all look."
5. The little elephant looked under the bench. She found a red flower.
6. The ostrich looked next to the board. He found a yellow seed.
7. Musa looked by the tree. He found a grey stone.
8. Kiki looked in her bag. She found a brown mango.
9. "Red, yellow, grey, brown," said Kiki. "But no blue."
10. Then the little elephant lifted her trunk. "Look up!"
11. The blue crayon was in the nest, high in the tree.
12. "Thank you, friends," said Kiki. "Ten again!"
