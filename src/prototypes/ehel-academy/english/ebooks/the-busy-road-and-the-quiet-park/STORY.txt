THE BUSY ROAD AND THE QUIET PARK
Grade 1 - Level 1
Grade 1 shelf - Fable series, book 5 (Term 3, Unit 9: City Places)

1. The Busy Road and the Quiet Park. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Lulu flew back to the big city to see Kiki.
3. The road was busy. Cars, buses, beep, beep!
4. "It is too loud here," said Lulu. "Where can we talk?"
5. Red means stop. The cars stopped. Kiki and Lulu waited.
6. Green means go. They crossed with all the other feet.
7. Past the big shop. Past the tall library.
8. And there it was: the park. Green, clean and quiet.
9. A little bird was singing. Now Lulu could hear it.
10. "The road is busy," said Kiki, "but the park is quiet."
11. Somebody had dropped a paper. Kiki put it in the bin.
12. "A clean park is the best park," said Lulu. And she sang too.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: busy, quiet, big, small, clean, dirty, road, shop, library, park, stop, go, litter.
Themes: a city has a quiet part in it if you know where to walk
Cameos: Lulu and Kiki from Lulu in the City, on the same streets.
