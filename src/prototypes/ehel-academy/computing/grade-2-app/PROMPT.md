# The prompt this build was made from (improved)

The request arrived as six words, after Grade 1 had been built, verified,
deployed and routed:

> now build grade 2 on the same kit

Below is the version that states what those words assume, what they leave
open, and the checks that decide whether it was done. It is the Grade 1
prompt (`../grade-1-app/PROMPT.md`) with the Stage 2 parts stated, and it is
written so it can be reused for Stage 3 by changing the named things.

---

## Build a Grade 2 Computing standalone lesson course for Ehel Academy, on the Grade 1 kit

### What to build

A **standalone lesson build** at `src/prototypes/ehel-academy/computing/grade-2-app/`,
generated by the kit Grade 1 was generated by (`../lesson-kit/`), wired by the
shared pipeline in `mathematics/lesson-app-tools/` and gated by its
`check-lessons.py` and the kit's `check-coverage.py`. One self-contained HTML
page per lesson plus a hub, the design and voice of the Grade 1 build
unchanged.

**"On the same kit" means the kit is extended, not forked, and Grade 1 is the
proof.** Stage 2 asks for things Stage 1 has no renderer for — repeat blocks,
several objects in one program, two bugs in one program, drawing from precise
instructions, a block graph, designing a survey, labelling hardware, racing a
computer — so `lib/computing.js`, `_rules.py`, `build-lessons.py`,
`build-hub.py` and `check-coverage.py` all grow. Every growth must leave
Grade 1 exactly as it was: rebuild `../grade-1-app` on the extended kit and
require its `LESSON` data blocks to be identical, page for page, before
building anything at Stage 2. A change that moves Grade 1's data is a change
to Grade 1, and Grade 1 is live.

**Design, style and UI: unchanged.** No new hue, no new file in the design
system; Stage 2 styles are added to `computing.css` under a marked heading.

### The content: Cambridge Primary Computing 0059, Stage 2, all of it

- The framework file exists (`src/curriculum/cambridge-computing-0059.json`,
  all six stages); Stage 2 has **31** objectives across the same five strands.
  Nothing under `computing/grade-2/` (the Word-pack course) is read or
  written.
- Organise by **strand**, ten lessons: precise instructions; bugs and
  predictions; programs and repeats; objects and plans; debugging together;
  Bee-Bot journeys; collecting data; presenting data; connected devices;
  hardware, software and robots.
- **Every step names the objective codes it exercises**; the builder refuses a
  code Stage 2 does not publish; the gate fails if any of the 31 is reached by
  no step. Behaviours, not labels: "precise" is an instruction the page can
  draw from (2CT.06), and a vague one draws the silly thing it actually says;
  "predict" is answered before Run (2CT.05, 2P.03); a repeat is a block that
  really repeats the next block (2P.02); the benefit of a partner is a hint
  that points at the block still wrong (2P.05); a survey method that would not
  work is chosen and seen not to work (2MD.03); the computer's advantage is a
  stopwatch, and the child's is the question after it (2CS.04).
- Language: British English, six-to-seven-year-old reading level, the learner
  addressed as "you". Home projects unplugged.

### The shapes that are the point (beyond Grade 1's)

- **Repeats (2P.02, 2P.03, 2P.06):** `repeat2`/`3`/`4` blocks repeat the NEXT
  block; the page and the gate compare EXPANDED programs, so `repeat3 jump`
  and `jump jump jump` are the same program except where the round says a
  repeat must be used.
- **Objects (2P.04):** two sprites on one stage; a round names the object its
  program is for.
- **Two bugs, and a partner (2P.05, 2P.07):** a round can carry two bugs, each
  with its own fix; an Ask button gives the partner's hint.
- **Precise drawing (2CT.06):** the one precise instruction draws the next part
  of the house or the boat; every wrong option draws what it literally says.
- **Data (2MD.02–06):** a survey is designed for a purpose (which ways would
  work?), a block graph is built column by column, and a table is read; every
  key computed from the data.
- **Hardware (2CS.01–03):** the parts of a drawn laptop and tablet, tapped by
  name; inputs and outputs; the race (2CS.04); the device for the place
  (2CS.05); story robots against working ones (2CS.06).

### Wiring and progress

- `app.config.json`: grade 2, stage 2, `courseKey` `ehel-comp-g02`,
  `progressUnitPrefix` `l`, `fromParam` `comp2`, hub `g2-index.html`, remote
  `Ehel Primary/app/computing/grade-2-v2`; `objectiveFloors` recorded at the
  measured value after the first green build.
- The same pipeline in the same order as Grade 1.
- Do NOT deploy Grade 2, do NOT redeploy Grade 1 (its pages rebuilt on the
  extended kit differ from the live ones by the inlined kit only), and do NOT
  route: all three are owner decisions to be asked for.

### Definition of done

1. Grade 1 rebuilt on the extended kit: pipeline and gates green, `LESSON`
   blocks identical to HEAD, 8 of 8.
2. `build-lessons.py` builds 10 pages with 0 refusals and reports 31/31;
   `check-lessons.py` and `check-coverage.py` exit 0.
3. Every inline classic script of both grades parses under `node --check`.
4. The gate is mutation-tested at Stage 2, pages restored byte-identical:
   a lesson losing an objective, an unpublished code, a race keyed to the
   wrong sum, a chart keyed against its columns, a `mustRepeat` round with no
   repeat, the second fix of a two-bug round wrong, a label part named twice,
   a precise round with two keys, a survey with no key, a lesson under its
   floor, a table key that disagrees with the rows.
5. Every step of every lesson of BOTH grades is driven to completion in a
   browser; dot rail, header and shelf agree; only the five platform-module
   404s in the console; no step overflows at 375px.
6. A README beside the build; the kit README's step-kind table updated.

### What is deliberately out of scope

Deploying or routing; recorded narration; a Scratch Jr embed; anything at
Stage 3; a human reading of the content.

---

### Why six words needed this

- "the same kit" is a constraint on the KIT, not a description of the content:
  Stage 2 cannot be authored in Stage 1's step kinds, so the kit has to grow,
  and the only thing that makes growing it safe is a proof that Grade 1 did
  not move. The prompt states the proof.
- "grade 2" is Stage 2 of 0059 — 31 objectives, three more than Stage 1 and
  a different emphasis (repeats, objects, partners, data collection design,
  hardware terminology) — so the lesson plan is stated rather than inherited.
- What was NOT said (deploy, route, redeploy Grade 1) is stated as not done,
  because the Grade 1 flow did each only on an explicit instruction and a
  rebuilt Grade 1 now differs from the live one.
