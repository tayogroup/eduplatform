2. One afternoon I was walking home from school, the same way I always walk.
3. Omar was outside his shop with a basket full of fruit and vegetables.
4. It looked much too heavy for one person. I stopped and watched him for a moment.
5. Then I remembered what Teacher Yasmin says about being kind to your neighbours.
6. "Can I offer some help?" I asked. Omar looked surprised, and grateful.

8. We lifted it together, and took small steps, so that nothing would fall out.
9. At the door he set it down and wiped his forehead.
10. "Thank you," he said. "You have a kind heart."
11. The feeling stayed with me the whole way home, and all evening as well.
12. Now I look for small ways to help, every single day. There are more of them than I thought.
