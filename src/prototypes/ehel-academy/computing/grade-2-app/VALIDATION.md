# Grade 2 Computing (computing/grade-2-v2) validation

Reviewed 2026-09-11 and revised twice the same day, after the remaining fixes and after the small items. The first two rounds were deployed the same day, and so were the small items (a43aee2ac); revised again on 2026-09-12 after the open items (6728927a0). Reviewer: Claude (automated measurement + judgement); no human teacher review yet.  
Against the 27-area framework in 'computing-grades validation.docx'. Scale: 1 Unsatisfactory; 2 Needs major improvement; 3 Acceptable with revisions; 4 Strong; 5 Excellent.  
What was reviewed: the standalone lesson build that ehel-comp-g02 launches (computing/grade-2-app, served as app/computing/grade-2-v2), not the Word-pack course under computing/grade-2, which the shell serves when no override applies and which declares a different framework.  

The Word report beside the owner's other validation reports (OneDrive: `computing-grade-2-v2 validation report.docx`) carries the same content; both are written from one script.

## Summary

The build teaches all 31 Cambridge Primary Computing 0059 Stage 2 objectives across eleven lessons. A check by hand of every objective against the steps that claim it found each one taught through something the child does, and nothing from Stage 3. After this review's three rounds of fixes no objective rests on a single activity, 72 of the 80 teaching steps can tell a child “not quite”, each of the 132 questions has one key and a reason, and every lesson was played to the end in a browser after the last change, with no errors and nothing wider than a phone screen.

The first round corrected eight kinds of error by reading every line, the widest spread being that data on a computer “does not get lost” or “cannot be spilt on”, and that telling jokes marks a robot as fictional, when the course's own smart speakers tell jokes. It also made a hot-water home project safe, stopped the voice reading “slash” aloud in six labels, replaced three emoji that older tablets cannot draw, and enlarged five tap targets that were under the 24 px minimum. Those fixes were deployed on 2026-09-11, and all ten lessons were then checked on the live pages: every step stored, every lesson recorded complete, and every reopened lesson opening where it was left.

The second round did the content work the first version listed. Four activities give the four thin objectives a second step each, and 2MD.03 is now met in full. Every lesson has a two-question warm-up, and lessons 2 to 10 open with a line recalling the lesson before. The toast, the cheese sandwich and two debug rounds that repeated Grade 1 almost word for word are replaced with new tasks. It was deployed on 2026-09-11 and all ten lessons were checked again on the live pages.

The third round did the small items. Lesson 10 had grown to 21 steps and about an hour; it is now two lessons, Hardware and Software (15 steps, 2CS.01 to 2CS.03) and Computers, Devices and Robots (14 steps, 2CS.04 to 2CS.06), each with its own quiz, lecture, words, home projects, recap and warm-up, and the thirteen activities unchanged. Where lesson 3 first shows the repeat block, it now says that other apps' repeat blocks hold several blocks and repeat them all. Lesson 9's wired-or-wireless sort and its offline test use their own items instead of Grade 1's. It was deployed on 2026-09-11, and all eleven lessons were then played to the end on the live pages: every step stored, every lesson recorded complete, and a reopened lesson opening where it was left.

What is left is work no measurement can do: a teacher reading the content and the question keys, a screen-reader walk-through, children watched and timed, and the school's own tablets. There are two owner decisions: whether a quiz score should gate completion, and whether to hide the “Computing world” placeholder.

Change status at 2026-09-12, after the open items: 15 items need no change or are done, 7 have their fix deployed with a human check or an owner decision still open, 5 are open and not started.

Average score 4.3 / 5 across 27 areas (4.1 in the first version, 4.3 in the second); lowest 3 (Multimedia Quality); 8 areas at 5.

## What was fixed during this review

Three commits on 2026-09-11, all three deployed the same day.

First round, `7cc8338cf` (deployed):

- Data on computers: “it does not get lost” is now “it is harder to lose”; “takes up no space at all” is now “fit on one tablet”; “cannot be spilt on” and “does not get wet” now say that the spill only reached the paper, and that a spare copy can be kept (lessons 7 and 8, seven places).
- Robots: a story robot is marked by feelings and finding things funny, not by telling jokes, which smart speakers do (lesson 10, seven places); robot vacuums are “simple” where their whole program is “forward until a bump” (three places); a delivery robot moves “turn by turn”, not “square by square”.
- A temperature graph going down “shows the fever is going down”, not “means the medicine is working”; a spinner that never stops “usually” means no network (three places, as in Grade 1); a tablet's home button lets you “always find your way back” rather than “never get lost”; “Who is a program's bug the fault of?” is now “A program goes wrong. Where is the bug?”.
- Safety: the “Tea for a robot” home project is acted out with an empty cup and no water.
- Speech: six sort labels written “repeat 3 times, jump / jump, jump, jump”, which the voice read with the word “slash”, are now “A: repeat 3 times, jump. B: jump, jump, jump.”.
- Emoji: toothbrush (2020, three uses) became a tooth, a potted plant (2020) a seedling, a heavy equals sign (2021, two uses) a plain “=”.
- Tap targets (shared kit): the laptop's speaker and charging port and the tablet's volume buttons, charging port and speaker grew from 17 to 23 px to 28 px or more on a 375 px screen, into empty space, with no ring reaching another. Only Grade 2 draws these figures.

Second round, the remaining fixes, `6f2f7c6f2` (deployed):

- Depth and alignment: four new activities, one for each objective that rested on a single step: “Which device way fits the job?” (lesson 7, 2MD.03: a tally app for cars at the gate, a form for five classes, a camera at the bird feeder, a picture form for children who cannot read yet), “Which way does the information go?” (lesson 10, 2CS.03, eight real situations), “Computer job, or people job?” (lesson 10, 2CS.04) and “Why that device?” (lesson 10, 2CS.05: a nurse, an architect, a taxi driver, a shop).
- Structure and assessment: a “Last time” recap on lessons 2 to 10 and a two-question warm-up on all ten, both carried by the overview, never marked.
- Overlap with Grade 1: “Only the steps you need: make toast” is now “take a photo”; “Follow it exactly: a cheese sandwich” is now “feed the cat”, on the kit's own cat-feeding scene; the debug rounds “jump twice, then say hello” and “move right twice, then jump” are now “grow, then jump twice” and “move left twice, then say hello”, with the quiz question that described the second one.

Third round, the small items, `a43aee2ac` (deployed):

- Lesson 10, 21 steps and about an hour, is two lessons: Hardware and Software (2CS.01 to 2CS.03, 15 steps) and Computers, Devices and Robots (2CS.04 to 2CS.06, 14 steps). The thirteen activities are unchanged and in the same order. Each lesson has its own quiz of eight (eight questions carried over from the old quiz, eight new), about, lecture, words (touchscreen, accurate, sensor and fiction are new), home projects (an in-and-out hunt, and spotting a story robot in a film or a book, are new), recap and warm-up. - Lesson 9: the wired-or-wireless sort's printer on a cable, smart watch and laptop on the sofa are now a hospital wired through its walls, a café's wi-fi and a library's wi-fi; the offline test's six apps, five of them near-copies of Grade 1's, are new. ## Verdict on Grade 2 Computing

Nothing found stops the children already using Grade 2. The first round's corrections are live and the progress fix is confirmed on all ten live lessons. The second round gives every objective at least two activities, meets 2MD.03 in full, adds a recap and a warm-up, and removes the repeats of Grade 1; it is live. The third round splits the longest lesson in two and closes the small items; it is live too, checked on all eleven live lessons.

Before it is called validated rather than measured, a teacher should read the content and the question keys, and somebody should watch a child use a lesson and walk one with a screen reader.
