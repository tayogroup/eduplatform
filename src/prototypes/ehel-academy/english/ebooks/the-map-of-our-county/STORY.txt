THE MAP OF OUR COUNTY
Grade 3 - Level 3
The day after the bus trip, the class draws everything they saw: the hospital, the court, the market - and the border.

1. The Map of Our County. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. The day after the trip, Teacher Yasmin unrolled a big sheet of paper.
3. "We saw our county from the bus. Now let us draw it."
4. Sami drew the hospital, with Doctor Sarah waving at the door.
5. Nora drew the court, with its five white columns.
6. I drew the market, with Omar and his baskets. I drew the mangoes very carefully.
7. Adam's college went next to the market, and then the long road.
8. At the edge, Teacher Yasmin wrote BORDER, right where the sign stands.
9. "Something is missing," said Maya. And she drew the school bus, with Nadia driving.
10. We hung the map on the wall, beside the window.
11. "Now we know our county by heart," said Teacher Yasmin, "and our address too."
12. When I grow up, I will visit every place on that map. Maybe beyond it.
