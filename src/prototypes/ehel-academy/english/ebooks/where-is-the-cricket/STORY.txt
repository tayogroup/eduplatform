1. Where Is the Cricket? Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Something was chirping in the garden. Zuri could hear it. She could not see it.
3. Under the flat stone? No. That is an ant, and an ant does not chirp.
4. On the leaf? No. That is a butterfly, and a butterfly is very quiet.
5. In the flower? No. That is a bee. A bee hums. It is not the same sound at all.
6. Between the tall grass? No. That is a worm, and a worm has no legs to rub together.
7. Above, in the web? No! And that one has eight legs, so it is not an insect either.
8. In front of the log there was nothing at all. Zuri sat down. The chirping stopped.
9. So she waited, and kept still. Chirp. Chirp. It was BEHIND the log.
10. Six legs. Two long back ones, for jumping. And two wings, for chirping.
11. Zuri did not catch it. She sat very still and listened to it instead.
12. Now, every evening, Zuri knows exactly where to look. Behind things.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: on, under, in, between, above, in front of, behind, cricket, ant, butterfly, bee, worm, spider, chirp, listen.
Themes: a search is a list of places; watching beats catching
