THE SOUVENIR THAT DISAPPEARED
Grade 4 - Level 4
Nora's brass key ring vanishes from her desk — and thinking it through leads the class to a very unusual thief.

1. The Souvenir That Disappeared. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Nora brought her souvenir to school: a small brass key ring from the capital.
3. By break time it had disappeared from her desk.
4. "Think it through," said Amal. "Every mystery has a reason."
5. They wrote down every detail: the desk, the open window, the empty bag.
6. Their first guess was incorrect. The key ring was not in the lost-property box.
7. Sami wondered about the window. "Something unusual flew past it," he said.
8. On the sill lay one small grey feather.
9. In the yard, high in the acacia, something glittered inside a nest.
10. The caretaker brought his ladder and lifted the key ring down gently.
11. "Birds love bright things," he said. "That is the whole reason."
12. Nora rewrote the ending of her trip report: souvenirs can travel too.
