How Much, How Long

Warm up

1

Longer and shorter

They all start in the same place, so look at where each one ends.

2

Measure with cubes

Lay cubes along it and count them. Every cube must be the same size.

3

Reading a ruler

Start at 0. Read the number where it ends. How many centimetres?

4

Which unit?

Pick the unit that makes sense.

5

Balance the scales

The balance tips down on the heavier side. Add weights until it is level.

6

Reading a kitchen scale

Each mark is 100 g. What does the needle say?

7

Reading a jug

Each mark is 100 ml. Pour, then read the level.

8

Which holds more?

Fill them with cups of water and count. The one that takes more cups holds more.

9

Estimate, then measure

Look at the ribbon. Guess how long it is, then the ruler will measure it.

10

Draw a line yourself

Tap the ruler where your line should end. Start is already at 0.

11

Between the marks

A scale is a number line. Not every mark has a number. What is the arrow pointing to?
