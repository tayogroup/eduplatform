# Grade 1 Global Perspectives (global-perspectives/grade-1-v2) validation

Reviewed 2026-09-11. Reviewer: Claude (automated measurement, an independent line-by-line content read, and judgement); no human teacher review yet. Scale: 1 Unsatisfactory; 2 Needs major improvement; 3 Acceptable with revisions; 4 Strong; 5 Excellent.

Against the 27-area framework in 'global-prespectives-grades validation.docx'. Every figure below was measured on the built or live pages during this review, not recalled. The Word copy of this report sits beside that file in OneDrive; this copy lives with the build and is written from the same rows.

## Summary

The build reaches all 18 Cambridge Primary Global Perspectives 0838 Stage 1 objectives, every quiz key is single and explained, and every lesson is driven to completion in a browser. The review found two things a learner or a school would feel. First, the platform never heard about two self-ticking steps in every lesson, so a lesson finished to the last sticker was never recorded as complete, and a reopened lesson lost the child's place. Second, a line-by-line read found 21 content problems, among them a step that marked 'I am telling the teacher' as the wrong thing to do, family questions that had a five-year-old tap 'my mum, my dad and my brother' as the right answer about their own family, question builders that rejected real questions as 'not a question that works', and a vet recommending carrots for rabbits.

All of it is fixed in the build: 68 content edits across the eight lessons and two small changes to the shared Global Perspectives kit, verified in the browser with the real platform modules, both gates green on all four grades, and every lesson of all four grades driven to 100% again. It was committed (cb5cd7dbd) and Grades 1 to 4 were redeployed the same day, so the live pages now report completion and carry the fixed content.

A follow-up the same day, from the review of Grades 2 to 4, added these fixes to Grade 1. The opinion step praised any reason about the topic, whichever way it argued ('I do not like fruit much, because it is sweet and juicy' was accepted); Lesson 5's nine reasons now say which opinions they support, the page refuses the other kind and says why, and the builder and gate refuse an opinion without enough supporting reasons. Lesson 2 called three of six children 'most of the class'; it now says 'more children walk than come any other way'. Grade 1's pages also carry the kit's fix for spoken lines that lower-cased 'I' or a name. On the owner's instruction to use judgement on the open items, Lesson 1's own pet is now a cat rather than a dog, and every emoji that needs a 2020-era device was replaced with a long-supported one. These follow-ups are in the working tree, not yet committed or deployed.

Average score **4.1 / 5** across 27 areas; lowest 3 (Assessment Balance). Two High-priority findings, both fixed and live since commit cb5cd7dbd.

Change status at 2026-09-11: 14 items need no change or are done in the build, 5 have fixes built with a human or device check still open, 8 are open and not started. The first-pass fixes are live since commit cb5cd7dbd; the follow-ups (opinions, 'most', the cat, the emoji) are in the working tree, not yet committed or deployed.

## Required changes, by priority

- **High.** Progress and completion (area 22) and question quality (area 10): both fixed, committed as cb5cd7dbd and live on Grades 1 to 4. The follow-up to area 10 (opinions that accepted counter-arguments) is in the build and reaches learners when it is committed and redeployed, which is the owner's decision.
- **Medium.** A teacher's read of the eight content modules and their keys (areas 1, 4, 10, 19); a screen-reader walk-through and a SEN view of how listening is pictured (15); two lessons on the school's own devices (21); watching two or three children use it (13, 27); a cross-subject decision on self-hosting the fonts (20).
- **Low.** A second application step for 1As.01 and 1Ea.01 (3); a one-frame recap of the previous lesson (9); a diagnostic opener (11); timing two children to calibrate the minutes (17).

## What was fixed during this review

Every change below is in the working tree and verified: both gates green on Grades 1 to 4, every inline script parsing, the mutation harness passing, and every lesson of all four grades driven to 100%. The first seven are live since commit cb5cd7dbd; the follow-ups are in the working tree, not yet committed or deployed.

- **Progress (kit).** The overview ticks when the learner moves on from it and Our world on arrival, never while the page draws, so both are reported. Verified with the real platform modules: every finished step recorded, a reopened lesson opens where the child left off.
- **Question builder (kit and content).** Lessons 1 and 7 accept every real question an ending makes; a real but unwanted question is told what its word asks for ('Where asks for a place').
- **Safeguarding.** 'I am telling the teacher' is no longer a wrong answer (Lesson 6, two places).
- **Family questions.** Lesson 7's family answers are Omar's, asked by his classmates, not the child's own.
- **Facts.** Rabbits eat mostly hay and grass; bread's question is how it is made; fruit comes from a farm near our town; an opinion is what you think and others may think differently; sharing is not a trade; a tap that still drips needs a grown-up; water 'more left for everything else'; no winter for the squirrel.
- **Explanations and keys.** Telling says something; Lesson 3's wrong reasons are no longer presented as true; the milk item's question matches its answer; Lesson 8's listening key agrees with Lesson 7.
- **Words and pictures.** 'A kind of soup' replaces the homophone 'a kind of sauce'; Lesson 5's duplicated source question replaced; a drum replaces a music CD; the wheel and nest emoji replaced with long-supported ones; two agreement and two punctuation errors.
- **Opinions (kit, follow-up).** Lesson 5's reasons say which opinions they support; a reason that argues the other way is refused, and the builder and gate refuse an opinion without enough supporting reasons.
- **'Most' (follow-up).** Three of six is half, so Lesson 2 now says more children walk than come any other way.
- **Decisions on the owner's instruction.** Lesson 1's own pet is a cat; every 2020-era emoji replaced (look-back rewind in every grade, a tooth, a bowl, a no-entry sign); the four grades stay the same length.

## Beyond Grade 1

- **Grades 2 to 4.** Validated the same day, each with its own report. The too-narrow question-builder word lists noted in the first version of this report are fixed there, along with the kit changes listed above, which are in all four grades' rebuilt pages.
- **Other subjects.** The Computing and Art & Design kits tick their overview and Our world while the page draws, exactly as this kit did, and Science ticks its overview on arrival at load, which races the same way. Each will have the same missing-completion and lost-resume defect; each belongs to its own session.

## Verdict

Grade 1 is sound and well aligned, and with this review's fixes, now live, it is strong in almost every area. The two follow-up fixes should go out with Grades 2 to 4's validation fixes in one commit and one redeploy. Then schedule the teacher read, the screen-reader session and the device check; none of those blocks a pilot with a teacher in the room.
