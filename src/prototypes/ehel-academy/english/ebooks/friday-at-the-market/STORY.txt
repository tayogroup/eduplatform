FRIDAY AT THE MARKET
Grade 3 - Level 3
Amal series, book 4 of 4, from the listening text "At the Market" (Term 2, Unit 4: Places and Community)

1. Friday at the Market. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. "Where are we going this morning?" "To the market. On Friday my mother always goes."
3. "Is the market busy on Fridays?" "Very busy. Look at all the people between the stalls."
4. "What do you usually buy?" "Bananas and rice. Tomatoes and mangoes too, if they look fresh."
5. "I like markets," said Sami, "because they are noisy and full of life."
6. "I like the colours," I said. "Yellow bananas, red tomatoes, green vegetables everywhere."
7. "Do you ever get lost in such a big crowd?" he asked.
8. "Not really. I hold my mother's hand, and I stay close beside her."
9. "Meet us by the gate at eight o'clock," I said, "and bring a basket for the rice."
10. So the next Friday Sami came with the biggest basket in his house.
11. We filled it. Then we could hardly carry it, and we laughed the whole way home.
12. A market is a crowd if you have no plan. With a plan, it is just a Friday morning.

Unit vocabulary reinforced: market, garden, exit, village, county, address, doctor, officer.
Themes: a crowd is only a crowd if you have no plan
Cameos: the eight o'clock meeting at the gate and the basket for the rice are the last lines of the Unit 4 dialogue.
