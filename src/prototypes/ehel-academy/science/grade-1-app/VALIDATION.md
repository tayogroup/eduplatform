# Grade 1 Science (science/grade-1-v2) validation

Reviewed 2026-09-10. Reviewer: Claude (automated measurement + judgement); no human teacher review yet. Scale: 1 Unsatisfactory; 2 Needs major improvement; 3 Acceptable with revisions; 4 Strong; 5 Excellent.

Against the 27-area framework in *science-grade-1-v2 validation.docx*. The Word copy of this report sits beside that file in OneDrive; this copy is the one that lives with the build and is regenerated from the same rows.

## Summary

The build reaches all 35 Cambridge Primary Science 0097 Stage 1 objectives, every quiz key is single and explained, every lesson was driven to completion in a browser, and the fixes made during this review (four factual softenings, a phone-width overflow, keyboard access to the figures, accessible names on every simulation, learning-time estimates, and a teacher-and-parent section with objectives, at-home experiments and answer keys) are built and gated. Two things stand between this and a formal sign-off: a human teacher has not read the content, and a reopened lesson does not resume at the learner's last step, which is a shared-pipeline behaviour affecting the Mathematics and English builds equally. Nothing found blocks starting Grade 2; the resume gap should be fixed in the shared tool before Grade 2 ships.

Average score **4.1 / 5** across 27 areas; lowest 3 (Balance Across Grades, Assessment Balance, Multimedia Quality, Learner Progress and Completion); one High-priority required change, in the shared pipeline rather than in this build.

Change status at 2026-09-10: **11** items need no change or are done, **7** have their fixes deployed with a human check still open, **9** are open and not started.

## Required changes, by priority

- **High.** Resume on reopen (area 22): `mathematics/lesson-app-tools/wire-progress.py` should mark stored sections done and open at the resume step after hydrate. One change to the shared tool; it lifts all three standalone builds, and each needs a re-release. Not started.
- **Medium.** A teacher's read of the eight content modules and 90 quiz keys (areas 1, 4, 10, 19); a screen-reader walk-through (15); two lessons opened on the school's own devices, checking the six Emoji-13 glyphs (18, 21); watching two or three children use it (13, 27). None started.
- **Low.** A second step for 1Cp.01 (3); a one-frame recap of the previous lesson (9); a short diagnostic opener (11); timing two children to calibrate the minutes (17); re-scoring areas 7 and 8 once Grade 2 exists.

## What was fixed during this review (all deployed 2026-09-10, commit c4afe801d)

- Magnet test: 'copper coin' became kitchen foil, because some real coins are steel and stick.
- Material tester: the squashable plastic bottle and paper are out; a drinking glass and a toy brick are in.
- 'Many people thought the Earth was flat' became 'some'; sun cream 'protects your skin'.
- A duplicated quiz stem in lesson 4 replaced; the yo-yo standing in for a spinning top replaced; two emoji needing 2021/2022 support replaced.
- A 131 px horizontal overflow on the six-bin sort at phone width (the stage item sized to its content's max width).
- Figure parts are keyboard buttons with names; every simulation drawing has an accessible name.
- Learning-time estimates on every card; a printable teachers-and-parents section with objectives, at-home experiments and answer keys.

## Verdict on moving to Grade 2

Nothing found blocks starting Grade 2. Build Grade 2 on the same generator and gates; fix the resume behaviour in the shared tool before Grade 2 ships, so both grades get it; schedule the teacher read of Grade 1 in parallel rather than as a gate on Grade 2 work.
