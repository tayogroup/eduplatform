[TITLE] Marking Out and Cutting a Halving Joint
        Calaamadaynta iyo Goynta Isku-xidhka Nus-nus
EN  Two pieces of timber have to cross, and finish flush. This is how that is done.
SO  Laba xabbo oo alwaax ah waa inay isku tallaabaan, dhammaadkuna siman yahay. Sidan ayaa loo sameeyaa.

[JOINT] What a halving joint is
        Waxa uu yahay isku-xidhka nus-nus
EN  A halving joint. Each member loses exactly half its thickness where they meet.
SO  Isku-xidhka nus-nus. Xubin walba waxay ku lumisaa si sax ah nus dhumucdeeda meesha ay isku yimaadaan.

EN  Take half off one, half off the other, and the two faces come together level. That is the whole idea, and everything else is accuracy.
SO  Nus ka jar tan, nus ka jar tii kale, labada wejina way isku imanayaan iyagoo siman. Taasi waa fikradda oo dhan; waxa intaas ka hadhayna waa saxsanaan.

[TOOLS] Three tools
        Saddex qalab
EN  Three tools do this job. A try square, to prove a line square.
SO  Saddex qalab ayaa shaqadan qabta. Xagal-qiyaase, si loo caddeeyo in xariiqdu xagal qumman tahay.

EN  A marking gauge, to score the same distance from a face every time.
SO  Astaan-qiyaase, si mar walba isla masaafadaas looga xoqo wejiga.

EN  And a tenon saw: fine teeth for a clean cut across the grain, and a stiff back that keeps the cut straight.
SO  Iyo miinshaar ilko-yar: ilko fiiqan oo si nadiif ah u gooya dunta alwaaxa, iyo dhabar adag oo goynta toosan ka dhiga.

[FACES] Face side, face edge
        Wejiga aasaasiga ah, cidhifka aasaasiga ah
EN  Start with a prepared piece. Planed straight, planed square, gauged to size.
SO  Ka bilow xabbad horay loo diyaariyay. Si toosan loo randeeyay, xagal qumman loo randeeyay, cabbirna loo qiyaasay.

EN  One wide face is marked as the face side. One edge, square to it, is marked as the face edge.
SO  Hal weji oo ballaadhan waxaa loo calaamadeeyaa wejiga aasaasiga ah. Hal cidhif, oo xagal qumman kula jira, waxaa loo calaamadeeyaa cidhifka aasaasiga ah.

EN  Every measurement from here comes off those two surfaces. Not off a sawn end, and not off whichever side is nearest.
SO  Cabbir kasta halkan ka dib wuxuu ka imanayaa labadaas dusha. Kama imanayo dhammaad la gooyay, kamana imanayo dhinaca u dhow ee soo gacan-gala.

[SHOULDER] The shoulder line
           Xariiqda garabka
EN  Take the width from the piece that has to fit, not from the drawing.
SO  Ballaca ka qaado xabbadda lagu lidhi doono, ee ha ka qaadan sawirka.

EN  Square the shoulder line across the face, and down both edges. The stock of the square presses hard against the face edge.
SO  Xariiqda garabka xagal qumman uga jiid wejiga, kuna soo dejiso labada cidhifba. Gacanta xagal-qiyaasaha si adag ayay ugu cadaadinaysaa cidhifka aasaasiga ah.

EN  If the stock lifts even slightly, the line is not square, and neither is anything you cut to it.
SO  Haddii gacantu xitaa wax yar kor u kacdo, xariiqdu xagal qumman ma aha, waxa aad ku gooysaana sidoo kale ma aha.

[GAUGE] Half the thickness
        Nus dhumucda
EN  Set the gauge to half the thickness. Lock it, then measure it again before it touches the wood.
SO  Astaan-qiyaasaha ku habee nus dhumucda. Xidh, kadibna mar kale cabbir ka hor inta uusan alwaaxa taaban.

EN  Score the cheek line with the fence riding on the face side, on both members, from the one setting.
SO  Xariiqda dhabanka ka xoq adigoo darbiga ku wadaya wejiga aasaasiga ah, labada xubinba, isla hal habayn.

EN  One setting used twice keeps the halves equal. A gauge a millimetre out puts the joint two millimetres out, because the error lands on both.
SO  Hal habayn oo laba jeer la isticmaalo ayaa nusasyada siman ka dhigaysa. Astaan-qiyaase hal milimitir qaldan wuxuu isku-xidhka ka dhigayaa laba milimitir qaldan, maxaa yeelay khaladku labadaba wuu ku dhacayaa.

[WASTE] Mark the waste
        Calaamadee qashinka
EN  Now hatch the waste. This is the last mark before the first cut that cannot be undone.
SO  Hadda qashinka xariiqo ku calaamadee. Tani waa calaamadda ugu dambaysa ka hor goynta koowaad ee aan dib loo celin karin.

EN  It takes seconds, and it is why nobody who does it ever saws off the wrong half.
SO  Waxay qaadanaysaa ilbiriqsiyo, waana sababta qofkii sameeya uusan weligiis u gooyn nuska khaldan.

[SAW] Which side of the line
      Xariiqda dhinaceeda kee
EN  The saw takes out a kerf about a millimetre wide. That millimetre has to come out of the waste.
SO  Miinshaarku wuxuu saaraa jeex qiyaastii hal milimitir ballac ah. Milimitirkaas waa inuu ka baxo qashinka.

EN  Saw on the waste side and the line stays on the work. The joint is full size, and a shaving brings it home.
SO  Ka gooy dhinaca qashinka, xariiqduna shaqada way ku hadhaysaa. Isku-xidhku wuxuu noqonayaa cabbir buuxa, xoqid yarna way dhammaystiraysaa.

EN  Saw down the middle of the line and half the kerf comes out of the joint. It is slack, and nothing puts wood back.
SO  Ka gooy xariiqda badhtankeeda, nus jeexana isku-xidhka ayuu ka baxayaa. Wuu dabacsanaanayaa, waxbana alwaax dib uma celin karaan.

[FIT] Pare, and fit it dry
      Xoq, kadibna tijaabo engegan
EN  Pare the waste with a chisel, cutting from both faces towards the middle, so the far edge is supported and does not tear out.
SO  Qashinka izmiil ku xoq, adigoo labada wejiba ka gooyaya oo badhtanka u socda, si cidhifka fog loo taageero oo aanu u jabin.

EN  Then fit it dry. If it needs a mallet it is too tight: take a shaving off the cheek and try it again.
SO  Kadibna tijaabo engegan ku sameey. Haddii uu dubbe qori ah u baahdo, aad buu u cidhiidhi yahay: xoqid ka qaad dhabanka, mar kalena tijaabi.

EN  It should go together by hand and stay there. Check both diagonals before any glue.
SO  Waa inuu gacanta ku wada galaa oo halkaas ku hadhaa. Hubi labada xariiq ee geesaha isku xidha ka hor inta aan xabag la marin.

[RECAP] The order of work
        Habka shaqada
EN  Face side and face edge. Shoulder squared, cheek gauged, waste hatched.
SO  Wejiga aasaasiga ah iyo cidhifka aasaasiga ah. Garabka xagal qumman loo jiiday, dhabanka la qiyaasay, qashinka la xariiqay.

EN  Sawn on the waste side, pared to the line, fitted dry. In that order, every time.
SO  Dhinaca qashinka laga gooyay, xariiqda la gaadhsiiyay, tijaabo engegan lagu sameeyay. Habkaas, mar walba.


================================================================================
GLOSSARY - which of these are settled words, and which are mine
================================================================================

The tools and the timber have ordinary Somali names. The JOINT ANATOMY mostly
does not, because the vocabulary of English bench joinery has no settled
Somali equivalent in print. Where that is so, the translation below either
extends an existing body-part metaphor - which is what English itself does:
shoulder, cheek, face - or builds a compound. Those are marked.

  SETTLED - ordinary Somali, safe to use
  ------------------------------------------------------------------
  alwaax ................. timber, sawn board
  qori ................... wood
  miinshaar .............. saw
  ilko ................... teeth
  randa / randee ......... plane (tool) / to plane
  izmiil ................. chisel
  dubbe .................. hammer; dubbe qori ah = mallet
  miiska shaqada ......... workbench
  xabag .................. glue
  dhumuc ................. thickness
  ballac ................. width
  siman .................. level, flush
  fiiqan ................. sharp
  af beelay .............. blunt ("has lost its edge")
  qashin ................. waste
  suul ................... thumb
  cidhif ................. edge
  weji ................... face
  garab .................. shoulder
  dhaban ................. cheek

  MY CONSTRUCTIONS - a carpenter must confirm or replace these
  ------------------------------------------------------------------
  isku-xidhka nus-nus ....... halving joint. Literally "half-half joint".
  xagal-qiyaase ............. try square. "Angle-measurer".
  astaan-qiyaase ............ marking gauge. "Mark-measurer".
  miinshaar ilko-yar ........ tenon saw. "Small-toothed saw".
  miinshaarka dunta-raaca ... rip saw. "The saw that follows the grain".
  dunta alwaaxa ............. the grain. "The threads of the timber".
  wejiga aasaasiga ah ....... face side. "The base face".
  cidhifka aasaasiga ah ..... face edge. "The base edge".
  xariiqda garabka .......... shoulder line. Extends the body metaphor, as EN does.
  xariiqda dhabanka ......... cheek line. Same.
  jeex ...................... kerf. "Slit". There is no Somali word for the cut
                              a saw takes out as distinct from the cut itself.
  gacanta ................... the stock of the try square.
  darbiga ................... the fence of the gauge. "The wall".
  tijaabo engegan ........... dry fit. "A dry trial".
  xariiqaha geesaha isku xidha ... the diagonals.

WHAT THIS IS NOT
================================================================================

1. NOT REVIEWED BY A SOMALI-SPEAKING CARPENTER. This is the same caveat the
   English carries, and it bites harder here. The English at least uses trade
   words a tradesperson would recognise on sight. Half the joint anatomy above
   is a term I built. A wrong word for "kerf" or "face edge" teaches the wrong
   thing in a way a learner cannot detect and an assessor will.

2. NOT NARRATED. Nothing has been bought. A Somali film would need a Somali
   voice, and the tool's voice is fixed to one English speaker - that is a
   separate decision with its own cost, not a re-render.

3. NOT WIRED INTO ANYTHING. No page reads this file. It sits beside SCRIPT.txt
   as the source a Somali edition would be built from.

4. REGISTER KEPT. The English is a tradesperson explaining a job to an adult at
   grade 8-11 level, not a teacher addressing a child. The Somali keeps that:
   imperatives, short sentences, nothing softened.
