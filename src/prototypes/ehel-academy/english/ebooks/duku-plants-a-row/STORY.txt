DUKU PLANTS A ROW
Grade 1 - Level 1
Grade 1 shelf - Fable series, book 5 (Term 2, Unit 5: On the Farm)

1. Duku Plants a Row. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Duku the donkey had a bowl of seeds.
3. First he dug a row in the field.
4. Then he put one seed in each hole.
5. Koko the hen watched. "Is that all?" she asked.
6. "No," said Duku. "Now they need water. And they need days."
7. It rained. Duku waited.
8. The sun came out. Duku waited.
9. One morning, little green leaves came up!
10. The goat wanted to eat them. "Not yet!" said Duku.
11. They grew and grew. Carrots, tomatoes and beans.
12. "First the seed, then the food," said Duku. "Thank you, rain!"

Unit vocabulary reinforced: seed, field, planting, growing, rain, sun, wait, carrot, tomato, beans, first, then.
Themes: the part of growing food that is only waiting
Cameos: Duku from Duku Makes a Scarecrow, with Koko and the goat; the row he digs is the one the scarecrow guards.
