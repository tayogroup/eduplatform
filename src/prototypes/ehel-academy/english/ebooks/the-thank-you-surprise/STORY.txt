THE THANK-YOU SURPRISE
Grade 4 - Level 4
A discussion, a division, one good decision — and the look on Teacher Yasmin's face when the cloth comes off.

1. The Thank-You Surprise. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. The class wanted to thank Teacher Yasmin for the play. But how?
3. The discussion took all of break time. Everybody had a different idea.
4. Then came division: half the class wanted a card, half wanted a cake.
5. "Both," said Nora. That was the decision, and it felt right at once.
6. At home, Mum and Grandma Hana helped with the mixing and the baking.
7. There was some confusion about the sugar, so they measured it twice.
8. Nora drew the card. Amal wrote one kind sentence from every child inside.
9. They hid everything under a cloth on the quietest desk in the classroom.
10. On Friday, Teacher Yasmin lifted the cloth. The whole class watched her face.
11. Her expression went from surprise to the widest smile we had ever seen.
12. "The best decisions," she said, "are the ones you make together."
