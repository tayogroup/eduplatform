THE FAIR AFTER THE STORM
Grade 4 - Level 4
The morning after the storm, the class dries out the science tent and holds the fair the weather tried to cancel.

1. The Fair After the Storm. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. The storm had gone, but the field was a mess. Puddles shone everywhere.
3. "A fair can survive a storm," said Teacher Yasmin. "We will try again tomorrow."
4. Everyone helped. Amal wiped down the tent, and Nora rescued the posters.
5. Sami and Adam propped the poles straight and taped everything down twice.
6. By evening the tent stood tall again, drying in the warm wind.
7. In the morning the sky was a clean, washed blue.
8. Visitors came from all over town. Nora explained her weather project twice.
9. Amal showed how air holds water you cannot see. "The storm showed us first!" she laughed.
10. Then a rainbow appeared over the field, as if the sky wanted to join in.
11. "Preparation, and a little luck," said Teacher Yasmin, delighted.
12. That night Amal wrote in her notebook: the best demonstrations survive the weather.
