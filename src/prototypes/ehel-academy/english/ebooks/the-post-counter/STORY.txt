THE POST COUNTER
Grade 4 - Level 4
Amal series, book 1 (Term 1, Unit 1: Daily Life & Communication)

1. The Post Counter. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Omar runs the market shop, and the town post counter stands beside his till.
3. Every morning he sorts the mail, weighs the parcels, and checks each address twice. A small mistake could send a letter to the wrong street.
4. He reads letters aloud for neighbours who find long words difficult. Grandmother Salma listens for news of a cousin far away.
5. "Not everyone owns a phone," Omar says. "A letter can bring peace, and make somebody feel remembered."
6. I came to interview him for the school paper. Maya lent me her list of questions.
7. "How do you keep it fair?" I asked. "First come, first served," he said. "Every parcel matters to somebody."
8. At home I wrote up my notes. Mum said a good interview is mostly listening.
9. Nora read my draft and said the middle was slow. So I cut it, clearly and without arguing.
10. On busy days Omar simply works a little longer, instead of leaving a job half finished.
11. Teacher Yasmin read my report to the class. "Daily effort," she said. "That is the whole story."
12. I posted a copy to my cousin. It is a small thing to send a letter - and a large thing to get one.

Themes: unglamorous daily effort; fairness as first come, first served
