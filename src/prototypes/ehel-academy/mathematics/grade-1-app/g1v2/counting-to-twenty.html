Odd and even

Tap a number. If every counter has a partner, the number is even. If one is left alone, it is odd. Sort 8 numbers.

More or fewer?

Put them in order

Numbers to 20

Two numbers. Which one is bigger? Tap it. The number line will help you.

13

Compare to 20

Two pictures. Does the first one have more, fewer, or the same? Look at the tens first, then the ones.

14

First, second, third

Ten runners in a race. The one nearest the flag is first. Press Next runner and say the places with me.

15

Guess, then count

Don't count yet! How many stars do you think there are? Tap your guess.

Estimate to 20

How many apples? Slide your guess on the number line and lock it. Then tap each apple to count it into the frames.
