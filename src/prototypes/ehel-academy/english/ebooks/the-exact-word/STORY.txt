THE EXACT WORD
Grade 4 - Level 4
Busy is not good enough for Maya's article — so she and Amal walk the town hunting the word that fits each place exactly.

1. The Exact Word. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. Maya was writing an article about our town for the school paper.
3. "Busy is not the exact word," she sighed. "Our town deserves better words."
4. At the market they watched the crowded stalls. "Crowded," wrote Maya. "Exactly right."
5. Down by the lake, the morning was quiet and the air was fresh.
6. The old acacia was enormous. No other tree came close.
7. "My fruit is local," said Omar. "It grows one field away from this stall."
8. The bakery was popular with everybody, especially Sami.
9. At the clinic, Doctor Sarah chose careful words. "Words must be exact here too."
10. Maya read her list to the class: crowded, quiet, enormous, fresh, local, popular.
11. "Excellent," said Teacher Yasmin. "The exact word is a powerful thing."
12. The article went up on the wall, and every single word was the right one.
