THE SECOND SHOW
Grade 3 - Level 3
The play went so well that the families want to see it too. The drama club gets ready to do it all again.

1. The Second Show. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. The morning after the play, Teacher Yasmin had news. "The families want to see it too!"
3. So the drama club got ready for a second show, on Friday.
4. Nora practised her part again. This time I did not need to teach her.
5. Mina asked, "Can I be in the play?" "You can clap," I said, "in all the right places."
6. We pressed the yellow vest, and I made a brand-new paper hat.
7. On Friday the hall was full. Mum, Dad and Grandma Hana sat in the front row.
8. My hands kept still this time. I knew every word of my part.
9. Nora spoke her lines slowly and clearly, and nobody felt lost.
10. At the end, Mina clapped in exactly the right place. Everybody clapped with her.
11. Grandma Hana said, "A play is even better the second time."
12. Junior did the play twice, and it was not small either time.
