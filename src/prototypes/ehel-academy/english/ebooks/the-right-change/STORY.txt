THE RIGHT CHANGE
Grade 3 - Level 3
Sami spends a Friday behind Omar's stall, where plus and minus carry the whole market.

1. The Right Change. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. "Sami," asked Omar on Friday, "will you help me at the stall today?"
3. "Watch the prices," he said. "Adding and taking away, all morning long."
4. Grandma Hana bought three mangoes. I added the prices: plus, plus, plus.
5. She paid with one big coin, so I counted her change back: minus the mangoes.
6. Then several people came at once, with baskets, bottles and bags.
7. "Various things, one total," said Omar. "Add them item by item."
8. Idris had one small coin. "Is it enough for a mango?" It was not.
9. "It is plenty for two bananas," said Omar, and Idris smiled again.
10. At midday we counted the coin box together, coin by coin.
11. Despite the busy morning, the total was exactly right.
12. "Plus and minus are little words," said Omar, "but they carry the whole market."
