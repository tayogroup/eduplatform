GUESS WHO I MEAN
Grade 3 - Level 3
On family game nights, one good describing word gives the answer away.

1. Guess Who I Mean. Written by Ehel Academy. Illustrated by Ehel Academy Learning Studio.
2. In the evenings my family plays a guessing game.
3. "I am thinking of somebody kind, calm and very patient," I said.
4. "Grandma Hana!" shouted Mina. But it was Mum.
5. Mina went next. "Somebody busy, who is always fixing things."
6. "That is Dad," I said, and Dad laughed in his chair.
7. Dad chose somebody honest, who always tells the truth politely.
8. We all pointed at Adam at exactly the same time.
9. Grandma Hana picked somebody noisy, friendly and a little bit muddy.
10. That one was easy. It was Mina, and she took a bow.
11. Then Mum said, "Somebody clever, who describes people beautifully."
12. Everybody looked at me. It is the loveliest game I know.
