Equal parts first

A fraction only works when the parts are equal. Is this cut into equal parts?
For the grown-up: what to have to hand

Paper to fold and cut, a piece of fruit or bread to share at snack time, and about 20 beans. Folding is what teaches equal parts; a screen can only show it.
